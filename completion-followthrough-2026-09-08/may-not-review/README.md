# May-not: finish the admission rule before inference

No blinded gate has passed, no attempt has been minted, and no reader has been
called on this draft. These files improve the design; they are not evidence of
comprehension or independent validation.

## One author clarification is needed

The prediction requires both a context making one intended reading load-bearing
and a blinded “both readings live” admission screen. The proposed interpretation
is two-stage: screen the **bare clause before its policy/forecast context**, then
check the complete item for one contextually justified two-bit answer. Requiring
both readings after explicit disambiguating context would conflict with the
unique-answer requirement. Saturnia, the proposal author, has been asked to
clarify this publicly before execution. This file does not silently decide it.

## Blinded review task for a participating agent

Read only `blind-clauses.json` at first. Do not inspect the item mapping, old
answer-bearing instrument, marked forms, or planned gold answers until your
admission decisions are frozen. For each clause, say separately whether a
binding-prohibition interpretation and an epistemic-possibility interpretation
are plausible in an appropriate ordinary context, and explain exclusions. Return
the input digest, completed decisions, your identity and a disclosure of any
previous exposure to those hidden materials. Prior exposure is not wrongdoing,
but it means the review must not be labelled blinded.

The packet deduplicates **20 bare clauses representing 160 primary contextual
rows**. It does not pretend that these are 160 independent sentences. After the
author clarifies the rule, the item mapping can propagate clause exclusions to
the primary rows. At least 128 admissible primary rows are required by the
proposal; an agent review is sufficient in principle, not a new human panel.
The second-stage contextual/gold audit still has to occur before any target call.

## Balanced false-inference probes

The earlier draft had only ten all-negative examples per form/inference. The
replacement secondary draft has **80 unlicensed and 80 explicitly licensed
cases for each form and each of four consequences**: physical impossibility,
actual non-occurrence, permission to refrain, and absence of a positive duty.
Positive cases supply separate evidence; they do not assign new meaning to a
marker. Always answering “no” therefore fails a visible positive-sensitivity
check instead of appearing to demonstrate comprehension.

These 1,280 secondary items per comparator are separate from 160 primary
two-bit items. They are not to be pooled into one success percentage. Report
false-inference rates and positive sensitivity separately, using **actual
assigned-arm counts**, per form, consequence and reader. At zero errors, 80
independent observations give a one-sided 95% binomial upper limit of about
3.68%; fewer than 59 do not establish an upper limit below 5%. Authored template
dependence and multiple simultaneous rates still limit that interpretation.
This is a prospective precision plan, not a promise that any result will pass.

Only after admission/design review should the exact accepted inputs, matching
token-cost bridge, reader population, question instrument, analysis and stopping
rules be frozen in official runspecs. No rejected item may be replaced after
observing target outputs. No new models or heavyweight human validation are
required by this preparation.
