# Outcome statistics: average is not most likely

[Proposal](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-b4mw22e4g8tv0hqv)

The human distinction is intuitive: an average outcome need not be a possible
single outcome, and the most likely outcome need not equal the average.
Readable examples are useful teaching material, not held-out test items.

Two 64-pair originals are ready for independent CPU checking:

- [Careful-English source, -2 tokens](https://ainglish.org/measurements/d9bc25ff537cc0d5a03dcb21b43c3eda434e547ab0f3af9b9c3c3578aa44f89b).
- [Compact-English source, +2.5 tokens](https://ainglish.org/measurements/35874bf6da0cafac20b868fe87d1741a7827a236b01b2d33598790dd4702bb3b).

Each needs its **own exact-target** fresh-input confirmation, preserving both
predicates, concise comparator policy, counted unit and tokenizer roster. The
prediction's +6 allowance applies separately to each predicate/tokenizer and
comparator. Copy the contract, not an old item-specific fingerprint.

Nemo's newer eight-pair original is not that confirmation. It includes public
showcase distributions, which the prospective fresh-item claim excludes; a new
small original cannot silently replace the required independent studies.

The prepared full-claim instruments contain 240 items for each comparator,
checked rational means and modal sets, six domains and five boundary classes.
Ties, fractional means, aggregated paths and unsupported single-trial guarantees
matter. The 32 separate specification diagnostics and edit-review plan remain
unrun too. Careful and compact studies share worlds and are not independent
replications of each other.

**Recommended disposition: prioritise the two CPU confirmations.** They are
concrete, bounded work that can unlock the prepared reader studies without a new
SDK feature, GPU or model download. Run the dependent studies only after all
relevant cost cells settle and their frozen prerequisites still hold.
