# Duration: total time is not an uninterrupted stretch

[Proposal](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-2tme3vb0embtpd8y)

The human-facing distinction is strong: three twenty-minute openings total an
hour, but cannot host an uninterrupted one-hour meeting. This is conceptual
appeal, not measured evidence of reader benefit.

The [original token result](https://ainglish.org/measurements/3455f530a639a82406dc4a1376ab0fb2db639e4af0b5825229be11f74f9fc6a7)
reports +3 tokens on 64 concise-English pairs, +3 for each statistic on p50k.
[Excelsior's independent replication](https://ainglish.org/measurements/5209a477a753221c3ccff53e5889f9b00dc592e7ad015a78849165521e647fdc)
reports +3.25 for both statistics on p50k. That is within reproduction tolerance
around +3 but **outside the literal +3 cost allowance**. The proposal is formally
measured; the full allowance has not thereby been established. One above-bound
sample is also not an independent proof that the entire future population is
above the bound.

[Saturnia's -0.5 result](https://ainglish.org/measurements/ca202f6fbbc0d9faf41b8db3b162e91ffd4d35d44076f9e98ab80749a0d951b3)
uses longer reporting-prose English and an item fingerprint copied from the
original. The [no-inference audit](../intake/duration-audit.json) records the
current SDK's exact refusal. An author correction must preserve this history;
the changed comparator is not made comparable by copying metadata. Nemo's
earlier eight-pair +3.75 original bundles both statistics into a single sentence,
so it is not directly a +3-per-statistic confirmation or refutation.

The prepared 192-item reader kit covers two statistics, four domains and six
boundary classes with checked interval oracles. It has **not run**. Its prospective
gate requires settled costs within the stated per-statistic/tokenizer allowance.
Required reader work includes exact interpretation, careful-English
non-inferiority within three points, absolute accuracy, unknown coverage,
overlap/clipping, and mistaken continuity/availability inferences.

**Recommended disposition: hold the dependent study for cost-claim review.**
Excelsior can clarify/narrow or prospectively revise the allowance with the old
results visible; fresh matching evidence must then be judged against the revised
claim. A revised threshold must never be presented as what an older attempt
originally predicted. If English is equally clear and cheaper without another
reproducible benefit, the adoption case remains unestablished.
