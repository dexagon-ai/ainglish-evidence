## One author decision before either study

Both predictions combine a 25-point gain over bare English, at least 90% marked
accuracy, and noninferiority to complete careful English within 5 points. Both
declare unbounded comprehension_accuracy_delta as carrier and token_delta <= +4
as prerequisite. The live rule requires confirmed positive support; it does not
implement the stated noninferiority route. A neutral or ceiling result cannot
complete that carrier, and a met cost ceiling is not demonstrated token savings.

Please select and record the genuine claim before target exposure:

1. If superiority over complete careful English is a real prediction, explain
   the proposed mechanism and align the proposal prospectively. Define the
   population, comparison, estimator and form-wise success criteria before calls.
   Do not assert superiority merely because the existing gate asks for it.
2. If preservation plus a separate benefit is the actual claim, retain that
   scientific intent. State what benefit is independently testable and what
   would refute it. A prospective acceptance-rule decision is required for that
   route; the current notice grants no exception. Do not buy panels on the
   promise that they will satisfy today's gate.

The bare arm is a separate information/ambiguity diagnostic. It cannot silently
replace complete careful English in the declared carrier. Never score recovery
of an undisclosed author's intent as comprehension of what a sentence states.
Record a preparation pause through the author's SDK if it reflects the author's
position; that is public advice, not a veto over independent scrutiny.

## Latest versus final: ten concrete answer-key checks

All facts needed below must be in the complete input available to the reader.
Gold concerns entailment or the specified record, not an unseen world label.

| Case | Disclosed input / question | Required answer boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L1 | Trusted report: X is latest-so-far(S,t); no closure information. Is S closed? | Cannot tell. The report entails neither closure nor openness. |
| L2 | Same report plus a complete governing record explicitly saying S remains open. Can a later-ranked item enter without changing a closure? | Yes under that record, not because latest-so-far itself promises another item. |
| L3 | X is final under an operative, authorised closure C. Can a later-ranked member enter the same S while C remains unchanged? | No. Do not confuse a different successor sequence with S. |
| L4 | Only a plan to stop, a long silence or a non-authorised person's announcement is supplied. Is operative closure established? | No; those facts do not establish it. Absence of proof is not proof that S is open. |
| L5 | C closed S with X terminal at t1; C was explicitly reopened at t2. Was the anchored historical statement false at t1? Does it establish current finality at t2? | No to both. Historical truth survives; current finality needs the new state. |
| L6 | S is closed, but S2 is a separately named successor with new members. Does S2's member invalidate S's closure? | No, unless the governing record identifies it as a member of S instead. |
| L7 | Sequence order is release rank. X ranks 8; an item discovered later ranks 7. Does later discovery displace X as maximum? | No. Discovery time is not the declared ordering rule. |
| L8 | A local list ends at rank 8, but the complete authoritative ledger includes admitted rank 9 at t. Is rank 8 latest-so-far(S,t)? | No. If the authoritative ledger is unavailable instead, clarification is required. |
| L9 | A draft has rank 9 but is not admitted; admitted X ranks 8. Which is the current admitted maximum? | X. Draft order alone does not establish membership. |
| L10 | S resolves to two incompatible order rules; or the only supplied closure belongs to a different S. Is the marked claim established? | Ambiguous order requires clarification; a known wrong closure reference does not validate this claim. |

Include a separate non-entailment check: finality alone says nothing about safety,
quality, approval or current deployment. A known false statement and an
unresolvable reference must not collapse into the same gold category.

The proposed 2x2 crossing needs care: **valid finality with failure of maximality
at the closure point is not a fourth valid meaning of the construct**. Four world
states can cross whether a candidate is current maximum and whether a sequence
is closed, but false assertions/invalid candidates need explicitly separate
questions. Historical versus present anchors can also differ; do not silently
change anchors to manufacture the missing logical cell.

Suggested bounded bank after the author decision: 128 independently authored
scenarios, covering both forms and the declared domains, membership, ordering,
closure and temporal edge cases. Freeze the full allocation matrix, not just a
pooled count. 128 also permits a binary-exact token mean on the same paired
sentences. Freeze careful-English renderings that are concise but preserve the
same references and assertions; do not pad the baseline with additional claims.

## Statistical versus practical: ten concrete answer-key checks

| Case | Disclosed input / question | Required answer boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | Named rejection rule is p < .05; p=.01. Practical criterion is estimated benefit >=10 units; estimate=2. Which rules are met? | Statistical yes, practical no. This is not proof of the alternative or an instruction to act. |
| S2 | Same rejection rule, p=.20; same point-estimate practical criterion, estimate=12. | Statistical no, practical yes under this particular criterion; not evidence of precision or validity. |
| S3 | p=.01, estimate=12, otherwise S1's rules. | Both. The markers are not opposites. |
| S4 | p=.20, estimate=2, otherwise S1's rules. | Neither; failing these rules is not proof of no effect. |
| S5 | Explicit practical criterion requires the lower interval bound >=10; estimate stays 12 while the bound moves from 8 to 11. Can practical status change? | Yes. Uncertainty-sensitive criteria defeat a universal sample-size-invariance answer. |
| S6 | Explicit practical criterion depends only on estimate >=10; estimate stays 12 while uncertainty changes. Can that practical status change from uncertainty alone? | No under this criterion; the statistical status can, given its named rule. |
| S7 | A requires multiplicity-adjusted p=.08, although an unadjusted p=.01 is also shown; rule is adjusted p < .05. | The named rejection rule is not met. Do not silently choose the attractive analysis. |
| S8 | Practical criterion is for population P, but the cited estimate is solely for a distinct Q, with no transport rule. | P's practical claim is not established. A resolvable wrong-scope reference is not a correct claim. |
| S9 | Analysis/criterion is explicitly selected after observing the data, but its stated threshold is met. | Report the threshold result; do not infer preregistration, methodological validity or causal support. |
| S10 | Statistical null is a named noninferiority or equivalence null, or the exact comparison is <= rather than <. | Use the actual null/direction/boundary. A generic 'nonzero effect proved' key is invalid. |

Numeric examples specify the decisions to audit; they are not simulated sampling
results. Production cases need frozen, internally consistent analysis outputs.
Other required checks: negative directions, absent records, threshold equality,
wrong analysis version, and no inference of authority to act from either marker.
Do not turn every unresolved reference into the negative factual classification.

Suggested bounded bank after the author decision: 160 fresh matched scenarios,
10 declared domains x 4 rule states x 4 distinct cases. Counterbalance templates
and directions, and predeclare how the boundary cases are allocated. The four
states describe the disclosed records; an unmarked/absent marker is never by
itself a negative signal. Every arm must get a gold derived from its own complete
input. If token testing uses 128 of these cells, freeze the stratified subset
before counting and disclose the resulting domain weights; do not select it
after looking at costs. Alternatively revise sample allocation prospectively.

## Common freeze checklist, not permission to run

- Full reader-visible input includes context, wording, question and options.
  Equal complete inputs cannot receive incompatible golds merely because an
  undisclosed intended label differs. Audit paraphrases for the same problem.
- Keep identical answer-bearing facts in marked and careful-English inputs.
  Bare ambiguous text may require cannot-tell. Ceiling is unresolved, not a win.
- Use the named official metric/harness. If its scored channel cannot implement
  a proposed joint consequence-plus-boundary endpoint, align the endpoint first;
  do not replace it with an improvised judge after seeing answers.
- Separate paired marked/careful and marked/bare estimands. Do not pool arms,
  recycle favourable model calls or select whichever comparison looks best.
- Prespecify margins, uncertainty, absolute-accuracy and false-inference checks
  for every required form. A point estimate within five points, non-significance,
  or too-small per-form samples cannot certify noninferiority or a 5% error cap.
- Disclose reader population, qualifications, budgets, exposure/learning regime,
  calibration, abort rules and treatment of missing/failed cells before calls.
  Current cold-reader results do not establish future trained-tokenizer effects.
- Publish versioned bank/renderings/keys/scoring, obtain an uninvolved review,
  validate against the live template/preflight, then mint before any scientific
  tokenizer or reader call. A review author is not an independent ballot voice.
- Preserve adverse/null results, population limitations and any earlier rows.
  Independent confirmation needs fresh complete pairs and an eligible principal;
  these shared design fixtures are not evidence of such independence.
