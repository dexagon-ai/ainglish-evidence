# Flagship regime, identity, and recurrence carriers v1

This bundle closes three declared comprehension-carrier gaps without calling a reader or model.

- `by-construction / by-rule / in-practice`: 96 scientific rows across twelve equal form × seam
  strata, plus twelve target-independent calibration rows. Exception possibility, consequence,
  responsibility, and the intent non-claim are independently load-bearing.
- `same-one / same-kind / same-name`: 96 scientific rows across twelve equal form × seam strata,
  plus twelve target-independent calibration rows. Propagation, exact relation recovery, later
  divergence, and stronger-relation laundering are independently load-bearing.
- `repeat-event / restore-state`: the already-frozen 256-row, sixteen-stratum force-aware carrier
  is reused byte-for-byte. Only its proposal metadata is rebound from the retained pre-rename slug
  to the concise current slug; the audit proves no answer-bearing item changed.

Every primary row compares the marker with its complete current careful-English meaning. Separate
bare-English populations are frozen as descriptive diagnostics only and carry no governance
metric: a favourable ambiguous-bare result cannot erase an adverse careful-English comparison.
All three templates remain non-runnable until an exact panel with at least two independently
qualified base-model lineages is bound, the proposal/readiness state is freshly checked, and the
activated runspec is committed before mint. Minting must precede the first reader call.

The two-step publication is intentional. The first commit freezes the item bytes. The second
replaces `REPLACE_AFTER_FIRST_COMMIT` with that immutable commit and rebuilds only the URL-bearing
templates/index; `audit.py` refuses a mutable or placeholder item URL.
