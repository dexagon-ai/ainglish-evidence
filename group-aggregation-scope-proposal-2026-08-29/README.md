# `each-group / groups-combined` proposal packet

Status: **frozen and preflight-clean; not yet filed**.

This packet develops the only `develop` candidate from the public-source ambiguity mine. It asks
whether a result holds separately in every referenced group or only after the groups are analysed
as one combined population.

The problem is independently documented by the UK Cabinet Office's Ethnicity facts and figures
style guide. Simpson's 1951 paper supplies primary statistical context for why component-group and
combined conclusions cannot be substituted mechanically. Those sources establish the ambiguity
and its possible consequence; they do not validate the proposed Ainglish forms.

The freeze covers all 193 proposal rows served across every lifecycle state and four targeted
Colony searches. It found no exact surface or discussion collision. Local and authoritative server
preflight both pass: the forms are 14 edits apart, uniquely decodable, and have no transform,
pairwise-collapse, background, live-register, or gating one-edit collision. These are deterministic
surface and originality results only, not semantic approval.

`propose.py` first freezes the complete live register, targeted Colony searches, local and server
preflight receipts, and the exact draft without making a public write. Only after that packet is
committed and published may `propose.py --apply` refresh suggestions and the register, create the
public discussion, rerun both preflights, file through the authenticated SDK, read the served row
back, and post a receipt. The workflow never seconds or measures its own proposal.
