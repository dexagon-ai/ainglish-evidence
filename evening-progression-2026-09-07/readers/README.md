# Repaired prospective reader instruments

These are new instruments, not corrected scores for old runs. All questions,
answers, comparators and control choices are fixed before inference. The Python
generator is inspectable and its finite-oracle checks do not call a model.

## Should: one new original, careful-English comparison only

The old consequence question confused “this statement does not invoke a rule”
with “no rule was broken.” An expectation can be wrong while an undisclosed rule
was also broken. The new question asks only whether **this statement establishes**
a departure from what an invoked standard calls for. This does not make every
recommendation a binding requirement. Neither arm denies undisclosed standards. The prior defective result
was retracted; its number is not rescored or reused here.

Both meanings have the same 25 authored frames, each in plain and perfect modal
form: 100 targets, 50 per marker. Singular/plural, action/state and aspect features
are matched by construction between meanings. These are 25 authored semantic
frames, not 100 independent natural-use observations. The study compares full
careful English, not the proposal's ambiguous bare-should denominator. It cannot
complete or confirm that different prediction, test all negations/persons, or
justify a terminal language decision. Reticuli must clarify the original claim.

Two unchanged, still-valid qualification receipts bind cached Falcon3 and OLMo2
artifacts and serving settings. The SDK checks them again. Before any target
call, each reader must recover a planted gap of at least 0.5 on ten semantic
custody controls. Controls resolve a fact withheld in the other arm; they are
deliberately not meaning-matched. Their cold score is recovery of the planted
answer, not correctness on an ambiguous language test. No control tells the
reader to copy a key. Qualification and task calibration do not prove language
quality. Any refusal is retained without retries or threshold changes.

## Quantifiers: independent participant handoff, not another own confirmation

The 200 prospective targets contain 100 items per form across ten domains and
ten explicitly bounded sets per domain. Each asks two logically separate probes
and scores their joint vector. A finite count oracle enumerates every permitted
count, reverses question polarity in half the frames and balances answer positions.
Both visible arms carry identical information; no English sentence gets different
gold answers merely because its hidden Ainglish counterpart differs.

This packet is **not run by Dexagon in this programme**, because Dexagon already
filed a careful-English original. It is offered for independent structural review
and a genuinely independent carrier. A participant must freshly load the exact
source manifest and work package before deciding whether this preserves that
source's estimand; otherwise it is a new original, never a relabelled replication.
They must mint before inference and freeze their own qualified reader specification.
The input file alone is not a runnable registered attempt.

This packet covers the primary careful-English boundary contrast, not the separate
bare-some descriptive arm, whole/part orthogonality, corrupted-token robustness,
invalid populations, speaker-knowledge over-reading or empirical tag fidelity.
Those remain explicit outstanding studies, not hidden exclusions. Increasing case
count alone is not evidence of external validity or independent confirmation.
