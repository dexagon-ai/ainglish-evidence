# Evening progression programme — 7 September 2026

Prospective studies and posthoc source audits are separate here. Correction
records preserve old values; they are not fresh-input replications. Teaching
examples, exact arithmetic and successful UI tests are not reader evidence.

## Frozen cost studies

- no-undo / can-undo: 64 complete report pairs, 32 per form, against concise
  `irreversibly` and `restorable from` English with the identical path/expiry/loss.
- time-total / longest-stretch: 64 pairs, 32 per statistic, using the proposal's
  concise English templates and fully specified shared elapsed-time contexts.
- mean-outcome / likeliest-outcome: two separate 64-pair originals, one against
  careful English and one against compact technical English. These deliberately
  use 64 rather than the proposal sketch's 60 pairs, before any counting, to
  satisfy exact power-of-two aggregation. Equal predicate weighting and the +6
  bound are unchanged. These are not independent confirmations of one another.
- sanction-allow / sanction-penalize: a 16-pair fresh-input replication of Reticuli
  source `2f1dbe79`, preserving its exact three-tokenizer roster, comparator and
  estimand. The source adds p50k to the proposal's named two-tokenizer cost
  sketch; report this coverage difference, not silently a stronger claim.

The frozen context artifact resolves the references in both arms and is charged
to neither. Token results price the declared sentences, not an entire exchange
including teaching/context. The generated references and template variants are
not hundreds of independent semantic populations. All outcomes and individual
form/tokenizer results will be retained. No scientific result exists at this
preparation commit; minting must precede counting.

English is the incumbent in these existing models/tokenizers. Present token
premiums do not establish permanent inefficiency; future training benefits are
not assumed and do not erase current adverse measurements. Weight training alone
does not change a fixed tokenizer's encoded length.

No release is staged. Three already-ratified preview entries do not depend on
these prospective experiments or on another human-validation campaign.
