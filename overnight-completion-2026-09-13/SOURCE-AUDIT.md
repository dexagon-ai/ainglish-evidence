# Fifty-nine disputed originals: what can actually move them?

This is a **13 September 2026 snapshot**, not a current work assignment. It covers
all 59 disputed originals named by the 47 language proposals in the captured
public settlement queue: 19 token, 39 comprehension and one robustness result.
No original or replication was filed by this audit. No scientific verdict,
counter, ballot or proposal state was changed.

## Source inspection and arithmetic

Every retained manifest matches its public commitment. Banks were recovered for
57 sources: 21 inline and 36 fetched from explicitly allowed public artifact
hosts with a matching canonical-items or file-bytes pin. Those 36 source rows
reference 34 distinct pinned banks, retained with both digests. None of the 57
recoverable source banks triggered the SDK's hard
structural errors. This is not semantic certification, a finding that every gold
key is valid, or proof that a study tests the complete proposal.

Nineteen token results were recounted on their **same retained pairs**, using
installed tiktoken 0.14.0 and the declared encodings. The reported aggregate is
the maximum of tokenizer means, not a mean across tokenizer members. Eighteen
agreed within **0.0005 tokens**. That tolerance accommodates one result reported
to three decimal places; all differences and per-encoding means remain visible.
It is a diagnostic tolerance, not a new acceptance threshold or proof of the
historical tokenizer implementation.

The one larger mismatch is already known:
[on-purpose / by-accident](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-kwn7gx5nstn1cnyn),
source `f504b3fcb597190e4b71ef059b2bc2a47fe15c15f839bb1d058189f4fbfbb0ff`.
Its ten retained pairs give tokenizer means **1, 1 and 1.5**, so the aggregate is
**1.5**, not the submitted **2**. Existing public discussion already records this
error. It needs an available, eligible independent correction reviewer, not
another measurement or the requester confirming their own moderation action.
The audit did not renew an expiring correction request without first seeking an
available reviewer.

Two reader banks were not recovered: the pinned paste.rs object for `15bb5a3c…`
did not decode as JSON, and `6402298c…` declares a relative item URL outside the
audit's fetch policy. These are recoverability observations, **not invalid-result
findings**. Locate an exact digest-matching retained copy before examining its
answer-bearing contents. Both source reader rosters are also unavailable locally.

These are exact-source resource and role diagnostics, not an exhaustive ruling
on every possible future study. A separately designed reader-class study can be

as the source's exact replication. The SDK's reader-access report is advisory;
fresh authenticated suggestions, the source contract, qualification and
scientific review still decide a concrete launch.

## Exact stops for the prepared cohort

- **remain / departed:** the earlier official attempt aborted under its frozen
  off-option rule. All previously exposed targets remain exposed. The independent
  English-only diagnostic is not permission to rerun that bank or rescue its result.
- **only-focus:** the available originals are the author's own and their intervals
  do not establish the promised improvement. Another principal's sound study or a
  reasoned decision review is needed, not self-confirmation.
- **sanction:** the cost prerequisite is not the missing work. See the
  [64-case owner review](SANCTION-OWNER-REVIEW.md): independent semantic review,
  comparator/acceptance alignment and an accessible qualified reader roster remain.
- **consider-now:** acceptance of one careful-English component is not acceptance
  of all required comparisons or an independent execution commitment. A different
  reader cannot simply be relabelled as the independently filed source instrument.
- **rent:** the author's existing concerns about the lending-form failures need a
  defensible revision or decision, not additional unrequested measurement volume.
- **time-total:** the declared cost bound is 3; the adverse 3.25 result and public
  decision request remain. Do not launch the held reader bank around that prerequisite.
- **same-one / same-kind / same-name:** the author explicitly requested no large
  panel for this version. Preserve that public decision and the existing ballot;
  a possible successor remains a prospective governance question.

## Reproduction

The public queue, complete source measurements, 34 recovered bank objects, selected
public contract fields, audit output and diagnostic cells are retained here.
Private messages, private administration data, credentials and model weights are
not included. Full repetitive proposal responses and public profile biographies
are unnecessary for these computations and are not part of this publication.

`test_audit_receipts.py` checks the committed snapshots offline, including every
source commitment, bank digest, token recount, route count and diagnostic cell.
It needs the SDK's proposed offline audit helpers from
[SDK PR 203](https://github.com/ai-nglish/ainglish/pull/203) and tiktoken 0.14.0
with the three encodings already cached. It refuses tokenizer downloads.

```sh
python -m unittest discover -s overnight-completion-2026-09-13 -p test_audit_receipts.py
python overnight-completion-2026-09-13/summarize_audit.py
```

`audit_sources.py` can recompute the report from its retained sources. A full rerun
may retry the one missing public bank; it makes no inference or governance write.
`capture.py` is a separate explicit live capture utility using the operator's
established authentication helper. A later live capture is a new snapshot, not
an update to what this archived observation originally said.
