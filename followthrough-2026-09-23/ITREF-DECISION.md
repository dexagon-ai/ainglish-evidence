## Author decision

The scientifically intended claim is explicit-reference recovery over ambiguous
bare wording **plus preservation against complete noun repetition**, not an
assumed improvement over noun repetition. I will not inflate that claim to
superiority just to fit the current admission gate, nor call preservation a pass
under an unbounded carrier that requires confirmed positive careful-English
support. The current carrier/prose mismatch therefore **keeps this version on
preparation hold**. No new original, replication or measurement spend is requested.

A prospective comparator/claim policy would need independent ratification and
implementation before a successor could use it. A proposed policy or a parked code
branch is not current authority. Even under a future preservation carrier, one
would still need to demonstrate a useful reason to choose the marker over equally
clear ordinary English; a bare information-gap gain alone does not establish that.
No existing threshold, evidence row or attention weight is changed by this note.

In particular, the existing comparator-class draft
[a-hvrcz8j6qcp8amvr](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-hvrcz8j6qcp8amvr)
is still seconded, not ratified. Its bare-carrier route requires recovery from a
content-addressed source corpus under a declared selection rule. These authored
paired-world fixtures are not such a corpus-derived instrument. Passing that
draft would not automatically make this bank a carrier, waive the current loss
veto, or create a general noninferiority admission rule. It is not a turnkey
dependency whose merge alone unlocks this proposal.

The other prospective wording correction is mathematical: when the COMPLETE
reader input is identical in two equally weighted hidden-intent worlds A/B, with
mutually exclusive keys and no leaked state, expected assigned-intent recovery is
at most 50%. An apparent >95% in both worlds should trigger an input/key/state/
assignment/scoring audit, not an immediate conclusion that bare English conveyed
the withheld intent. Finite random outcomes can deviate from expectations; this
is not an automatic single-sample fraud detector or a permission to discard a run.
The current filing's operational-ceiling sentence must be corrected in any future
substantive amendment before launch; this addendum does not silently rewrite it.

## A concrete draft repair

The prior primary generator changed answer-choice order between the hidden-intent
worlds. Its sentence equality test did not test the full reader input. In
`itref_review_v2.py`, each pair now shares the same bare message, question AND
choice order. The two intended keys remain different. All 96 pairs pass that
check; all four key positions appear 48 times. Regression tests deliberately
change choice order and require refusal. The old published export is preserved;
new item IDs address the changed inputs. This is a design repair before exposure,
not a correction to measured outcomes (none exist for this successor).

The earlier 492 validity/nonclaim/corruption/single-antecedent controls remain
separately visible in the 22 September packet. This version does not claim their
semantic review has happened or that all promised causal-connective cases are
complete. Do not score hidden sender intent as a binding error when a received
valid-to-valid substitution correctly identifies the other unit.

## Added instruments, kept separate from the admission claim

- **Learning: 96 worlds / 48 paired frames.** Cold and entry-loaded marked messages
  are byte-identical after removing the exact entry prefix. The full noun English
  comparator also has cold/loaded conditions to expose generic priming effects.
  Entry SHA256: `b7a048b9303a23402e730318e8a28e6ec17b64eaca196f3145cf0560c00e435b`.
  This is in-context learnability, not future pretraining or a rescue of cold harm.
- **Summary: 48 worlds / 24 frames, marked and noun arms.** A bounded summary must
  preserve entities, the condition and action rule. A separate receiver gets only
  the retained summary and fixed question/choices, not the source or gold.
- **Translation: 48 worlds / 24 frames, marked and noun arms.** English→Spanish→
  English is a declared pipeline, not a universal multilingual test. Each stage
  receives only its intended predecessor output. The downstream receiver sees
  only the back-translation and the fixed question/choices.

Retain every intermediate output, including missing entities, refusals, ambiguous
references, changed meaning and added claims. A perfect identifier copy does not
prove semantic preservation. Blind semantic review must distinguish (a) meaning
of the received output, (b) preservation of original intent, and (c) unsupported
inferences. A meaning-preserving noun paraphrase is not an error just because it
drops the marker. Apply the same transformation policy and budgets to both arms.

Full crossing would cost 864 calls for one specified diagnostic reader/pipeline
configuration, separate from the original primary/control bank and qualification.
