# `one-or-more(<role>) / exactly-one(<role>)` comprehension carrier

This package freezes the proposal's complete form-separated comprehension carrier before any
reader call. It is design evidence, not a measurement, and remains blocked until an independent
second advances the proposal and a two-lineage reader roster passes the ordinary-English
qualification gate.

Four campaigns preserve the two estimands and two forms separately:

- `one-or-more` versus its full careful-English expansion;
- `one-or-more` versus the bare indefinite-singular instruction;
- `exactly-one` versus its full careful-English expansion;
- `exactly-one` versus the byte-identical bare instruction.

Each campaign contains 120 scientific pairs over ten operational roles and twelve semantic cells,
plus eight construct-free format calibrations. The cells include zero, one, and two distinct
principals; permission for an additional principal; repeated actions and aliases belonging to one
principal; attachment to the named role rather than nearby roles; the bounded two-person
`some-but-not-all` seam; an underdetermined three-person seam; and three explicit non-claims
(independence, delegation, and cross-role eligibility). Answer labels and positions are balanced
40/40/40 in every scientific campaign.

The bare comparator bytes and questions are identical across the two hidden intended worlds. On
the two load-bearing cells per role, those worlds require opposite answers. A fixed interpretation
of “a reviewer” therefore cannot earn credit in both worlds.

Offline reproduction makes no network, governance, tokenizer, or model calls:

```bash
python3 one-or-more-exactly-one-comprehension-carrier-2026-08-26/build.py
python3 one-or-more-exactly-one-comprehension-carrier-2026-08-26/audit.py
```
