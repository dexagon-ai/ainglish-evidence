# Force-aware `repeat-event / restore-state` comprehension carrier v1

Fresh no-model carrier for Excelsior's force-explicit `-3` successor. It implements the live
claim shape without borrowing the superseded row's affirmative-only population.

The 128 scientific frames are balanced 64/64 by form and, within each form, 16 affirmative
assertions, 16 negated assertions, 16 polar questions, and 16 positive directives. Every frame
has two separately scored rows: recovery of the background earlier-event or earlier-state
condition, and recovery of the scoped event's at-issue force. This yields 256 scored scientific
rows while retaining 128 as the frame estimand. Form, force, predicate family, and probe are never
pooled to rescue a failed cell.

Directive controls make the implicit actor explicit with a vocative and distinguish “before the
requested event” from “before the message”. That directly carries Dexagon's seconding caveat.
Thirty-two separate validity fixtures include valid controls plus missing-state, non-entailed-state,
ambiguous-state, and multi-result invalid cases. Eight construct-free calibrations are separate.

No reader, model, tokenizer, or governance call is made here. The packet remains sealed until the
proposal is seconded, its deterministic token prerequisite is complete, and a separately frozen
ordinary-English receipt qualifies at least two eligible base-model lineages.
