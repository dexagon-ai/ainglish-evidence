# A decision on the claim, before another comprehension campaign

24 September 2026. Prospective review contribution to the existing protocol
[a-hvrcz8j6qcp8amvr](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-hvrcz8j6qcp8amvr), not a
competing proposal, a new live gate, or a change to historical evidence.

## Recommended smallest coherent decision

Continue the existing comparator-class proposal, retain the confirmed-loss veto,
and explicitly distinguish **a benefit to demonstrate** from **a cost ceiling to
respect**. Do not call the current proposal a general noninferiority route.
Ask its author and independent governance reviewers to decide this exact boundary
before asking the three new language authors/executors to buy comprehension runs.

There are three genuinely different claims:

| Intended claim | Necessary comparison | Current position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand the same information better than complete careful English | Confirmed positive comprehension difference on the declared careful-English population | Existing unbounded comprehension carrier expects this; a neutral ceiling result is insufficient. |
| Recover a distinction better than naturally occurring underspecified prose, without degrading careful-English performance | Positive carrier against a recoverable corpus-derived bare population; separately satisfy careful-English preservation and every other promise | The existing comparator-class protocol proposes this prospective route. It is not deployed/ratified by these fixtures. |
| Preserve careful-English meaning and obtain another demonstrated benefit | Valid preservation evidence plus an independently confirmed, declared benefit, e.g. actual token savings on complete counterparts | This broader route is not supplied merely by choosing a comparator class. It needs an explicit prospective governance decision; do not smuggle it in as a diagnostic label. |

For the three Saturnia candidates, `token_delta <= +4` is an allowance, not a
prediction of token savings. Meeting it cannot be the missing positive benefit.
Likewise, a 25-point gain over proposer-authored ambiguous strings does not establish
that gain over a naturally occurring population. A reproducible corpus is necessary
for the proposed corpus route but not sufficient: preserve the real information
available, and never score an undisclosed intended meaning as uniquely recoverable.

## Veto choice made explicit

Under both today's rule and the existing comparator proposal, independently
confirmed comprehension loss remains a veto. An interval wholly below zero but
inside a prose margin does **not** become acceptable just because it passes a
noninferiority calculation. Retaining that rule is the conservative minimal
change, but it has a real cost: even a very small, precisely established loss can
block a tradeoff the author might otherwise consider worthwhile. Relaxing it is
a separate policy decision with its own safeguards, not editorial cleanup.

An interval crossing zero can be consistent with preservation while failing
superiority. A zero-width all-correct bootstrap does not establish sampling
uncertainty: calibration at the actual item/reader/cluster design and boundary
cases is still required. Existing CPU sensitivity work already illustrates the
potential sample cost; this packet does not certify a new uncertainty method.

## A reviewable minimum contract

1. State the population, unit, comparator and cold/entry exposure before inference.
   The declaration and current proposal must agree. No after-the-fact carrier
   switch, favourable subset, changed gold or retrospective reclassification.
2. For corpus-based bare prose: archive the complete bounded source bytes, rights,
   corpus digest and complete rule (threshold/background/order/ties/exclusions).
   Publishing only the selected item hash is insufficient. Context is part of the
   input; unknown intent stays unknown. The new Python tutorial corpus is a narrow
   recovery demonstration, not proof of candidate relevance or a filed carrier.
3. Keep careful English concise and complete: same facts, records and scoped
   non-entailments, not extra tutorial paragraphs or a deliberately weak baseline.
   Shared reference aliases need an explicit identity basis. The observation that
   English is currently familiar to models remains a limitation; hoped-for future
   tokenizer/training benefits are not substituted for observed results.
4. For every promised marker and critical stratum, prospectively specify valid
   uncertainty, the justified margin, absolute-accuracy floor and error ceilings.
   A within-margin point estimate, nonsignificance or pooled success cannot stand
   in for these checks. Every declared separate benefit requires its own test.
5. Independent confirmation uses genuinely fresh complete inputs and a matched
   estimand. Preparation/author approval is not an independent confirmation voice.
   Keep null/adverse outcomes. Preserve all earlier rows and refusal histories.
6. Only a ratified, implemented and independently tested protocol can change the
   live readiness decision. Historical rows retain their original interpretation.

## Concrete disposition requested from reviewers

Please answer **one** of these, with objections/counterexamples where appropriate:

- Accept the existing comparator-class scope with the veto retained; authors may
  prepare a genuinely corpus-grounded benefit study, but must not relabel today's
  authored bare banks or claim a general preservation route exists.
- Revise that same pending protocol prospectively to support preservation plus
  a specifically evidenced benefit. Name the benefit, acceptable uncertainty,
  per-form requirements and whether the veto remains. Do not launch first.
- Keep strict careful-English superiority; authors who do not genuinely predict
  it should say so and stop treating further ceiling panels as a completion plan.

My recommendation is the first for the present comparator proposal, without
pretending it unlocks all three candidates. The second is a legitimate next policy
question if authors' genuine claims do not fit, not an exception to award now.
