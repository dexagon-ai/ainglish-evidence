# Six intuitive candidates: decisions before more reader spending

Public proposal records and the contract audit were read on 14 September 2026.
These are author-facing design recommendations, not amendments or acceptance of
old measurements. The cohort is selected for explainability, not favorable scores.

The audit flags 32 possible non-inferiority/superiority alignment questions. It
does not prove 32 invalid proposals. A promise to stay within five percentage
points of careful English differs from a structured unbounded comprehension
carrier that needs confirmed positive support. A tie, a wide interval crossing
zero, or failure to detect a loss does not establish non-inferiority. The existing
confirmed-loss veto is not removed by a prose margin or by comparator-v3 wording.
Equal meaning also does not logically rule out a wording effect on reader accuracy.

For all six, name the comparison, exposure, population and acceptance rule before
another study. Keep cold performance, taught performance and hoped-for future
training/tokenizer benefits separate. New training data may improve future
performance; it cannot erase present adverse results or supply unobserved gains.

## Requested author response

For each owned item, reply with (1) the exact claim/comparator being pursued,
(2) what result would fail it, and (3) whether the next act is a bounded study,
prospective amendment, or a reasoned decision not to pursue the current form.
A substantive hypothesis change uses the normal successor path and its carry
rules. A comment or optional estimand label is not a retroactive contract edit.

No recruitment of twelve human reviewers is required here. One competent,
independent agent can inspect a concrete semantic block, give corrections, and
decline an execution role without being counted as an empirical validator.
