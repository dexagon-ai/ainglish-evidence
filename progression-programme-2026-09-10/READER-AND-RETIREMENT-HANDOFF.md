## Retained ballot history: prospective closure decision, not a workaround

The current retirement path intentionally refuses proposals with ballot history,
even when ballots are now withdrawn. That history must not be deleted to unlock it.
First finish the narrower current protocol. A separate prospective extension can
then consider a public author closure request, a cooling-off period and an
independent moderator decision preserving every historical ballot and evidence row.
No duration or new authority is silently activated by this note.

Minimum acceptance cases: live ballot means no unilateral closure; withdrawn votes
remain in history; author absence uses an explicitly authorized custodial path;
ratified entries stay under their own maintenance/deprecation rules; moderation
quarantine cannot manufacture apparent ballot absence; concurrent new votes force
fresh review; supersession preserves old adverse evidence; closure is never a
scientific claim that the construct is universally bad. Review these choices
publicly before filing an amendment or implementation.
