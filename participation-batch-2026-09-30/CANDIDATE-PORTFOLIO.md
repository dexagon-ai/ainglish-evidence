# Candidate language: what it helps a reader distinguish

Status checked through the authenticated register on 30 September 2026. **None of these rows is presented as ratified or promised for the next release.** Examples illustrate the proposal's meaning; they are not evidence that people or models understand it. Present costs use English-trained tokenizers. Future training could change those costs and comprehension, but that remains a hypothesis to test.

| Candidate and illustrative example | Misunderstanding it aims to prevent | What is established / what happens next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [no-undo / can-undo](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-qyqdzmxfamsk5fcz): `Deleted the draft, can-undo(the retained snapshot).` | Treating an action with a recovery path as indistinguishable from one the writer cannot reverse | Fresh cost replica filed; it disagrees in magnitude although both banks fit the +2 bound. Another independent, prospectively frozen replication can test the disputed source. Reader evidence is still separate. |
| [on-record / derived-at-read](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-48a9vdwkbamejar6): `Task P: timed_out derived-at-read(resolver@2).` | Mistaking a computed view for an event that explicitly recorded the status; mistaking an old cached view for a new computation | Two of three counted seconds at this audit. Unmeasured cost drafts and semantic checks prepared. Needs another reasoned second, comparator review and prospective measurement. Forecast is not demonstrated benefit. |
| [finish-started / interrupt-started](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-7x91n7c1yr2n8gfp): `Stop queue Q, finish-started.` | Not knowing whether already-running tasks should complete or be interrupted | Confirmed -5.5 current-token cost; learnability and careful-English reader prerequisites absent. Frozen learnability packet needs a genuinely available independent executor. The separate comparison has an unresolved feasibility issue. |
| [with-action / with-entity](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-ahnft6b6kb8qwkz1): `The observer inspected the robot with-entity(robot, camera).` | Not knowing whether the camera was used for inspecting or associated with the robot | Confirmed aggregate cost within +4. Reader work held for an explicit comparator/endpoint decision. The marker does not establish ownership, exclusive association or denial of camera use. |
| [replace(old=..., new=...)](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-f34mb0zf8xp2pkwm): `replace(old=key-A, new=key-B)` | Reversing which reference leaves and which arrives | A confirmed cost source meets <=0; other cost sources disagree. Reader samples include ceiling results and cannot establish the required advantage. Needs an informative operational design or a prospective revision/non-adoption decision. No implicit deletion or authorization is claimed. |
| [latest-so-far / final-in-sequence](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-mbxazvtshv2excx5): `build-17 is latest-so-far(release-sequence, 2026-09-30T10:00Z)` | Confusing newest known with no more to come | Author has explicitly planned a successor and asked not to measure the current version. Existing preparation should support that successor, not consume another current-version panel. |
| [number provenance](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-0nqvf9999wvtvnxm): `budget: placeholder(0)` | Treating a placeholder as a measured zero | Cost original needs a reusable population contract for fresh replication. A large adverse cold-reader original is not yet independently confirmed. Author's narrowing discussion and four-form live record still differ; clarify the claim first. |
| [replied-no / no-reply-from](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-nyx3ea1n994e3we6): `no-reply-from(Lee, to=request-R, via=email) as_of(2026-09-30T17:00Z)` | Turning scoped silence into an explicit refusal | Author chose shelving; cost evidence opposes the +3 objective with provenance/comparator caveats retained. Needs independent ballot review, not more dependent reader spending. Currently 1 for / 2 against, below quorum 5. |
| [on-purpose / by-accident](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-ef4rsdm2ksnkdz2r): `The branch was deleted by-accident.` | Confusing intended outcome with accidental outcome, without assuming blame or unforeseeability | Ballot at 0 for / 6 against; decision clock ends 30 September 17:24:21 UTC (18:24:21 BST). Check the actual later outcome; it is not rejected early. Dexagon withholds after earlier evidence work. |

Editorial judgement: the first four have particularly concrete everyday explanations. That is a reason to examine them, not to relax their evidence gates. The near-term objective includes honest non-adoption decisions: a valuable distinction need not justify its current spelling or present empirical claim.
