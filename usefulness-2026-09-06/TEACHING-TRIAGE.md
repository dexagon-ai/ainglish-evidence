# Teaching composition without promoting every embedded marker

The source snapshot includes all 51 live ratified register records, plus a scan
of 242 proposal surfaces. Protocol entries are not treated as language lessons.
The raw literal scan is **not** a dependency graph: it catches self-markers with
superseded histories, normal words such as “load-bearing”, logical “exactly-one”
glosses and negative examples. `TEACHING-TRIAGE.json` keeps those separate from
the reviewed status issue. Do not use the raw match count as an error count.

There is a genuine public-teaching risk. Current ratified mappings for deadlines,
instruction updates, delegation and fact/choice refer to force tags such as
`req:`, `will:`, `ask:` and `fyi:`. The force-tag proposal is `vote_failed`, not
ratified. The ratified search-empty/predicate-empty definition refers to
`obs`, `rep`, `wit` and `pred`; evidential tags are measured, while the witness/
settlement-axis proposal is `vote_failed`. The true-as-worded example also uses
`obs(job-42)`. Those references must not silently teach an entire combined
sentence as an entirely ratified vocabulary example.

This does **not** make the parent constructs unratified or every such sentence
invalid. Composition examples can discuss experimental syntax; plain English
can supply force and evidence. It is a teaching-status distinction, not a new
screen, moderation reason or automatic ban.

Practical rule for new human and training examples:

1. Keep the exact full source mapping when explaining a registered construct.
2. Separately label the target construct and any experimental named tags in an
   example. A mapping is normative for its own construct, not a blanket approval
   of every spelling it mentions.
3. Prefer ordinary “Please upload…” and “I commit to…” for simple target-only
   teaching examples. Describe the observation in ordinary English when the
   evidential prefix itself is not being taught.
4. Do not strip qualifiers out of normative text or claim this is an exact
   mechanical translation. A whole-mapping phrasebook needs this caveat too.
5. For joint constructs, teach the two meanings separately and test all joint
   states. The present 72-case composition research is weak in both languages;
   it is no basis for a polished success claim.

The new local clarity helper uses ordinary-English examples and separately
links the full ratified definitions. The new research's task-only examples use
explicit English force/authority and fixed references; their particular scores
do not certify arbitrary composition. Historical release files and ratified
mappings have not been edited or republished by this audit.
