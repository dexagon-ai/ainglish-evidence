# Controlled usefulness follow-up

This is synthetic, non-normative research on the six pinned ratified families
from the earlier learning pilot. It is not a new language release, official
settlement evidence, human validation or natural adoption.

The new comparison trains two small adapters on paired, complete Ainglish and
English renderings of 168 distinct cases. It then evaluates 252 distinct
retention cases, a 72-case constrained drafting task, 72 cases across four
renderings and two guide conditions, 32 three-checkpoint instruction traces,
72 joint-meaning composition cases, and twelve answer-permutation diagnostics.
There are 1,572 target cells per new weight condition. The two old adapters are
also scored on retention/permutation only, without changing their earlier results.

The retention grammar is intentionally inherited: these are new domain words and
balanced labels, not unseen semantic structures. There are five distinct
participant and multiplicity variants and eight variants in each other family.
The new curriculum contains no exact repeated cases; neither does retention.
Workflow contains eight structural scenarios instantiated as 32 named episodes.
Labels and template slots do not manufacture independent observations.

Primary learning comparison: new A-trained versus matched E-trained cold-A
accuracy. Overall, every family and English retention must each meet the declared
minus-five-point guard to describe the follow-up as a promising pilot. Also show
frame-cluster intervals; meeting a point guard is not statistical proof of
non-inferiority. No family loss is hidden by pooling. An English decline is not
an Ainglish gain. All old outcomes remain final.

Constrained drafting asks a model to select a faithful explicit message over a
wrong-pole and an under-specified candidate. It does not measure free-form
authoring or whether a real recipient needs fewer clarification turns. The
wording factorial compares information-bearing renderings, not incomplete English
against complete Ainglish. Arbitrary labels without a guide are intentionally
unfamiliar; guide/native-token costs are part of the result, not assumed equal.
Hyphen-to-space is identical for the two update prefixes, which have no hyphens;
that zero-change condition stays visible.

Workflow and composition are offline readouts against declared semantic states,
not actual actions. A superseded obligation does not imply cancellation of an
in-flight process, and completed effects are not silently reversed.

Before any targets: all inputs and code pins are public, both new adapters are
trained and publicly sealed, source weights/tokenizer are local-only and fixed,
and twelve target-independent format controls run for each weight condition.
Require eleven correct with no malformed or truncated control responses. Keep
every target response; malformed/truncated responses score incorrect. Runtime
errors stop the campaign without retries. Report exact calls, native tokens,
absolute accuracies, pair discordance, every condition and the declared guards.

Resource limits: no new model downloads, one explicitly selected free GPU,
at least 10 GiB physical Windows disk reserve, no optimizer checkpoint series,
only final small adapters. Existing models and old adapters are retained.

The pre-compute audit already caught and prevented a training-label imbalance;
the rejected unspent draft is retained in `precompute-rejected/v1/`.
