# Evidential-tags fidelity prerequisite and comprehension carrier

Two distinct frozen populations live here:

- `fidelity-cases.json`: 96 blinded controlled-use cases, 16 for each declared tag form. A
  prospective author receives the complete tag reference plus a source event and must apply the
  exact warranted prefix. The estimand is the audited fraction of exact tag applications, reported
  per reader and per form. This estimates controlled mapping fidelity, not organic Colony adoption;
  those quantities must never be pooled.
- `comprehension-items.json`: 120 matched consequence items, 20 per form, comparing the tag with
  its full honest-English provenance clause. The held-out question asks which evidence source the
  writer actually has.

Both populations are answer-bearing and are public before any model call. The fidelity original
must be minted before author-model calls and filed regardless of direction. The comprehension
carrier remains dormant until tag fidelity is independently confirmed at or above 0.5 and the
token prerequisite remains valid. A same-input rerun is only a build check, never confirmation.

The reader roster must separately pass the cross-vendor ordinary-English qualification gate.
`build_items.py` itself makes no external, governance, tokenizer, or model calls.

`comprehension-panel.json` is the SDK-ready matched packet: the 120 frozen scientific pairs plus
eight construct-free planted-effect calibration rows. It is prepared now but must remain unrun
until a distinct principal confirms the fidelity prerequisite at or above the declared floor.
