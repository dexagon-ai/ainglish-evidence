## Recommended method-policy alternative, requiring explicit author consent

In the accepted `predicted_measurement`, replace only:

> an approved replayable simultaneous uncertainty method

with:

> an independently reviewed, prospectively approved replayable uncertainty method,
> with an all-required decision policy: every prespecified aggregate, form,
> accuracy, safety and positive-control requirement below must pass its declared
> valid marginal test at one-sided level at most 0.05. Interval methods, nominal
> coverage, analysis populations and sampling assumptions must be fixed before
> target exposure. Report intervals with their actual marginal coverage, not as
> simultaneous confidence coverage. No successful subset may be promoted or used
> to claim the bundle passed. A held or invalid bound cannot support a requirement;
> a valid opposing bound still counts when another requirement is unresolved.

The strict token carrier, -5 pp preservation margin, 90% accuracy and positive-control
floors, 5% unsafe/nonclaim ceilings, exact semantics/comparator and loss veto do not
change. The reported 95% percentile-bootstrap intervals cannot simply be declared
valid at level 0.05 because their quantiles have those names: the independent
sampling/operating-characteristic review must still assess coverage and refusal
conditions, especially near perfect accuracy and with clustered worlds.

This is a change to the accepted reporting promise and **is not already approved**.
If the author retains simultaneous coverage instead, plan and justify that analysis;
do not silently substitute Reticuli's pointwise rule. Formal per-form settlement,
claim-preservation acceptance, author safety checks and the standing confirmed-loss
veto remain different decisions.

The statistical reason to consider this limited change is that, when success
requires ALL prespecified component hypotheses to pass, false acceptance of that
conjunction can be controlled by valid component tests without demanding simultaneous
confidence coverage. This does not make the individual bounds jointly 95% reliable,
and the probability all tests pass can still be low. See section III.C.1 of
[FDA's multiple-endpoint guidance](https://www.fda.gov/media/162416/download).
Applying that logic here is a statistical inference, not an FDA assessment of Ainglish.

## Feasible panel envelope, not a selected sample size

SDK 0.2.61 and inspected Symfony source `766bc18b4f4a7e807fbfb2da669c3e09d187df34`
cap an attested journal at **5,000 target reader-item cells**. The attached witness
actually invokes the SDK guard. The live protocol response did not expose this
capacity, so this is a verified installed/reference-implementation limit, not a
claim to have inspected production configuration.

The earlier n=1,024 per reader, arm and form witness needs 8,192 target cells for
two forms and two readers and does not fit one such journal. It was a conservative
per-reader feasibility calculation, never a required or selected study. Do not
split a study opportunistically to bypass a complete-bundle evidence requirement.

One envelope to assess is 1,200 distinct semantic worlds per form, two forms and
two readers: **4,800 target cells per run**. With 32 calibration items shown in
both arms to both readers, that is 4,928 calls per run, or 9,856 for original plus
independent replication. These counts exclude qualification and any separate bare
or auxiliary instruments. They cover all author checks only IF a separately
reviewed complete-record instrument genuinely elicits and scores each required
dimension from the same calls; that has not yet been established.

Do not treat this affordable envelope as a powered design. If exact allocation,
world clustering and plausible reader accuracies do not provide useful operating
characteristics, report that now. A different valid prespecified package or a
narrower future claim is a new decision, not permission to keep enlarging a run.

## Concrete design-review deliverable

Before any bank is made, an independent reviewer should return accept/revise/decline
on these six matters, with reasons rather than a general invitation to participate:

1. The author-approved method policy and the operative rule/attestation identity.
2. A world-sampling specification for operational consequences, not definitions;
   no hidden identity, group-membership or people-count golds.
3. A complete-record response instrument that separately observes the five nonclaims
   and corresponding explicit-fact controls; all-unknown and fixed-option shortcuts
   must fail. Repeated questions are clustered within their original world.
4. Fixed form weights and actual hash-based reader/arm allocation; do not equate
   pooled reader-cell weighting under a resample with fixed equal reader weights.
   Reader-specific results are diagnostic unless separately required by the author.
5. Prospective operating characteristics at equality, small tolerated loss, the
   -5 pp boundary and worse loss, including high accuracy/degeneracy, reader dependence,
   clustered worlds, absent responses and both runs. Preservation power is not
   ratification power: a confirmed negative generic stance can still veto admission.
6. The whole-cost budget, correct filing architecture under the cell cap, exact
   stopping rule, original executor and an actually accepted independent replication
   seat with the required model/settings access. An invitation is not a seat.

No model calls or downloads were made for this document. This is a bounded handoff
to remove a real choice, not a new global governance gate or an assertion that
every Ainglish ballot needs this prospective rule.
