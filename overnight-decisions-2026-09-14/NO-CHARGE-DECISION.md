## Recovered source: a concrete freshness limitation

The local resolver could not resolve dpaste.com. A public DNS lookup returned the site's
actual apex backend; a TLS-verified request using that resolution recovered the source.
The array digest is exactly `193a6b7e7b81078c031a0e1c76aef24286a831f1a5357a845ba0fd47b68e9b65`.
`source-audit/saturnia-price-items-mirror.json` is an unchanged-content durable mirror;
`audit_price.py` and `price-audit.json` make the following finding reproducible.

**128/128 replica cases have the same complete wording, questions, option mappings, gold
answers and strata as the 128-case original after normalising just the 730x/830x resource,
invoice and pool identifiers and the stated observation timestamp.** The item metadata
IDs also differ. Literal complete-pair hashes differ, but this is not 128 new semantic
scenarios. Both original and replica have only eight underlying domain frames.

That does not prove misconduct, invalidate the measured outputs automatically, or alter
the server's `settlement_eligible=true` / `reproduced_ok=false` classification. It does
mean the result should not be presented as successful generalisation to new scenario
content. The claim is narrower: a differently labelled/allocated presentation of the
same authored semantic cases gave sharply different performance.

From the served replay attestation:

| Reader | Marked correct | English correct | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Gemma3 12B | 12/64 | 18/64 | −9.375 pp |
| Mistral Small 3.2 24B | 56/64 | 4/64 | +81.25 pp |

The same reported model digests/settings are useful provenance, but these results alone
cannot identify whether allocation, lexical/identifier sensitivity, instrument behaviour
or some unrecorded factor caused the difference. Raw answer and serialized request journals
are the next useful evidence, not another target run chosen to arbitrate the sign.

## Requested next decision

1. Ask Saturnia to assess the scenario-freshness statement and publish the original request
   and answer journals if retained. Keep the finite positive result visible during review.
2. Ask Morgan for a digest-preserving public mirror; a local filesystem path is not a
   remotely retrievable replication kit. This is source recovery, not invalidation by default.
3. Do not ask a new reader to repeat this bank as an independent semantic confirmation.
   A justified future original should use genuinely new operational situations, retain the
   nine-state joint answer space, and separately test the remaining permission/health,
   deposit/future-charge and corruption obligations. Freeze its question, not a desired sign.
4. An author decision request or a substantive successor remains reasonable if the current
   version's claimed performance cannot be supported. Failure to prove non-inferiority is
   not proof that every future trained reader or human will find the distinction unsuitable.

Current token prerequisites, cold comprehension, prompt-time learning, future weight training
and current-model uncertainty are separate facts. No adverse finding is erased by English's
training/tokenizer incumbency, and no present finding proves what future exposure will do.
