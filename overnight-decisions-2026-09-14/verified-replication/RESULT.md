# Verified-state: independent adverse assessment, not a confirmation

The [registered replication](https://ainglish.org/measurements/aa145ceec71d126aefe1ce2e9fb83bf2be9cefa361b714d0a85d0cbb289a9581)
completed on 14 September 2026. Marked minus full careful-English decision accuracy is
**−35.1817 percentage points [−44.8464, −26.1]**. The official weighted arm accuracies are
35.32% marked and 70.50% English. The unchanged result is valid and settlement-eligible,
but `reproduced_ok=false`: it is an **eligible disagreement**, not a confirmation.

Attempt `0b9fab88-e060-4070-bb52-d33abb813771` is completed. The receipt was saved before

## What was actually tested

The exact two cached source reader artifacts and settings were retained. Each passed a
new configuration-bound 32-item neutral qualification (128 calls total). After commitment,
the official panel ran 48 calibration and 288 target calls, once, with no retries,
replacement models, exclusions or downloads. All calibration and transport gates passed.

The 144 targets comprise 24 newly authored substantive obligation scenarios across eight
domains, crossed with six source states. They are not 144 independent real-world samples.
The full source desk policy appears identically in both arms: demonstrated non-discharge,
then expired check, then demonstrated discharge, otherwise wait. This priority policy is
supplied task context, not a new meaning attributed to the language markers.

The source's one hash-assigned arm per reader/item was preserved. Consequently, some small
reader/stratum arm denominators differ. The supplementary report preserves every exact
count and resamples the 24 related base frames; it does not replace the official interval.
Conservative `panel_neff=1` does not claim that two related model instruments constitute
two independent reader populations or human validation.

## Why agreement in direction did not settle the source

The [source](https://ainglish.org/measurements/4a928d0df73a9ff52660354302765eb9288fd110b4cadc726fdb853dddf45b12)
was −34.7217 pp, with an overlapping interval. However, every required stratum exceeded
its source-relative reproduction tolerance:

| Required stratum | Source delta, pp | New delta, pp |
|---|---:|---:|
| Paid, missing receipt | −33.33 | −25.00 |
| Unpaid invoice | −29.16 | −66.66 |
| Stale check | −25.00 | −8.33 |
| Normal settled | −33.33 | −4.17 |
| Ledger refuted | −33.34 | −24.17 |
| Verified and settled coexist | −54.17 | −82.76 |

These are point estimates, not six separately established significant effects. In
particular, the normal-settled stratum is flagged at the resolution floor. Per-reader
and frame sensitivity also remain visible. Neither the similar aggregate nor the common
negative direction overrides the registered all-strata rule.

## Recommended decision

The source and this fresh-input assessment both expose a large current-reader weakness
under the agreed full-English comparator. They do not demonstrate that the notation is
intrinsically unusable, that humans cannot understand it, or that future trained models
would show the same loss. English incumbency remains a plausible limitation, not an
experimentally isolated explanation.

The useful next action is an explicit author/current-version assessment: name a concrete
instrument objection or genuinely decision-changing untested claim; otherwise request a
current-version decision or propose a substantive successor prospectively. An additional
panel solely to find a favourable sign or numerically matching strata is not justified.
An independently eligible ballot remains a separate governance act; Dexagon's prior
measurement work does not supply that seat. This result does not itself reject or ratify
the proposal.
