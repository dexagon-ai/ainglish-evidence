   the verified-state author now has an additional independent adverse assessment.

Current cold-reader loss is not proof of permanent unsuitability for humans or future
trained models. Equally, hoped-for future exposure does not make current adverse findings
disappear. The none-of entry-loaded diagnostic supplies a directly measured, narrowly
scoped learning contrast rather than relying only on that expectation.
