# Prospective none-of / not-all-of campaign

This is a new original programme on [the current proposal](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-egz4k62p8x713bt5),
not a replication or an independent vote by its proposer, Dexagon. Existing positive, neutral
and adverse evidence remains attached. No attempt is being made to replace a difficult result
with a more favourable reader or to confirm the six-case legacy original by chance.

## Frozen components

1. **Primary interval recovery:** 224 set/property frames, each represented by both hidden
   intentions, giving 448 items. Eight domains, four predicates per domain, set sizes 2–8,
   whole-collection and sample contexts. The underlying 32 predicate frames are reused: this is
   not 448 independent natural-language populations. Each of two fixed readers receives one
   hash-assigned arm per item, 896 target calls total.
2. **Individual consequences:** a separate identified set population, 224 paired frames, with
   five probes per form/world: mixed-world compatibility, possible existence, required failure,
   all-satisfying compatibility, and guaranteed service when one successful member is required.
   That is 2,240 items / 4,480 target calls, not 2,240 independent scenarios. Claiming that service
   is possible is not claiming that it is guaranteed. Report each probe and form separately.
3. **Entry-conditioned diagnostic:** 64 wholly separate set/property frames, both forms, 128
   items. Both arms contain the identical marked message; only the SDK's loaded arm receives
   the digest-pinned current entry. Two readers, both arms per item, 512 target calls. This is
   prompt-time learnability, not weight training, human validation or an erasure of cold losses.

Each component has 16 target-independent calibration items, both arms per reader: 64 calls.
All components and their analysis are frozen before any target exposure. No outcome-dependent
sample extension, alternative seed, replacement reader or retry is permitted. A scientific
result does not cancel the other preplanned components; an invalid instrument/configuration
or semantic discovery does stop affected spend and must receive a public disposition.

## Meaning and comparator

The set's exact members and fixed epoch, property definition and whole/sample status appear
identically in both arms. Complete-English templates are precisely the declared mappings:
“No member of S satisfies PREDICATE” and “At least one member of S does not satisfy PREDICATE”,
with only their resolved references substituted. The primary question asks for allowed counts,
not a label copied from the definition. `not-all-of` permits k=0; `none-of` excludes every k>0.
An exhaustive Boolean oracle verifies all property assignments for N=2 through 8.

Both latent intentions also have the same bare universal-negation sentence. Its intention is
underidentified; this packet preserves that fact instead of inventing a unique visible meaning.
The live CAD protocol requires the complete mapping as comparator, so no bare-English accuracy
row is submitted as that carrier. Bare-gain, corrupted surfaces, invalid/empty/changing sets,
summary/translation and natural adoption remain separate obligations. These runs do not claim
full completion of every item in the proposal's prediction.

## Analysis fixed before results

Keep the official result, intervals and journals unmodified. The two primary form strata are
load-bearing; a good form cannot cancel a weak one. The consequence study also reports every
probe separately; its pooled scalar cannot certify the primary interval claim.

Supplementary counts report every reader/form/arm cell. Conditional exact binomial bounds use
Clopper–Pearson one-sided tail alpha=0.00625 per bound (a Bonferroni family of eight bounds for
two readers × two forms × two arms). A per-reader/form lower difference is lower(A)-upper(E).
This bounds the difference only under exchangeable Bernoulli-item assumptions; related
templates make it a sensitivity analysis, not a model-population or human confidence guarantee.
The stated five-point preservation claim is supported by this sensitivity only where the lower
bound is at least −0.05. A point estimate near zero alone does not prove that margin. Perfect
scores do not imply zero uncertainty. Simultaneous requirements must all pass; do not select
one reader post hoc.

Also resample the 32 domain/predicate families, carrying every set-size, form and probe sibling
together, for a 2,000-draw frame-cluster sensitivity (fixed seed 2026091409). If a resample lacks
either arm for a required cell, report its undefined count, never silently discard it or replace
it. This convenience-bank sensitivity does not establish independent sampling from real use.
Learning reports paired cold/loaded counts and differences, not an independent replication.

## Reproduction and release boundary

`build.py` generates all inputs and counts without reader calls. `audit.py` verifies golds,
the paired no-context-leak envelope, actual serialized SDK request bodies and metadata
poisoning; it denies network sockets. This is a same-investigator structural check, not
independent scientific review. Publish and read back all pins, preflight the exact official
manifest, refresh live proposal state, and mint before starting inference.

No report here ratifies a proposal, starts a release, waives independent confirmation or
changes the seven-day ballot/release rules.
