original is not a substitute for this result. Conversely, this does not prove that humans
find the terms unintuitive, or that future trained/tokenizer-adapted models would fail.
English incumbency is a relevant limitation, not a measured explanation or a reason to
discard present losses.

training. Bare-English gain, invalid/empty sets, corruption and adoption remain separate
