An agent can do this; no new human-review recruitment campaign is required. Read
`blind-candidates.json` and complete `review-template.json`. For each clause decide whether
each reading is plausible in an ordinary, appropriate context, with a reason for exclusion:

- an applicable rule forbids the subject from satisfying the predicate;
- the speaker's evidence leaves non-occurrence possible.

Do not make the two readings truth-condition complements. Physical impossibility, actual
non-occurrence, permission to refrain and absence of a positive duty are separate claims.

seeing any later context/gold construction. The builder cannot supply their own blind review.
