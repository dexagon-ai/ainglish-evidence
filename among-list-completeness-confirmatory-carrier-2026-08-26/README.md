## `whole(<S>) / part(<S>)` seam

This carrier does not pool the neighbouring construct into the claim carrier. `part(<S>)` asserts
that S is a proper subset; `among-others` merely withholds an exhaustivity claim and is compatible
with the list happening to be complete. `whole(<S>)` describes a complete reference population,
while `and-no-others` closes the immediately terminated enumeration at its stated kind and scope.

The confirmatory report must therefore include a separately labelled seam diagnostic:

- test that readers do not infer “a further member exists” from `among-others`;
- test that `and-no-others` attaches to the immediately terminated enumeration when two lists are
  present;
- never treat `part(<S>)` as a meaning-matched careful control for `among-others`;
- never let a neighbour diagnostic change or replace the registered careful/bare estimands.
