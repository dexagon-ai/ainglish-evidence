# Ainglish language-growth cohort v1

This packet turns the flagship backlog into a rolling **six-entry** language cohort. It is a
quality-controlled decision queue, not a promise to ratify six entries and not a model result.

## The six

| Construct | Editorial lane | Evidence lane | Current decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| `moved-earlier / moved-later` | 5/5 flagship | advance | settle four form/comparator originals and file tag fidelity |
| `one-or-more / exactly-one` | 5/5 flagship | advance | run the frozen form-separated original when the reader gate clears |
| `among-others / and-no-others` | 5/5 flagship | repair then decide | retain the confirmed +2.5-token cost and test the prospectively stated comprehension trade-off |
| `may-as-permission / may-as-possibility` | 5/5 flagship | contract and instrument repair | align the served prerequisite with the pre-result +4 bound, then repair the low-floor probe |
| `some-or-all / some-but-not-all` | 5/5 flagship | repair or retire | preserve the near-null/adverse disagreement and require a declared settlement-or-redesign choice |
| `whole(S) / part(S)` | 4/5 showcase | settle or retire | obtain a third independent carrier or remove it from the flagship lane |

An entry leaves only through a recorded decision: ratification, rejection, narrowing, supersession,
or explicit evidence-blocked deferral. Only then is a replacement admitted. This prevents new ideas
from hiding unresolved adverse evidence.

## Process correction

Two separately screened proposals were filed after this packet was published, before any cohort
entry exited. That was stricter than the user's authorisation but contrary to this packet's own
replacement-pool timing rule. They remain outside the six-entry cohort and are not replacements.
`process-correction.json` records the deviation; no further discovery candidate may be filed or
admitted until a cohort exit is recorded. The proposals' semantic and deterministic checks are
unchanged by this workflow correction.

## Evidence design

Each entry keeps three questions separate:

1. **Zero-shot comprehension** is the governance claim carrier. Marked Ainglish is compared
   separately with bare ambiguous English and its complete careful-English meaning.
2. **Definition-conditioned learnability** asks whether a reader can learn the distinction from
   one digest-bound register entry and apply it to unseen, byte-identical scientific messages. It
   is a separate diagnostic and cannot erase a poor zero-shot result.
3. **Current token price** is reported under the tokenizers that exist now. It is not comprehension
   evidence and is not represented as the cost after hoped-for future Ainglish training.

Every pole remains visible. Careful-English inferiority, false cross-inference, calibration
failure, transport loss, and independent disagreement remain veto-bearing rather than being pooled
away.

## Current execution boundary

The local Ollama inventory contains many models, but its retained no-retry audit has only one
qualified base-model lineage. The cohort instruments require two. GPU availability therefore does
not authorize reader spend: no model was called and no attempt was minted while that gate remained
closed. Exact frozen carriers and external handoffs already exist for the useful seats.

## Lightweight human role

The existing 5/5 and 4/5 scores are builder/editor judgements under the published five-check
flagship screen. No new native-speaker validation is attributed by this packet. A future operator
confirmation may be recorded per construct as a bounded editorial observation, never as measured
comprehension or representative preference.
