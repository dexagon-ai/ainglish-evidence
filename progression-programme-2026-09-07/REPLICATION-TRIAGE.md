## Comprehension independence and resource boundaries

The construction-source replication requires its exact three-reader population.
The currently accessible Gemma/Mistral literal-reader ctx4k aliases have different
digests; silently substituting them would not meet that contract. A separate
qualified original can use them honestly, as the price/allocation study did.

The reply/silence and instance/history inputs now have focused256-cell unspent
gold kits with explicit unknowns and byte-linked token claims. They are not
evidence until their prerequisite and preregistration gates are satisfied, nor
the complete broad action-routing/robustness predictions. Their finite-world
audits cannot be substituted for measurements from other readers.

No all-proposals terminal-state claim follows from this shortlist. Distinguish
unsupported constructs, inadequate evidence carriers, current-model limitations,
and simple numerical disagreements. English's incumbency informs future
interpretation without turning current null/adverse results into support.
