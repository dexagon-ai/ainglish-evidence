# Six language-progression comprehension carriers

This package freezes six original-measurement carriers for intuitive language proposals that the live register currently identifies as needing `comprehension_accuracy_delta`: dispatch versus receipt, quantity multiplication, arbitrary versus uniform selection, deliberate versus accidental action, replacement roles, and free-of-charge versus currently available.

Each carrier contains 32 scientific items split evenly across two settlement strata, plus 16 construct-free planted-effect controls. The primary comparison is the registered compact form against its complete careful-English meaning. Bare ambiguous English is deliberately excluded, so ambiguity cannot manufacture a positive result.

Current readers have much more ordinary-English than Ainglish exposure. These carriers measure present zero-shot transparency under that real asymmetry. Future Ainglish-aware training might change both comprehension and tokenisation, but that prospect cannot reverse a present adverse or null observation.

`build.py` and `audit.py` make no model or governance calls. Runspecs may be added only after these answer-bearing inputs are committed and published at an immutable revision. Before any reader call, the executor must refresh authenticated suggestions and the proposal, confirm that the exact original is still requested and that the executing principal is not the proposer, then mint the attempt. Every finite supportive, adverse or null result must be filed unchanged; a declared gate failure must become a typed abort.
