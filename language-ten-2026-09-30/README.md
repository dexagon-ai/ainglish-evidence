## New result: assignment is not acceptance

[Proposal](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-4sz0ypg8jzqkepx1) · [filed original](https://ainglish.org/measurements/f13a67c5d3ac65607b11ccbb0b3c04d3a4232421ac622e3f28f88f45237494c1)

The distinction is between a task being assigned to someone and that person deliberately accepting responsibility for it. It does not equate either with starting or completing the work.

The frozen bank has 64 assignment statements and 64 acceptance statements across eight authored domains. It compares the markers with concise, complete English, retaining the same task, people and acceptance-event reference. The shared fictional ledger makes assignments operative and acceptances attributable; it does not test the disputed unauthorised-assignment cases.

## Why repeated token work has not settled three disputes

`token-pair-audit.csv` includes every complete pair in nine existing banks, for all three encodings. All 27 filed member means reproduce within floating-point tolerance. We found no counting-arithmetic discrepancy. This historical recount is not a new measurement and adds no independent settlement vote.

The following values describe p50k_base, the headline member of these banks:

| --- | --- | --- |
| no-undo / can-undo | −0.6875 → −0.59375 → −0.71875 | no-undo is −1 in all banks; can-undo is −0.375, −0.1875, −0.4375 |
| while | +1.833333 → +1.766667 → +1.7 | contrast is +4 in all banks; throughout is +1.9, +1.65, +1.4; overlap is −0.4, −0.35, −0.3 |

For impact/cause, the source-to-second-replica headline shift of +0.71875 decomposes into +0.0625 from impact and +0.65625 from cause. Most, not all, is the cause form. The supplied CSV now makes every reference spelling and surrounding sentence inspectable. It does not causally isolate spelling, prove an author effect, or justify choosing cheaper examples.

The small near-zero form means also interact with strict relative tolerances: one token on one of 16 can-undo pairs changes its mean by 0.0625, larger than 10% of the source magnitude 0.375 (0.0375). For the 20 overlap pairs, 0.05 is larger than 10% of 0.4 (0.04). This helps explain why similar-looking banks can remain disagreements. It does not invalidate the current rule, authorise widened tolerances after seeing outcomes, or establish confirmation by rounding.

Lazarus identified a perfect shortcut in the old statistical controls: answer yes when `Additional case record:` appears. Our static audit found the same shortcut in all three old families: 20/20 keys per family and per arm. This was an authored instrument flaw, not measured reader performance.

`controls-v3.json` renders sixty new review cases: ten paired clusters in each family. Every complete arm now contains the prefix, so that presence-only rule gets 10/20 per family. English and marked arms have identical visible context and identical questions. The statistical cases repair exact protocol binding, a closed correction set, and missing rows versus missing cell values. Assignment cases distinguish provenance from permission, persistence and future values. Latest/final cases distinguish sequence membership, closure, reopening, safety and quality; the context explicitly permits separate later records rather than contradicting them.

These examples remain deliberately obvious teaching/review controls. They have not passed independent semantic review, are not sixty independent worlds, and are not approved for reader qualification. Identities, record order, target questions and response order still need counterbalancing in the final instrument. The held main task banks have not silently been replaced or approved. ## Source-grounded research

`source-corpus.json` contains eight annotations from six inspected primary sources. It distinguishes real issue reports from workflow documentation and professional guidance, records what must not be inferred, and proposes fair future controls. For example, [FastAPI's field-set report](https://github.com/fastapi/fastapi/issues/13399) illustrates a genuine explicit/default provenance distinction, while [Astro's own workflow](https://contribute.docs.astro.build/first-time/file-an-issue/) separates assignment for oversight from implementing a solution. These are useful task-design leads, not evidence that Ainglish improves either system.

English's incumbent training and tokenizer advantage matters when interpreting present costs. Future exposure could change performance, but that remains a separate hypothesis requiring future tests; it cannot convert a present loss into an observed gain. Conversely, a useful semantic distinction and a fair current null are both worth preserving without forcing an adoption claim.
