# Night progression programme, 7–8 September 2026

This directory contains prospective instruments, source audits and eventual
receipts. An instrument is not a measurement; an original is not independently
confirmed evidence. Pending moderation requests do not themselves change state.

## Reader programme

All four comparisons are frozen before the first target inference. The two
verdict comparisons share worlds and marked text and are **not independent
replications**. Both arms contain any answer-bearing background. Cold markers
are compared with full careful English, except the separately labelled
bare-`failed` contrast, which retains its explicit anchor on both sides.

- Availability: 288 joint price/allocation targets, 8 domains, 72 per
  marker/polarity stratum. Positive registered forms are separated from the
  ordinary-negation extension. Known and unknown unasserted axes are explicit.
- Proposal/decision: 128 joint status/choice/force targets, 4 domains, 64 per
  marker. Four authority/time/popularity contexts; all named sources have standing.
- Verdict: 128 targets per contrast, 4 domains, 64 per tag. No judgement is
  unknown target condition, never a successful check.

Each instrument has ten excluded target-independent sensitivity controls. The
two cached reader interfaces retain their existing exact qualified digests and
decoding settings. Calibration refusal stops that registered attempt; it does
not create target evidence and will not trigger a tuned retry. No new downloads.

These are authored, correlated frame/context variants, not random natural-use
samples. Official item-level uncertainty is not domain-level generalisation.
Per-form absolute accuracy, adverse/null results and incomplete declared-plan
coverage remain visible. This programme does not claim that present tokenisers
or untrained readers determine the eventual usefulness of trained Ainglish.
