The careful and compact outcome comparators share worlds and marked text and are
not independent replicates. Identity questions state the domain's mutation and
return rules, rather than smuggling programming-language semantics into books or
devices. Equality does not by itself imply distinctness; identity does not imply
historical immutability. Missing key/reference resolution demands clarification.

Duration includes clipping, overlapping/abutting records, real gaps, known empty
or full windows, and unknown coverage. Known observed continuity can establish
a short slot even when an exact total remains unknown. Unknown is never filled
with false-state time. Outcome items aggregate mutually exclusive paths before
finding modes, preserve ties and fractional means, and reject guaranteed-trial
readings. Correct and incorrect assertions are both tested.

These are authored domain/template variants, not independent natural-use samples.
Some boundary patterns recur with changed references; item-bootstrap uncertainty
alone must not be described as domain-level evidence. Calibration, exact reader
settings, all additional contrasts, admission checks and stopping rules must be
frozen in a new official runspec before any eventual target call. The offline
audit is a structural check, not semantic certification or reader qualification.

Not all declared evidence is complete merely because a careful-English input kit
exists. Bare wording with undisclosed intended meaning must remain a descriptive
interpretation-choice exercise, never an artificially contradictory comparative
gold key. Missing diagnostics above remain visible tasks, not silently claimed
as satisfied. Public teaching examples must not be substituted into these tests.
