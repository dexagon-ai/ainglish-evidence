## Named requirements and directions

| Requirement | Unacceptable parameter region | Required direction |
|---|---|---|
| CAD aggregate and each form | delta < -5 percentage points | lower bound at least -5 |
| Marked and careful accuracy, each form | accuracy < 0.90 | lower bound at least 0.90 |
| Each explicit-fact positive control | accuracy < 0.90 | lower bound at least 0.90 |
| Unsafe action, each form | rate > 0.05 | upper bound at most 0.05 |
| Each form by each of five nonclaims | false inference rate > 0.05 | upper bound at most 0.05 |

The strict token-saving carrier and the standing confirmed-loss veto are
unchanged. Passing a -5-point preservation test does not neutralize that veto.
The formal rule's storage rounding and a study's statistical decision must be
explicitly reconciled before use, not treated as an invisible threshold change.

For a bundle that requires every component, if one component is truly
unacceptable, the event “all components pass” is contained in “that component
falsely passes”. A valid size-at-most-0.05 component test therefore bounds the
bundle's false acceptance. Endpoint independence is not required for this
argument; it does not establish joint interval coverage or adequate power.
This is our statistical application of the co-primary-endpoint reasoning in
[FDA guidance, III.C.1](https://www.fda.gov/media/162416/download), not an FDA
assessment of Ainglish.

## What the review changes, and what it does not

- Require genuinely separate observed answers for gender, known identity,
  unanimity, all-members participation and collective action. Each gets
  explicit-fact controls, per-dimension failures, and shortcut checks. Do not
  recode one answer as five successes. Correlated outcomes are allowed and
  remain clustered within their world; statistical independence between
  those five outcomes is neither assumed nor a new requirement.
- Treat unmodelled shared frames as a validity failure, not a row that can be
  ignored. The published four-world-frame counterexample undercovers.
- Retain all published near-ceiling hold rates beside admission rates. Never
  choose a less capable reader to make errors and escape a degeneracy hold.
- The 2-point-loss simulation can yield evidence of a loss. “Cannot confirm”
  is not the right blanket description: it simulated no formal settlement,
  and its many negative intervals may activate the separate loss veto once
  properly confirmed. Preservation power is not ratification power.
