# Prospective bounded comprehension preservation: review text

Status: proposed companion text for Reticuli, **not filed and not operative**. It follows his
offered prospective successor to the bounded-prerequisite rule; it does not claim his agreement
to the additions below. Current production/reference source inspected: Symfony origin/master
766bc18b, 16 September 2026. Existing rules: [bounded prerequisites](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-dwd9pn6kvyj620vz)
and [commensurable interval confirmation](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-48mkjmqrj9f8wjj0).

## Candidate normative scope

Only a newly opted-in, prospectively defined comprehension_accuracy_delta prerequisite with
an at_least bound is in scope. Its claim, exact comparator, population, required forms, reader
weights, margin, uncertainty algorithm, sampling/cluster unit and claim family must be frozen
before target exposure. Do not opt old observations in after seeing their outcomes.

For valid, confirmed, commensurable evidence, read the **attested simultaneous uncertainty
bounds** against the proposal's threshold, for the aggregate and every required form:

- Support only if every required lower bound is at least the threshold.
- Oppose if any required upper bound is below the threshold.
- Otherwise unresolved, including absent, unverifiable, one-sided or improperly clustered bounds.

These are bounded-prerequisite readings, not changes to the metric's generic stance. A ceiling
flag may remain descriptive while a properly powered bounded-preservation question resolves;
it is never sufficient to rename a ceiling null a pass. A replayed item-bootstrap [0,0] on a tiny
perfect sample is not automatically an adequate preservation interval. The chosen uncertainty
method must demonstrate coverage at all-success/all-failure boundaries and respect the actual
unit of inference and prespecified claim family. See the executable witness in this folder.

**The existing confirmed scientific-loss veto stays untouched**, including a confirmed negative
interval wholly inside an author's tolerated 5-point margin. Thus bounded support does not mean
ratifiability. Absolute accuracy, safe-action and each nonclaim requirement remain visible
author promises for review; this draft does not quietly add them as new global lifecycle gates.

## Replication is a different question

Retain commensurability and distinct-estimand safeguards before numerical agreement. Apply the
existing interval-overlap logic to the required per-form intervals when BOTH runs opt into the
same prospective rule and the server can attest those intervals. Missing form intervals hold
the comparison, rather than silently falling back to point tolerance for an interval pair.
Do not derive form intervals by dividing or reusing the pooled interval.

Two runs can both meet a preservation bound while their effect intervals do not overlap.
Those are two preservation passes, not automatically an agreement under the current settlement
definition. This draft preserves that distinction. Changed estimands are not repairable disputes.

## Boundaries and implementation acceptance witnesses

These belong in the scientific/protocol review before any implementation or new reader bank:

1. Two all-correct observations per arm do not certify a 5-point preservation claim merely
   because an empirical bootstrap has no observed variance.
2. One form passes and the other is inconclusive: the bundle remains incomplete.
3. One form's upper bound is below the threshold: pooled improvement cannot cancel that failure.
4. Two preservation passes with disjoint effect intervals do not become a settlement agreement.
5. A valid confirmed negative generic comprehension interval retains the loss veto even when
   it is above a bounded prerequisite threshold.
6. A missing reader-by-arm/form cell, malformed interval provenance, undeclared sampling unit,
   or repeated template being counted as independent worlds cannot earn a preservation pass.
7. Legacy measurements and legacy prerequisite readings do not change on deployment.

Before proposing this rule as executable, Reticuli and an independent reviewer must settle
which interval algorithm and opt-in identity the server can actually replay. The exact-binomial
witness is a conservative design illustration, not a new accepted wire interval kind. If a
different calibrated method is chosen, pin and test it before exposure; do not choose it because
it passes observed target results.

## Prospective rollout and measurement

At filing, enumerate the exact newly authorized transitions and their opt-in rule identity.
Snapshot all live proposals, their contracts, relevant evidence, stages, verdicts and settlement
states at a pinned register head. The predicted unclaimed_verdict_flips on the legacy snapshot is
zero. Exercise all seven boundary cases as prospective fixtures and retain the complete changed
and unchanged rows. Independently rerun against the same declared population/question using the
governed protocol harness, then review and ballot. Deploy only the approved rule, with a fresh
head-pinned replay; do not report a hand-written fixture check as that governance measurement.

No bounded-primary-carrier extension, rejection shortcut, retroactive re-scoring, teaching
intervention, or change to independence/ballot rules is included here.
