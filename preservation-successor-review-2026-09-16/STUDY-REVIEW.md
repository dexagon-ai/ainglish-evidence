# One complete study, only after the scope decisions

Status: **candidate design for independent review**. No target bank, source attempt, reader
qualification, replication commitment or statistical power claim is asserted by this document.
The existing 5 pp margin is retained for author review; it is not enlarged to rescue earlier data.

## Exact scientific question

On a prospectively frozen population of operational consequence tasks, does the two-form bundle
save tokens against its complete careful-English expansion while preserving comprehension in
each form, maintaining useful absolute performance and avoiding five explicitly excluded
inferences? The cold-reader result describes the declared existing reader configurations, not
humans, every model, or future trained/tokenizer-aware Ainglish performance.

The proposed exact subject spans are:

| Form | Complete careful-English subject |
| --- | --- |
| they-one | that one person or entity |
| they-many | those two or more people or entities |

The remaining message is identical. Use agreement-invariant predicates such as 'will submit'
or 'can access'. No extra identity, people-count, unanimity, all-members or collective-action
information may appear only in English. A committee can be one referent without being one person.
The changed mapping must be part of the author's successor before it is used as its comparator.

## Instrument and scoring

- Ask the held-out operational consequence, never merely 'one or many?', 'how many people?', or
  which definition was used. Answer labels must not leak through only one arm's mapping.
- Keep at least two feasible antecedent-number readings for the bare control. Before seeing
  intended form or gold, the design reviewer must reject cases where context, verb agreement,
  salience or an added identity makes the number evident. Freeze exclusion reasons before calls.
- Marked and careful arms receive the same entailed-action gold. Bare 'they' receives an
  arm-specific clarification/unknown gold when its text leaves the consequence unresolved.
  Do not score a safe English clarification as a misunderstanding.
  The current official panel has one gold answer per item, not an arm-specific gold schema.
  Therefore the bare control is a SEPARATE descriptive analysis, not a bare-versus-marked
  comprehension_accuracy_delta payload forced through that single-gold harness.
- Separately report correct comprehension, safe actionable resolution, clarification and unsafe
  action. Information gain from making a vague sentence explicit is not superior comprehension
  of meaning-equivalent careful English.
- Audit gender, known identity, unanimity, all-members participation and collective action
  separately for each form. Include shared-context positive controls where the fact IS stated,
  so always answering unknown cannot pass the audit. Positive-control accuracy is a separate
  bound; it is not diluted into the nonclaim false-inference denominator.
- Original and replication banks are authored independently from the SAME approved construction
  specification, with genuine disjoint semantic worlds. Names/option permutations do not count
  as new independent worlds. Freeze grouping and all answer-bearing bytes before inference.

## Candidate quantitative acceptance criteria (author approval required)

All of these are declared before exposure, not selected after a run:

1. Token carrier: strictly negative least-favourable registered-tokenizer mean delta versus the
   complete careful-English messages; also disclose each form/tokenizer. Require savings in both
   forms as an author promise. This is stronger than the author's current +1 tolerance.
2. CAD: a valid simultaneous lower confidence bound >= -5 pp for each form and for the aggregate.
3. Marked AND careful absolute comprehension: simultaneous lower bound >= 90% in each form.
4. Marked unsafe-action rate: simultaneous upper bound <= 5% in each form.
5. Each of the five form-specific nonclaim false-inference rates: simultaneous upper bound <= 5%.
   Matched positive-control correct-inference accuracy: simultaneous lower bound >= 90%.
6. The standing confirmed-loss veto and all independent ratification gates must still clear.

The 90% floors and the simultaneous method below are new candidate design choices, not author
decisions. A failed bounded claim, a generic scientific-loss veto, low accuracy, and inconclusive
precision must be reported separately. Noninferiority does not mean identical performance.

## Unit of inference, uncertainty and sample planning

Proposed confirmatory reader roster: the existing qualified Gemma3-12B and Mistral-Small-3.2-24B
opaque-choice wrappers, pinned by full model digest, settings and qualification receipts.
Use equal reader weights and report each reader, not just a pooled score. No new model downloads.
The replicator must verify actual access before this roster becomes an execution commitment.

A conservative candidate analysis uses exact one-sided binomial marginal bounds within each
reader/form/arm. Difference bounds are L(marked)-U(English) and U(marked)-L(English); weighted
averages preserve the simultaneous guarantee without assuming independence BETWEEN readers or
endpoints. Binomial assumptions still require independently sampled valid semantic-world units
within each marginal population. If the real bank is clustered or purposively selected instead,
this calculation is NOT applicable as a generalization interval: specify and approve an
appropriate cluster/sampling method first. It is not legitimate to call repeated questions or
renamings independent to meet a denominator.

For two fixed readers and two forms, allocate family error 0.05 over 60 one-sided bounds:
16 main accuracy bounds (two arms, two tails), four marked unsafe-action upper bounds, 20
nonclaim upper bounds and 20 positive-control lower bounds. Derived pooled/form difference
bounds reuse these marginals. Bare-control descriptive intervals are labelled exploratory and
do not enter the confirmatory pass decision. Independence between the 60 endpoints is not
required for the union-bound coverage argument.

The executable witness prints zero-error boundary limits and conservative main-comparison
limits at several denominators. These are design calculations, not target measurements or a
power calculation. The final sample size must be chosen from a preregistered operating-characteristic
study over plausible performance levels, including small losses, BEFORE bank generation/calls.
It must account for the actual official panel allocation, missing cells, semantic clusters and
independent replications. Do not silently switch to paired data: the current harness assigns
each reader an arm per item. No power or bank-size choice is claimed complete here.

The existing item-bootstrap wire interval is not this exact-binomial family interval. Do not
label a local report as server-attested or expect it to change the current evidence gate. The
prospective protocol review must settle the replayable accepted method before execution.

There is a less conservative option to discuss before adopting a large design: if the sole
confirmatory decision is that ALL prespecified endpoints pass, an intersection-union test can
control false acceptance of that conjunction using valid marginal component tests without a
60-way Bonferroni adjustment. That does NOT provide 60 simultaneous intervals or permission to
promote whichever subset succeeds, and joint power still needs planning. The distinction is
described in [FDA's multiple-endpoint guidance, section III.C.1](https://www.fda.gov/media/162416/download).
Applying that statistical logic here is a proposed inference, not an FDA assessment of Ainglish.
Reticuli and the author should choose the claim/reporting policy prospectively; the conservative
witness is not a reason to impose unnecessary model calls or a new global multiplicity gate.

## Bounded execution and independent seats

Before any target calls, collect actual receipts for: author-approved exact successor and its
dry-run; approved rule and deployment; independently reviewed construction/scoring/sample plan;
an original executor; an independent replication executor with the same accessible qualified
reader class; and at least one separate eligible ballot reviewer who does not measure/certify
the result. Invitations are not accepted seats. Prior source knowledge is disclosed.

Freeze the final population, seeds, worlds, options, arm-specific gold, assignments, cluster IDs,
estimand, qualifications, controls, retry/abort policy, sample size and stopping rule. Read the
live runbook/protocol/template, refresh the successor and target hashes, preflight, and mint
before the first inference. Use the official harness once. Retain null/adverse outcomes and
all failed qualification/aborted attempts; do not retry a favorable subset or substitute models.

After the complete original and pre-accepted independent verification, decide: the claim passes
and warrants an independent ballot; the claim fails and warrants non-adoption/revision; or it is
inconclusive and any further study needs a specific new justification. No automatic enlarged
bank, wider margin, new gloss, taught condition, or successful-only filing.
