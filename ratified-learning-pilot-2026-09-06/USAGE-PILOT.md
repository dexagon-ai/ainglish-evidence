# Try a distinction in real work

An optional, low-overhead field pilot—not a formal measurement or a new proposal gate.

If a ratified distinction fits your next genuine handoff, deadline or status report, use it once
and note whether it helped. Ordinary English remains a good choice when the distinction is not
needed. No GPU, new model or human-review panel is required.

Useful starting points include `we-including-you` versus `we-excluding-you`, `start-by(t)` versus
`complete-by(t)`, and `fact-not-known` versus `choice-not-made`. Read the current full mapping via
the SDK/API before use. State authority and task scope separately; a marker does not grant permission.

Record only what you can observe:

- Which real task and construct? Which source version?
- Was the recipient given a definition or example?
- What interpretation did the recipient explicitly express?
- Was clarification or correction needed? What happened next?
- Was the job completed, abandoned or still unresolved?
- If exact tokenizer/call accounting is available, what did all turns cost? Otherwise leave it unknown.

A response to this invitation is **prompted use**, not natural uptake. A synthetic exercise remains
synthetic. Do not manufacture extra messages or count a construct mention as successful use.
An author's English rewrite is not an observed controlled counterfactual. Non-response is unknown,
not a successful or failed interpretation. Preserve awkward outcomes alongside useful examples.

Private content stays private. Send a short non-sensitive description, not transcripts of private
work; we will ask separately before publishing an excerpt or reusing it as teaching data. A public
conversation is not automatically part of the CC0 language dataset. No training occurs merely because
the language appeared in a conversation; model-weight exposure needs its own receipt.

The [teaching cards](TEACHING.md) and [train-only archive](teaching-supplement.zip) are synthetic
non-normative material, distinct from any observation gathered through this invitation.
