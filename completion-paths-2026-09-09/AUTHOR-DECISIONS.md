## Outcome terms: a smaller question, with real positive controls

[mean-outcome / likeliest-outcome](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-b4mw22e4g8tv0hqv)
needs an author decision about the task, not another four-bit aggregate. Supplying
calculations helped the binary task but did not solve the joint question. The
likeliest-outcome form remained weaker; the original noninferiority, accuracy and
benefit requirements have not been established.

Proposed next design for author review:

1. Keep both meanings, units, distribution references, tie policy and complete
   careful-English comparator unchanged. Use exactly the same definition exposure
   and distribution facts in both arms. Keep a compact technical-English comparison
   separate. Do not market supplied facts as model training.
2. Use one consequential question per trial, not a four-part answer. Keep separate
   primary results for each form and for raw-calculation versus supplied-facts tasks;
   do not combine whichever parts look favourable after seeing results.
3. Cross mean outside support, non-majority unique mode, tied modes, coincident
   mean/mode and paths that aggregate to the same value. Retain every original
   domain if the author intends to keep the full current claim.
4. Add model-relative probability-one **positive** controls using degenerate
   distributions, beside non-degenerate negative controls. A constant “not
   guaranteed” answer must fail the positive controls. Those controls are part of
   semantic evaluation, not target-dependent reader qualification.
5. Compute every gold with exact rational arithmetic. The accompanying
   `outcome-decision-cases.json` supplies a small, checked review set, including both
   yes and no answers about realizability, uniqueness, majority and certainty.
   It is not the future scientific sample or evidence of performance.

Decision requested: endorse this narrower diagnostic design, revise it, or retain
the current full claim and request an independent complete test. An endorsement
of the design does not amend the proposal or authorize relaxed adoption criteria.
If a new scientific claim is intended, amend openly before freezing new evidence.

## Attempt / ensure: separate effort and reporting from outcome

[attempt / ensure](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-mznv1j4k869me22t)
has an adverse current diagnostic. Readers mostly recovered whether the outcome
occurred; they struggled with whether the instruction was discharged. Shared
definitions helped attempt in this sample, but did not make ensure qualify.

Concrete revision question: is `ensure: X` solely a condition on the final state,
or also an obligation to try? The reviewed current diagnostic uses the former:
an already-true outcome need not require action. `attempt: X` requires the stated
attempt and an honest report; a failure report is not proof an attempt occurred.
Neither tag creates authority for risky retries or actions outside the request.

Recommend two separately scored questions, with crossed evidence of attempted
action, final outcome and reported failure. Include already-true outcomes, no
attempt despite success, a claimed attempt contradicted by records, and an unsafe
next step that must not be taken. Do not reinterpret the existing joint score as a
pass on its easier component. The original author is currently unavailable;
surface-maintenance custody is not authority to rewrite this substantive meaning.
A new author-endorsed revision or explicitly attributed successor is needed before
new qualification work. Retirement remains legitimate if the distinction has no
clear incremental value over concise English.
