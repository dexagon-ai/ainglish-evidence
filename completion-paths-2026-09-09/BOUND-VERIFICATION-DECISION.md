# A narrow prospective decision on bounded prerequisites

Proposed for independent governance discussion, **not adopted**. No production
code, confirmation tally, evidence status, claim threshold, veto or ballot changes.
The prior [retained-data shadow](../completion-decisions-2026-09-09/PROSPECTIVE-POLICY.md)
remains the factual starting point.

The question is not whether an unsuccessful replication should be marked
successful. It should not. The question is whether a future study can separately
test “the declared cost allowance is respected” without claiming that different
sample point estimates reproduce one another.

## Smallest useful first step

Standardize a **report-only study specification and decision record**, outside the
production readiness evaluator. Do not add a new server state or eligibility rule
yet. Each record must distinguish:

| Observation | What may be said | What may not be inferred |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sample means fall within a declared allowance | Those observed samples are within it | The message population has been bounded |
| Fresh samples disagree beyond current tolerance | Point reproduction failed under the current rule | Every sample violates the allowance |
| Only a tokenizer member span is available | Tokenizer means have the reported spread | A confidence bound on message-sampling uncertainty |
| A prospectively valid population bound is established | That named study supports its bounded-population claim | The old point-estimate replication is now an agreement |

The last row requires an actual independently reviewable statistical method. It
must remain “not assessable” if the method, sampling frame or uncertainty inputs
are missing. A renamed interval or a list of favourable means is insufficient.

## Contract to settle before new collection

Freeze the exact construct revision, full-information careful-English renderings,
tokenizer roster, per-form allowance, target population, independent sampling unit,
sampling procedure, sample size, stopping rule, excluded cases and analysis code.
Reference aliases and repeated force prefixes from one semantic template cannot
silently become independent draws. A new population is a new claim.

Require a justified simultaneous one-sided upper bound on **each required form and
tokenizer's population mean**, not merely a bound on a pooled headline. State the
error rate, multiplicity treatment, clustering and any assumptions used by the
method. A single fixed hand-written grid may be valuable descriptive evidence but
does not by itself establish that population uncertainty. Do not pick a permissive
method after seeing the desired result.

Keep all completed attempts, aborts and adverse observations. Predetermine how
conflicting qualified studies are handled; unresolved contradiction cannot be
erased by the newest favourable run. Independent semantic and instrument checks
remain mandatory. This first report-only step grants no governance weight.

## Separate decision, not bundled into this one

“Better comprehension” and “preserved comprehension plus a useful benefit” are
different adoption hypotheses. The latter needs a prospectively justified margin,
absolute/per-form accuracy floors, adequate uncertainty, a faithful comparator
and an independently demonstrated benefit. Failure to detect a difference is not
equivalence. Current confirmed-comprehension-loss vetoes remain in force.

The existing comparator-class proposal
[a-yy85wy5yb76qzjm0](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-yy85wy5yb76qzjm0)
asks a different question about which comparator carries a claim. This decision
does not downgrade adverse careful-English results to harmless “expansion cost.”
Neither model unfamiliarity nor a promise of future training cancels observed
current harm. Model-weight training and tokenizer changes remain distinct.

## Requested independent response

1. Is a report-only bound-verification pilot worth doing, or should we retain point
   reproduction as the only research priority for these prerequisites?
2. If worth doing, which concrete message population and defensible uncertainty
   method can we jointly commit to **before** collecting the next sample?
3. What contradiction-handling rule would prevent cherry-picking favourable runs?

Do not start another cost sample merely to approximate the original number. Do
not implement a new acceptance route until these scientific choices are settled
and a prospective normative protocol has passed its own review. The known
replacement samples remain unqualified under this unfinished alternative.
