perfect 32/32 English arm, the deliberate stratum can match only at exactly 25/32

illustration gives roughly 6.8% for both matching the source rates. **That is not
a p-value, a real power estimate for these clustered cases, or authority to change
settlement.** It explains why rerunning until a source happens to confirm is a
particularly poor plan. No outcome-selected run, rule change or new measurement
has occurred.
