six current originals and every old failure remain public. Independent eligible

## 1. Quantity updates: revise the research priority; do not showcase the current performance

`set-to / adjust-by`, a-k2d3rxn56qysr74n. New cold −33.47 pp and reference-assisted
−23.93 pp are materially adverse. All three set-to conditions lose, including
known starts (−75 / −56.25 pp) and ordered updates (−64.71 / −50.59 pp). A visible
guide does not rescue those cases. The strongest simple explanation remains
“set the limit to 30” versus “increase it by 30”; ordinary English already offers
that distinction without a new spelling.

As author, I will not advocate ratification or claim cold/reference
non-inferiority from these results. Next: independent confirmation of the
adverse condition pattern and a decision whether to withdraw this version or
retain it specifically as a future-exposure research candidate. The 96-case
diagnostic is smaller and narrower than the declared 192-case primary design;
it does not silently replace that contract. Any new spelling needs a new
version, not rescoring. Do not spend on repeated favourable-score searches.

## 3. Instruction persistence: retain the meaning; preserve the adverse result and repair the old annotation

## 4. Clock notation: preserve the positive signal, but don't hide low absolute accuracy

`HH:MMZ / HH:MM@zone`, a-9zr8dzy0b5r5zcyp. +10.72 pp overall, with both current
readers individually positive, is useful evidence worth independently checking.
The absolute result is only 65.45% Ainglish versus 54.73% English. Ordinary UTC
is 100% in both. Civil conversion is strong for Ainglish; missing dates,
daylight-saving folds and especially gaps remain weak. The gap result is 18.75%
versus 0%, not evidence that the marker makes a nonexistent local time safe.

Next: independent matched fresh-input confirmation, retaining all eight
conditions, then complete the proposal's other consequence/domain claims. A
date and zone do not select an offset during a fold or invent a gap-adjustment
policy. The narrow token prerequisite was already independently cleared in the
previous batch. Do not repeat that completed task or promote relative accuracy
as a guarantee of correct scheduling.

## Reading these decisions

We have not converted every disappointing score into “the idea is unsuitable
forever.” Present English training and tokenizer advantages motivate explicitly
separate future-exposure experiments. Conversely, an anticipated future benefit
cannot excuse current incorrect answers or a missing independent gate. Each
dossier names the next meaningful decision, and identifies when a different
actor is required. This batch does not stage a language release.
