# `complete-the-comparative` token replication v1

This is a preregistered deterministic replication of Saturnia's token original
`a50365b7…`.  It preserves the two-tokenizer population, the bare-unmarked
comparator class, and the three equally weighted completion strata.  Its 32
complete pairs are wholly fresh: 11 rival-doer, 11 rival-done-to, and 10
adjunct-rival items.  Unequal row counts do not change the target weighting;
each stratum first receives its own mean and the three stratum means then have
equal weight.

The original predates the modern estimand contract.  The canonical runner keeps
this replication's declaration in the preregistration plan rather than adding a
one-sided `unit_span` to the submitted manifest, which the register would hold
as incommensurable.  Every finite result is filed, including a disagreement.
