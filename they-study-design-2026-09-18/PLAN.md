## Population and sampling unit

The claim is restricted to short, self-contained operational messages drawn
from an explicitly published generator, and a **fixed two-reader roster**. It
does not generalise to all English, humans, other models, or future trained models.
Both forms remain required. Every message has one singular and one plural
semantically live antecedent; identity can remain unknown. Entity referents
(including committees) are counted as entities, not their member populations.

Proposed frame mixture: equal representation of access routing, notification,
task ownership, acknowledgement routing, per-recipient quotas, quorum records,
handover receipts and resource allocation. Each frame's policy must make a
consequence depend on referent number, not on an unstated identity or number
of represented people. The future reviewed generator must draw complete new
records, policies and relevant facts, not merely rename a few exposed templates.

Sample **1,024 actual semantic worlds per form** for core CAD. Within each
fixed frame, draw independent complete worlds; keep the inference conditional
on this specified finite frame mixture. Any shared generated record, latent
scenario or paraphrase family is one world/cluster. Changing names, option order
or question wording does not create another independent unit. If the generator
cannot meet that design, revise and revalidate the cluster analysis **before**
target exposure; do not declare the independence problem solved by new IDs.

The original and replica need independently authored disjoint worlds under
the same accepted frame definition and sampling law. Audit exact-arm/pair
overlap and shared record/semantic-family identity before either run. Both banks
and sampling plans must be frozen before the first target outcome is known.
No original-outcome-based repair of the independent bank is permitted.

## Contrasts, observations and golds

Only the pronoun slot differs in the primary contrast. The canonical English
expansions stay verbatim after substitution; both sides carry exactly the same
facts. Do not make the English side name a referent that the marker leaves open.
Consequence questions ask what action the stated policy permits/requires,
not the marker's definition or which pronoun appeared. Balance affirmative and
negative consequences within each form/frame without selecting a seed for its
realised arm balance. Gold keys must be derived from the explicit policy/record,
not from a hidden author's intended antecedent. An independent semantic audit
must verify the future complete inventory.

Five nonclaim dimensions retain separately elicited/scored answers: gender,
known identity, unanimity, all-member participation and collective action.
Partial information and cue words need their own cells; contrasting questions
on a shared record retain that shared world ID. One model response must not be
copied into multiple endpoint observations. Future raw requests, response text,
answer extraction and call IDs bind each answer to its exact prompt. The exposed
55-world control prototype remains a test fixture, not fresh scientific input.

## Analysis candidate

- Core: official SDK allocation and unmodified item-bootstrap scoring, 2,000
  draws, plus the proposed *operative, attested* pooled/per-form reading if it
  has been adopted. Freeze the actual two-reader allocation and realised arm
  weights; do not advertise exact equal reader weights when the pooled-per-arm
  estimator does not have them. Item bootstrap must respect the actual world unit.
- Keep the pooled and each-form -5 pp lower-bound requirement; a degenerate
  bootstrap is held under the candidate rule, not interpreted as equality.
  Retain all reader effects and the separate confirmed-loss veto.
- Absolute accuracy and unsafe action: a separately frozen **512-world per-form
  paired assay**, both readers and both renderings in isolated sessions. The
  same-world relationship remains explicit; this is not four independent worlds.
- Nonclaims: **512 worlds per form and dimension**, both readers and both
  renderings, one separately answered question per call. Marked false-inference
  limits stay 5%; careful-English results are reported as matched diagnostics.
- Explicit-fact controls: **128 fresh worlds per form and dimension**, both
  readers; 90% floor. These omit the target markers, discriminate the questions,
  and are separate from official planted-effect calibration.
- Proposed auxiliary bounds: exact one-sided Clopper-Pearson bounds separately
  for each fixed reader at alpha 0.025, averaged with weights 1/2. A union bound
  gives at least 95% marginal coverage for the fixed-roster mean, allowing arbitrary
  within-world dependence between readers. This requires iid sampled worlds
  within that endpoint's declared population: draw the auxiliary frame, policy,
  record and gold together from the fixed uniform-mixture sampling law, without
  conditioning on realised frame or answer counts. The core's fixed frame/gold
  quotas are NOT reused for this binomial analysis. Shared-record paraphrases
  are not independent trials. A stratified auxiliary design would require its
  own preapproved bounds; a binomial calculation is not justified merely by
  distinct item IDs. This clarification was added after the numeric run; it
  changes no frozen simulation input or reported number. It does **not** assume independent
  readers or simultaneous coverage across endpoints. Reader-level results remain
  visible; the average is not a population estimate over AI models.
- Every required component must pass its own prespecified directional bound:
  marked/careful accuracy at least 90%, unsafe action at most 5%, each nonclaim
  at most 5%, and explicit-fact controls at least 90%. A valid opposing component
  opposes; missing/invalid/held components prevent support. No successful subset
  becomes a bundle pass. Label each interval and decision marginal-not-simultaneous.
- A **128-world per-form bare-they descriptive block** uses an appropriate
  clarification/unknown gold whenever the record is insufficient. It is not a
  superiority carrier or a substitute comparator.

This auxiliary method is a concrete review request, not a claim that the SDK
currently computes or the API enforces it. Core CAD and token cost have official
filings; the remaining public, replayable observations and endpoint results
must be linked to the admission case, with their enforcement scope explicit.

An original plus a matching independent replica is **63,616 calls**, excluding
provider qualification. This is not a 160-call study or a booking for today's
quiet slot. The actual host's approved time/rate/budget must support it. No new
models are needed or requested. A cheaper study must reduce a claim prospectively
or demonstrate equally valid precision; it must not omit a promised endpoint.

Order: resolve author/control/protocol/design reviews; obtain exact independent
executor/replicator acceptances and resource quotes; freeze complete banks,
identities, settings, golds, sampling, seeds, estimator, bounds and stop rules;
mint before any relevant spend. Official and meaning-sensitive controls run
before targets. Strict cost benefit is tested on all 2,048 complete paired core
messages using the accepted exact tokenizer roster, with each form reported.
Failure stops later target spending. A failed CAD or absolute/safety requirement
stops optional later inference, retaining completed results and marking the
unrun requirements incomplete. Never choose another reader, enlarge N, change
the comparator or rerun after an unfavourable target result.
