## Impact/cause: define the two bits and the bare-fixed comparator

Proposal: [impact/cause](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-mxcehfr17mygjpsv).

Proposed gold decision:

> The primary two bits indicate which claims the message asserts, not the full
> physical state of the incident. An unasserted axis is unknown, not false. Shared
> context binds incident/check/time/cause/test references without giving away their
> outcomes. A separate named workflow policy defines any action-routing questions.

For review only: impact assertion alone → `(1,0)` assertion coverage, cause alone
→ `(0,1)`, both → `(1,1)`, neither → `(0,0)`. The zeroes do not claim ongoing impact
or an unresolved cause. These four public explanation witnesses are excluded from
future held-out evidence. Physical 2×2 world design and assertion coverage must be
retained as different variables; do not score missing evidence as false reality.

The second author decision cannot be assumed: the current prediction calls bare
“fixed” descriptive but also requires a 25-point gain against it. Choose explicitly:

- Keep a genuinely scored contrast and specify what information licenses one gold
  without hiding a preferred intention from an honest reader; or
- Make the ambiguous arm descriptive-only through a prospective authorized revision,
  with the current-version evidence consequences visible.

An honest “not established” must not lose points for failing to guess hidden intent.
The direct assertion audit and held-out operational consequence score are separate
diagnostics. Neither automatically replaces the promised other. Same-cell cost,
per-form NI, hard negatives and scope tests remain after these two choices.

## Choose-any: exact comparator text, not a paraphrase implied to be approved

Proposal: [choose-any/draw-uniform](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-ppyzdf5qk6z67aty).
Current content digest:
`aae00fac9dedd82954d24ceac1f210d5833a08bff5fe576a4e659dac49191268`.

The author's 144-world diagnostic decision and the already published balanced
form assignment are preserved. The mapping is a definition paragraph, not two
ready-to-instantiate English request strings. A per-form excerpt is not authorized
merely because it looks equivalent. No new launch-ready bank is advertised here.

For a prospective author-reviewed explicit template, proposed English requests are:

> Choose exactly one member of S. Every member is acceptable; the selection policy
> is otherwise unconstrained. A deterministic first-member rule, a cost- or
> latency-based rule, a weighted rule, or a uniform draw can satisfy this request.
> No randomness, equal probability, unpredictability, independence, rotation or
> fairness is promised.

> Make exactly one stochastic draw from S. Conditional on the frozen eligibility
> set and before the outcome is known, every distinct member identity has
> probability exactly 1/|S| of being returned. This promises neither cryptographic
> unpredictability, public verifiability, independence between repeated draws,
> sampling with or without replacement, nor a balanced finite run. A realized
> outcome alone neither proves nor refutes that distribution.

The proposed substitution rule is only `S` → the exact bound set reference; preserve
`1/|S|` with that same reference, not a substituted probability estimate. Shared
context in both arms must explicitly resolve finite nonempty distinct eligibility,
identity/deduplication rules and a frozen version before selection. It supplies no
additional policy requirement. These are **proposed new explicit templates**, not
verbatim excerpts of today's mapping or permission to file a result under it.
The author must either approve a protocol-permitted exact construction under the
present mapping or authorize a prospective mapping amendment with evidence impact
preview. Do not quietly treat semantic equivalence as literal compliance.

New analysis for the fixed assignment is in [STUDY-FEASIBILITY.md](STUDY-FEASIBILITY.md):
publish official arm-pooled and separately equal-reader contrasts, denominators,
form/domain/N slices and uncertainty. No reroll, after-results expansion, new-model
