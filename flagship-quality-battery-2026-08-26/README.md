# Flagship quality battery

Frozen: `2026-08-26`. This is an editorial qualification protocol, not an Ainglish measurement
and not a substitute for ratification.

The battery answers three different questions separately:

1. **Would an ordinary English speaker grasp why the distinction is useful?**
2. **Has the register accepted the construct?**
3. **Do reproducible measurements support the claims made for it?**

A construct may pass the first question while remaining a pipeline preview. A ratified construct
may be publishable as a semantic example while its empirical claims remain guarded.

## A. Lightweight editorial screen

All five checks must pass for a homepage flagship. They may be judged by a builder/editor and one
available native speaker; this is not a statistical human study.

- **A1 — five-second contrast:** after one before/after example, each pole can be paraphrased in
  one short sentence without project jargon.
- **A2 — familiar ambiguity:** the unmarked English form is something normal people plausibly
  write, and it genuinely leaves the registered distinction open.
- **A3 — symmetric forms:** the labels occupy the same syntactic slot, mark one semantic axis,
  and do not smuggle in unrelated promises.
- **A4 — visible payoff:** a single everyday example makes a different action or interpretation
  follow from choosing one pole rather than the other.
- **A5 — clean seam:** the construct is not merely a synonym for an existing entry; attachment and
  interaction with neighbouring constructs can be explained without special pleading.

Human validation is recorded as a bounded editorial observation only. It must never be described
as measured comprehension or representative preference.

## B. Governance gate

- The current revision, rather than a superseded slug, is ratified before it is labelled
  “ratified” or placed in the main ratified gallery.
- Seconds mean worth measuring, not adoption.
- No unresolved protocol warning, unclaimed blast-radius flip, or author-owned contract defect is
  hidden by the editorial score.

Pipeline previews may be shown in a separately labelled area if they pass A1–A5, but their exact
stage must be displayed.

## C. Evidence battery

These checks govern empirical wording, not whether the semantic distinction may be illustrated.

- **C1 — claim-aligned baseline:** bare-English recovery, careful-English expansion cost, and
  learnability are separate estimands; the proposal says which one carries its claim.
- **C2 — per-form visibility:** every pole has a separately served result; a strong pole cannot
  conceal a weak partner in a pooled scalar.
- **C3 — controlled use:** fresh positive, negative, missing-baseline, and attachment cases test
  whether the labels are used only when warranted.
- **C4 — reader validity:** comprehension or learnability uses a preregistered, independently
  qualified multi-lineage roster and preserves adverse/null results.
- **C5 — settlement:** at least one eligible independent, disjoint replication settles the claim
  carrier; same-author reruns do not count.
- **C6 — price is a price:** token results are reported by form and tokenizer and never used as a
  proxy for comprehension.
- **C7 — honest card:** publication copy links the live receipts, states the comparator, and does
  not turn scanner hits into popularity or adoption.

## Stop rules

- Do not recruit a large human panel merely to re-run the editorial screen.
- Do not run answer-bearing reader items until the reader roster and attempt-mint gates pass.
- Do not repeat an original merely to seek a nicer outcome; open an independent disjoint seat.
- Do not publish a pipeline preview as ratified, and do not call a ratified semantic example
  experimentally proven when its evidence contract is absent or unsettled.
