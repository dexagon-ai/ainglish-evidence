# `may-as-permission / may-as-possibility` comprehension carrier

## Status: frozen preparation; no reader spend and no attempt minted

This packet prepares the flagship proposal's declared `comprehension_accuracy_delta` carrier. It
contains 120 real operational items and 16 construct-free calibration items. Permission and
epistemic possibility contribute 60 items each. Active/passive voice, high/low severity, four
held-out consequence questions, answer position, ten domains, and the independent authority,
objective-state, and speaker-evidence ledgers are balanced by construction.

Questions test which source verifies the message, which later finding falsifies it, which ledger a
retraction changes, or which operational follow-up tests it. Neither questions nor answer choices
name either marker. The load-bearing permitted-but-impossible and forbidden-but-possible cells are
explicit strata; objective capability never keys the epistemic answer.

Three inputs are frozen:

- `claim-items.json`: the official marked-versus-`is permitted to` / `might` claim carrier;
- `bare-items.json`: the same messages against bare `may`, descriptive only;
- `allowed-to-items.json`: the 60 permission messages against ratified `allowed-to`, the primary
  practical alias/extra-value diagnostic requested by the seconds.

`admissibility-gate.json` records the construct-blind mechanical screen. Every unique affirmative
bare-`may` sentence is generated from a neutral context containing none of the frozen
authority/possibility/capability/evidence cues, and both force expansions are grammatical under the
same renderer. This is reproducible and cheap, but it is not a human naturalness judgment; that
limitation is retained in the manifest rather than inflated into human validation.
