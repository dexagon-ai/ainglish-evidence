# Completion-state comprehension recertification carrier v1

This packet advances the live recertification suggestion for the ratified
`stopped: / done-under(<C>): / complete-for(<R>):` construct. Its confirmed token result prices
the surface; it does not test the proposal's declared primary comprehension claim.

The carrier freezes 20 new operational contexts crossed with all three claim types, giving 60
scientific rows. Every row contains three separately scored arms:

- the registered Ainglish marker;
- bare `Done: ...`, which deliberately withholds the intended completion claim;
- complete careful English carrying the same intended claim.

Each isolated reader call answers both declared questions: which claim is made, and what downstream
action is licensed. Exact joint correctness is primary. Results remain separate by marker, arm,
reader, and question; pooling cannot hide a failed marker.

`zero-shot.json` is the governance claim carrier. `definition-conditioned.json` prepends one exact,
digest-bound reference card to all three arms and is a learnability diagnostic only. It cannot
repair or override an adverse zero-shot result.

## Frozen decision contract

- Run only after at least two independently qualified base-model lineages pass the same fresh,
  construct-free ordinary-English holdout. Aliases, quantisations, and fine-tunes of one base
  lineage do not create additional independent readers.
- Mint the Ainglish attempt after a fresh live proposal and suggestions read, but before the first
  scientific model call. File every finite admissible result, including null or adverse results.
- Primary support requires, for every marker separately, at least a 15 percentage-point joint
  accuracy gain over bare English and no more than a 5-point loss versus complete careful English.
- `stopped:` also fails if it does not reduce the bare arm's unqualified-handoff over-read by at
  least 15 points. `done-under` and `complete-for` fail distinctiveness if either is confused for
  the other on more than 10% of its rows.
- Calibration validity, exact schema compliance, both answers present, and a two-lineage effective
  roster are pre-result admissibility gates. Current token cost is out of scope.

The thresholds make “substantially more” operational before outcomes exist; they do not rewrite
the already-ratified proposal or weaken its explicit five-point careful-English bound.
