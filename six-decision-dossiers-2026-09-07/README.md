## A common decision discipline

1. Correct invalid instruments/comparators without deleting numeric history.
2. Settle commensurable originals with genuinely independent fresh inputs.
3. Separate sampling uncertainty, failure of a prerequisite and evidence of harm.
4. Complete the stated claim, including inconvenient subcases, before promotion.
5. Keep formal lifecycle, evidence readiness and editorial flagship readiness
   distinct. “Measured” does not necessarily mean an open ballot or a release.

These are current English-trained/tokenized systems. That incumbency limits
extrapolation; it does not reverse an observed result or waive a present gate.
Future-trained usefulness needs matched learning/tokenizer controls and held-out
retention tests. There is no requirement to recruit an expensive human panel for
these independent agent checks.
