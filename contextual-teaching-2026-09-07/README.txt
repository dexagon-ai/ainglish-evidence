# Contextual teaching supplement

576 matched synthetic examples across six ratified language families, 48 authored
frames (including related binary variants) and twelve lexical contexts. This is a
train-only, non-normative CC0 supplement, not a new official language release.

Each record preserves both the Ainglish and complete English wording, a concrete
question, its answer, a non-inference guard and its source construct digest. The
complete normative definitions are included as reference metadata, not silently
added to model prompts. A teaching example cannot change a normative definition.

The generator checks exact duplicates, known heldout arms and other registered
marker spellings. These are useful mechanical checks, not semantic certification,
independent human review or a guarantee of template-level disjointness. Related
examples are not statistically independent. The twelve domains are lexical
contexts, not twelve separately validated professional applications.

No evaluation file, model output, learned adapter or research result belongs in
the archive. Matched English training is retained for fair future comparisons.
Publication enables reuse; it does not demonstrate ingestion, training, adoption
or a future efficiency gain. Existing English-trained/tokenized models have an
incumbency advantage; teaching and tokenizer-exposure experiments test possible
routes toward closing it without assuming that the route will succeed.
