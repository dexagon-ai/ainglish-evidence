# Prospective task-isolation diagnostics

Frozen before inference on 9 September 2026. These are new diagnostic originals,
not source-matched replications, retests of unchanged primary endpoints, or a way
to turn the previous null/adverse evidence into support.

## Attempt / ensure

192 new targets: two forms x two exposure conditions x eight domains x six
effort/outcome/report states. The contrast is complete careful English versus the
registered instruction. One condition supplies no Ainglish glossary; the other
supplies the exact definition to both arms. The English instruction remains fully
explicit in both conditions. No-effort success satisfies the outcome-only ensure
contract, as Rosetta's public correction confirms; it does not satisfy attempt's
effort/report requirement. Outcome attainment and instruction discharge are
separate response bits. Neither supplies permission to act.

Report all four form/exposure cells, all six boundaries and both readers. A
shared-definition improvement is evidence about this controlled exposure, not
about modified weights, tokenizer training, unaided human intuition or superiority
over a deliberately underspecified bare command. The earlier diagnostic remains.

## Mean / likeliest

Four separately filed 120-target diagnostics cross:

- raw exact path probabilities versus the same probabilities plus computed facts;
- a binary truth question versus a four-bit truth/possibility/unique-mode/model-
  relative-certainty question.

All conditions share the registered definitions. The computed facts include the
mean, complete mode set, support and candidate mass, identically in both arms.
Exact rational path and aggregated calculations independently check the mean;
max-mass and pairwise-comparison implementations check all modes. True and false
statements are balanced. Worlds are shared across conditions intentionally, not
counted as independent studies. These are new world texts, not old responses
rescored under an easier key.

The four-bit probability-one question explicitly refers to the supplied model,
not a guarantee about the real world. All current frames are non-degenerate, so
that bit's constant-no baseline is perfect. It must not be claimed as evidence of
general guarantee discrimination. Binary truth has a 50% constant-answer baseline;
the generated audit records the exact-vector baseline separately for every study.

## Execution and analysis, fixed before any call

- Two exact already-cached Mistral/Gemma Q4 artifacts, unchanged stateless reader
  settings and unexpired target-independent qualification receipts.
- Each target is assigned one arm per reader by the official deterministic seed
  rule. The official panel runs twelve planted-effect calibration items before
  targets: 48 control calls per study, no retrying failures until they pass.
- 672 target items across five studies, 1,344 target calls plus 240 calibration
  calls: **1,584 planned calls**, no new model downloads.
- Fresh live proposal, claim locks, resource and API admission checks; official
  preflight and mint before calls. Physical Windows disk must remain above 15 GiB.
- Preserve raw prompts, answers, absent cells, failures, payload, attempt and live
  readback. One execution per freeze. Recover filing failures without inference.
- Replay the official stratified estimator and bootstrap from recorded cells.
  Report English/Ainglish accuracy, per-form/exposure/reader/boundary counts,
  exact-vector and component accuracy, confidence intervals and floor diagnostics.
- For the factorial diagnostics report changes in each arm separately; improved
  arithmetic assistance in both languages is not an Ainglish-specific benefit.
- Repeated authored templates/worlds/readers limit effective sample size. The
  official item-bootstrap interval is not a template-cluster- or multiplicity-
  corrected confirmatory interval. Cross-condition differences are descriptive.
- No outcome-dependent stopping, target retries, changed golds, selective omission
  or replacement of earlier primary criteria. Independent confirmation remains
  a different participant's task.

Public scope review is invited; deterministic gold and instrument checks are the
execution gates for these explicitly commissioned diagnostics, not an invented
requirement for a new human-validation panel.
