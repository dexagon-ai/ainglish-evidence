# What the new reader diagnostics resolved—and what they did not

9 September 2026. Five prospectively frozen originals, two previously qualified
cached model families, **1,584 retained calls** (1,344 scientific and 240 calibration).
No model downloads, retries, selective deletions, changed answer keys or
self-confirmations. `analyze_followups.py` replays every scientific raw answer
through the official decoder and exactly reproduces the filed estimator, strata,
bootstrap interval and attestation. Full journals and receipts are retained.

## Attempt/ensure: familiarity helps one condition, not enough for qualification

Four prespecified form/exposure strata have point differences of −18.71
(unintroduced attempt), +11.38 (defined attempt), −23.53 (unintroduced ensure)
and −9.74 (defined ensure). These contrasts are descriptive, not four independently
confirmed conclusions. Defined attempt reaches only 68.52% Ainglish exact accuracy
on the joint discharge/outcome task; defined ensure reaches 78.26%.

Readers nearly always recovered whether the outcome occurred: 185/189 English
and 194/195 Ainglish cells. The larger failure is judging whether the instruction
was discharged. This supports focusing further design review on effort/reporting
obligations versus an outcome condition—not changing the observed outcomes or
assuming a promise authorizes further action. The frozen outcome-only ensure
rule includes success without effort, following the already-published semantic
review. Older differently keyed data has not been rescored.

## Outcome: calculation burden and joint-question burden are distinct

Supplying verified calculations to both arms raises the binary task's Ainglish
aggregate accuracy from 53.30% to 91.97%. The mean-outcome stratum reaches 100%,
but likeliest-outcome reaches only 83.93%. The latter is not concealed by the
aggregate. The study is not a valid demonstration of the proposal's complete
noninferiority/per-form criteria, and the official unresolved flag still applies.

Supplying those same facts does not solve the four-part task: Ainglish joint
accuracy is 27.22%, versus 26.01% English. An aggregate difference near zero is not
success when both arms fail. The four-part response includes truth, possibility,
unique mode and model-relative probability one. Their separate counts are retained
in the analyses, not substituted for the frozen exact-match primary scoring.

All probability-one golds are “no” in these non-degenerate distributions. A
constant-no response achieves 100% on that component; the programme therefore
does **not** establish general guarantee discrimination. A later positive-control
design would be a new frozen study, not a reinterpretation of these results.

The four outcome studies share worlds intentionally and use different random
arm allocations. Do not pool them as independent samples or assign the full
between-study difference to a causal effect of supplied calculations. The pattern
motivates the next discriminating test; it does not prove a universal mechanism.

## Practical decisions

- Keep both current adverse originals and all inconclusive studies visible.
- Seek one capable independent reader evaluation or a bounded semantic challenge,
  not a favourable rerun. Review exact instrument access before assigning it.
- Do not market either proposal as flagship-ready from these results. Prefer
  task simplification and a clear author decision on the actual supported claim.
- Definitions and supplied calculations are not Ainglish in model training data
  or a tokenizer vocabulary. Future learning remains a separate hypothesis.
- Dependent postpone/replacement studies remain held for their declared cost and
  semantic-review gates; the spare GPU is not permission to bypass them.
