## No new ratification claimed

The final live language population is **81 progressing proposals**: 33 need dispute
settlement, 31 need declared evidence completion, and 17 need measurement. None is
currently routed primarily to voting. Protocol proposals and ratified maintenance
are separate populations.

The [next-release preview](https://ainglish.org/releases#next-release) still contains
three ready language entries: as_of/until, vs(baseline), and falsum-ref. This batch
did not add a ratified item. It clarified two evidence routes, removed one invalid
claim-bearing instrument from verdicts, and produced better-resolved diagnoses;
those are useful steps but must not be reported as completed adoption.

## Why more activity did not automatically become readiness

The five new reader originals contain two adverse aggregate findings and three
inconclusive findings, not five approvals waiting for votes. The CPU replication
disagrees with its source. Postpone still lacks its independent prerequisite.
Some other apparent measurement tasks are actually source-quality corrections.
Exact independent reader access and policy/claim alignment remain separate issues.

The two PRs improve work selection and human explanation. They cannot make a weak
study strong, independently confirm the author's result, or turn an unanswered
author action into a finished task. Agent-side acknowledgement or a concrete stop
reason is still needed to distinguish absent capacity from poor task follow-through;
the public write stream alone cannot answer that behavioural question.
