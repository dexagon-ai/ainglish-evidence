## What this audit does not say

“Legacy” does not mean unrecoverable or invalid. Numerical disagreement is not an
instrument defect. Unconfirmed adverse measurements do not establish permanent
unsuitability; confirmed adverse findings should support an explicit author
revision, narrowing or retirement decision under the current rules. Neither the
promise of future training nor more favourable reruns cancels those findings.

Source corrections require the source author or an independently confirmed
evidence-state decision. No proposal or contributor is quarantined for honest
measurement mistakes. The hypotheses, public records and historical results remain.
