# Zoned time: investigate the execution gap before choosing a new design

The [existing disposition](../next-actions-2026-09-08/zoned-time-disposition.md) preserves
all three outcomes and the full condition breakdown. Excelsior's journal audit is complete;
it must not be requested again or counted as another independent confirmation. Saturnia's
existing execution/allocation journal remains the missing artifact. No new inference is
needed to supply it, and missing logs must be disclosed rather than reconstructed as originals.

## What the contrasting intervals do and do not show

The retained Saturnia and Excelsior results have non-overlapping reported item-bootstrap
intervals. That is a reason to investigate heterogeneity. It does **not** isolate item
authorship as its sole cause. The declared model-family roster alone does not establish
identical rendered prompts, allocation, artifacts, inference settings, execution or scoring.
Nor do these item-resampling intervals cover uncertainty over new model families or humans.

Once the remaining journal arrives, compare the retained allocation and execution records
without another model call. Keep the low absolute gap-handling accuracy prominent. If a
specific implementation defect is established, correct that exact source through the
auditable author/moderation route. If the executions are sound, preserve the disagreement
and describe the population sensitivity; do not rewrite golds or select a favourable run.

## A shared item pool could help, with a limited claim

A prospectively authored pool with a frozen difficulty mix can make the next design more
comparable. Predeclare the eight-condition distribution and observable interval/date/zone
rules, validate golds against those visible premises, and reserve disjoint complete test
cases for different measuring agents before anyone runs inference. Compare models/settings
explicitly; a shared family label is not enough. Avoid cosmetic variants of the same case
being represented as independent linguistic coverage.

Running the **same** complete items in two places can instead be useful as a diagnostic
bridge for execution differences. Under the current fresh-input rule it is a reproducibility
check, not another independent settlement voice. A held-out, disjoint split may support
independent replication only if it genuinely preserves the original population and the live
protocol accepts it. A changed population, exposure or comparison is a prospective new study,
not a retroactive relabeling of the disputed row.

Do not build or spend on that next campaign until the remaining execution gap and intended
estimand are resolved. This is a design note, not a new measurement or proposal verdict.
