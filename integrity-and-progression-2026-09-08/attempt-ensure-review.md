## What the original instrument actually asks

The frozen attempt gloss is “Try to [action]; make a genuine adequate effort and report either
way.” The record says that adequate effort was made, the requested world outcome is false,
and an accurate failure report was sent. The question asks whether “the instruction is
fulfilled.” The filed language mapping explicitly says that an honest failure report satisfies
an attempt instruction; subsequent author discussion adds the genuine-effort boundary.

The frozen gold **is consistent with that intended contract**. Inspection has not established
a wrong answer key, missing recorded effort, missing failure report, or allocation/parser defect.
The preamble nevertheless calls the desired world state “the requested outcome,” and the gloss
does not explicitly say “this completes your obligation even when the outcome is false.” In
ordinary use, “fulfilled” can be heard as the outcome having been attained. That is a plausible
shared interpretation problem, not proof the comparison is invalid.

All 32 observed completion-probe cells were wrong: 0/12 in Ainglish and 0/20 in careful English.
The study's successful authority, actual-success and ordinary-success controls should not drown
out this central result. The independent reply requested from Theox must distinguish semantic
fairness from the mere fact that both current model families gave unwanted answers.

## Prospective follow-up options, not yet authorised experiments

If review identifies a real wording ambiguity, a **new** comparison could explicitly distinguish
two questions: “Were all obligations stated in the instruction met?” and “Did the requested
world outcome become true?” A more explicit careful-English contract could say “a genuine
adequate effort followed by an accurate report completes the obligation even when the outcome
is false.” Giving the same definition to tagged readers would be a separate visible-reference
condition, not another cold-reading study or evidence of training effects.

Freeze a new full item set and prospective scope before any such run; preserve the original
cold result and golds. If the existing instrument is judged fair, an eligible independent
fresh-input replication of that original remains the ordinary path. A shared failure does
not justify rerunning until it disappears. No follow-up reader calls are made in this audit.
