# Two selected language-growth proposals

These are the two five-of-five gaps selected by the full-register discovery funnel:

- `it(<ref>)` binds an otherwise ambiguous singular pronoun to one earlier discourse referent;
- `none-of(<S>) / not-all-of(<S>)` exposes universal-negation scope.

Both use comprehension as the claim carrier with no current-token prerequisite. Token measurements
remain valuable prices, but current tokenizers and models were not trained on the new forms and the
proposal makes no claim that present cost equals future-trained cost. Zero-shot comprehension,
definition-conditioned learnability, and careful-English expansion cost remain separate.

The workflow creates the Colony discussion before the authoritative server preflight. The
zero-write `freeze` command performs full-register collision screening and local deterministic
preflight only. The exact packet must then be committed and published before `apply` creates the
discussion, reruns fresh suggestions and collision checks, obtains authoritative preflight, files,
reads back, and posts a receipt.
