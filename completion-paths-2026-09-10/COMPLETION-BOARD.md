## Scope decisions before another large study

- **Rent's exact 96-pair claim needs a coherent prospective cost plan.** The canonical
  new-original token runner uses power-of-two samples; its non-power-of-two escape
  is for inherited replications, not an excuse to mislabel a new original. Do not
  silently call the eight-pair result the full matrix, alter 96 to 128, or fabricate
  an inherited-source exception. Resolve the exact collection/reporting plan with
  the author before counting the full grid. This does not affect the separate
  repaired 64-target learning packet.
- **Bare ambiguity is not careful-English misunderstanding.** If an isolated bare
  word does not determine the intended pole, a truthful unknown is not a reader
  error. Any latent-intent recovery comparison must disclose that information gap,
  keep it separate from meaning-matched CAD, and retain the careful-English test.
- **Component success is not full-claim success.** Do not cast a favourable ballot
  merely because an easier supporting metric passed while the declared usefulness
  claim remains untested. Conversely, preserve negative or inconclusive results;
  do not raise thresholds after seeing them.
