# Current cost, first reading and learning are different tests

The public [training page](https://ainglish.org/training) already explains the
English training-data head start, the purpose of the public-domain packs, and why
publication is not proof that a lab trained on them. It also separates learning
from changes to fixed tokenizer segmentation. This batch carries those distinctions
into the prospective study materials.

| Question | Comparison | What a result would not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current token cost | Complete Ainglish and declared meaning-matched English under exact present tokenizers | Comprehension, future tokenizers, or future workflow savings |
| First-reading comprehension | Fresh stateless readers, declared exposure, same facts and held-out consequence | Learning from an entry or weight training |
| Learning from the entry | Byte-identical marked target text; the official harness supplies the exact entry only to the entry-loaded arm | Updated model weights, tokenizer changes, human validation or independent proof from the paired study |
| Future weight/tokenizer uptake | A separate controlled prospective training/tokenizer intervention with sealed evaluation | Benefit merely because an archive is public or a new token was added to a vocabulary |

## Fair accounting after exposure

Report actual entry and message costs as well as accuracy, clarification and repair
behaviour. In a system that truly amortizes an entry once across N uses, a bookkeeping
model is `(entry_A - entry_E) + N * (message_A - message_E)`. It is not an observed
gain. A positive per-message premium does not disappear as N grows. Stateless panel
calls resend their entry, so their entry cost is repeated; do not claim a one-time
exposure saving for those requests. Provider caching or billing discounts are a
separate accounting question, not a changed tokenizer.

Future uptake may reduce explanation and correction overhead even where the present
surface costs more tokens. That is a falsifiable workflow hypothesis, not permission
to hide today's costs or to assume eventual gains. No additional model download or
training run is part of this batch.
