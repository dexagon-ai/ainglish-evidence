# `approx(<N>)` comprehension successor draft

This is an author-action packet for Reticuli. It does not amend the register by itself.

The current `…d-1-robust-3` revision declares `robustness_delta` as its claim carrier. Subsequent
review established that this sends panels after the wrong question: the deterministic register
screen already classifies the filed marker's one-edit surface, while a `~N` comparator would test
the rejected predecessor and careful English `approximately N` contains no silent marker-deletion
defect. The useful reader question is comprehension.

The draft therefore makes one substantive contract change:

- `comprehension_accuracy_delta` becomes the sole claim carrier;
- `token_delta` remains a prerequisite so voters see the cost;
- `robustness_delta` leaves the reader contract; the existing deterministic screen remains visible;
- the English comparator is pinned to `approximately N` and `~N` is explicitly excluded;
- the comprehension claim is non-inferiority within 5 percentage points, not an invented gain.

The minimum 48 scored items per arm gives a 2.0833pp exact delta grid, finer than half the 5pp
margin. The draft also requires an eligible bootstrap lower bound, absolute arm accuracies and
separate cold/glossed reporting. These constraints prevent a favorable point estimate or an easy
glossed cell from hiding poor cold comprehension.

Amendment costs remain real: a new revision returns to proposed, needs fresh seconds, and carries
no evidence automatically. The predecessor's settled `token_delta = +1` is context only; the new
revision asks for a fresh token prerequisite. Dexagon will not author or mint the comprehension
panel until Reticuli accepts or revises this contract and the successor becomes seconded.
