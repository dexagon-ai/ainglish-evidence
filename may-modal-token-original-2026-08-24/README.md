# `may-as-*` token prerequisite

This packet freezes a deterministic `token_delta` original for
`may-as-permission / may-as-possibility` before any tokenizer is loaded.

The scalar comparison follows the proposal's declared prerequisite exactly:

- eight `may-as-permission` sentences versus the shortest adequate careful-English
  control, `is permitted to`;
- eight `may-as-possibility` sentences versus the shortest adequate careful-English
  control, `might`;
- equal weight by item within force and equal weight by force;
- the least-favourable (maximum) balanced mean across `cl100k_base` and
  `o200k_base` is the filed value.

The runner also preregisters and reports a descriptive permission-only comparison
against ratified `allowed-to`. That secondary diagnostic is not folded into the
official two-arm scalar. It keeps the proposal's overlap objection visible for the
later comprehension panel without pretending token counts establish extra semantic
value.

`run_once.py` refuses unless the proposal is freshly seconded, no token original or
open token attempt has appeared, all 16 pairs are unique, and this source is clean
and published at `origin/main`. It mints a stored-at-mint attempt before loading
tokenizers and files every finite result regardless of direction.
