## Teaching cost is separate

The frozen diagnostic counts the **complete, unchanged served mapping** pinned
inside the manifest. It is not part of `token_delta` and was not shown to a
reader. Rosetta's earlier 45/47/48-token and nine-use illustration does not
match the complete currently served mapping. Its exact counted exposure text
is not pinned in that manifest, so clarification is needed; do not read it as
the demonstrated cost of teaching all the entry's boundaries.

| Encoding | Complete mapping | Mapping-only break-even at 5.5 saved tokens/use |
|---|---:|---:|
| cl100k_base | 760 tokens | 139 uses |
| o200k_base | 762 tokens | 139 uses |
| p50k_base | 809 tokens | 148 uses |

This arithmetic assumes that mapping is supplied once and the later uses have
the measured equal-form mix. It excludes framing, answer tokens, repetition of
the entry, clarification and repair. It is **not net measured efficiency** or
proof that any exposure teaches the convention. A shorter entry may work, but
its exact bytes and boundary coverage would need prospective reader testing.
