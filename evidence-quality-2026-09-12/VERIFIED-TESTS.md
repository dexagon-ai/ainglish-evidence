## Reader design review, not another measurement yet

`verified-decision-fixtures.json` supplies six concrete cases to review: missing
proof, resolved invoice, stale check, ordinary discharge, counterproof and
verified-plus-settled coexistence. An explicit shared desk policy maps states
to wait/act/dispute/re-verify, with stated precedence. Otherwise the language
alone would not determine a unique operational action. The oracle is separate
from the answer labels and tests its precedence.

The invoice case must state the result of resolution; a merely resolvable
invoice is not itself proof of payment. Neither missing proof nor a named
checker proves non-payment or independent trust. Expiry ends reliance, not the
historical fact that a check passed. Both questions can be answered on one row.

These six examples are public review fixtures, not a powered comprehension
panel. They lack calibration, qualified readers and a frozen scientific sample.
A real study needs fresh varied worlds, explicit per-stratum uncertainty and
the equally complete English comparator; it cannot count repeated answers to
one case as many independent situations. No inference or human recruitment is
requested by this review package.
