# Prospective simulation plan, frozen before execution

This is a known-truth stress test of three illustrative bootstrap estimators,
not a measurement of Ainglish, a new protocol, or evidence for adopting an entry.
The complete fixed design and seed live in `preservation_simulation.py`.
Twelve scenarios, 400 experiments each, 399 bootstrap draws per experiment.
All outcomes are retained, with Monte Carlo intervals and source digest.

Compare naive cell resampling, item-only resampling, and independent reader/item
resampling on paired binary outcomes. At the illustrative -2 percentage-point
boundary, report false preservation; at zero difference report power. Include a
near-ceiling small sample and crossed reader/item effects. The complete pairing
differs from the current counterbalanced CAD instrument. Two and eight readers
are small simulated populations, not recommended panel sizes or lineage claims.

The study asks whether these methods actually control error under these explicit
generators. It does not assume that a cluster-aware label makes an interval valid.
Repeated bootstrap draws add numerical precision, not more independent readers.
No method will be selected and retrospectively applied to rescue historical rows.

Background: [Owen and Eckles](https://arxiv.org/html/1106.2125) analyse crossed-factor
bootstrap variance, including the failure of naive cell resampling and conditions
under which product weights are conservative. That is not a theorem guaranteeing
small-sample percentile coverage in this binary simulation.
[Lakens](https://pure.tue.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/80918653/lakeequi2017.pdf) explains
why a prespecified equivalence bound asks a different question from failure to
detect a difference. Our one-sided preservation component is not the two-sided
equivalence procedure and is not a complete acceptance rule.

Independent confirmation, valid unique answers, faithful careful English, a
justified margin, an absolute comprehension floor, every critical form and any
separately promised practical benefit remain separate requirements. A future
training hypothesis is not a present measured benefit. The live protocol and
Reticuli's formal amendment are unchanged by this simulation.
