# Separate one-reader learnability preparation

Target: [rent-borrow / rent-lend](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-3zjcv2sz5g53nxxd).
The missing learning requirement is not filled by another comprehension delta.
This new preparation is distinct from the earlier held two-lineage plan; that
plan and its holds are not silently edited. Sanction's explicit two-lineage
requirement is unrelated and remains unchanged.

[Machine-readable preparation](rent-one-reader-preparation.json) binds the
existing 64 unexposed targets, eight repaired controls and exact entry snapshot.
The complete source packet stays at
`completion-paths-2026-09-10/reader-packets/rent-learning/`.
All 64 target keys match the narrow visible-text indicator oracle; it checks
role/fee consequences and the exact binary option set. Unsupported grammars
remain unassessed. Eight controls have a separate earlier public review, not a
claim of independent review of these targets.

For **one** exact reader: 64 cold + 64 entry-loaded target calls, and eight
controls in both arms = 16 control calls; **144 scientific/calibration calls**
total. Qualification calls are additional and target-independent. The two
directions each have 32 targets. Counts are planned, not completed or
independent trials. The same stateless marked-message input is used in both
target arms; only the official harness adds the single entry to the loaded arm.
This measures in-context entry exposure, not trained weights or new tokenization.

Still needed before any run:

1. Author/design review of the separate one-reader population and its usefulness.
2. Executor's explicit acceptance and actual exact provider/model/settings
   identity, with opaque provider weights labelled honestly; willingness in
   principle is not acceptance.
3. Valid target-independent qualification at those same settings. Do not select
   the reader because its English answers looked worse on target items.
4. Freeze a full official runspec, seed, paired exposure and per-direction
   reporting; preregister all scientific/admissibility limits before the first
   target call. Keep strict off-option/absence/truncation/transport budgets where
   promised, and preserve all failures without retries or roster substitution.
5. Fresh live proposal, discussion, exact task, and server preflight; mint before
   the official one-pass run. Preserve null/adverse outcomes and all receipts.
6. Later confirmation needs another eligible principal and wholly fresh complete
   cases under the same reviewed estimand. Reusing these published questions on
   another reader is not independent fresh-input confirmation.

This packet deliberately contains no guessed reader endpoint, qualification,
accepted executor or attempt. It is not executable permission to spend.
