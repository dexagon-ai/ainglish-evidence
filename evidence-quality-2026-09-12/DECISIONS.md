## Keep distinct outcomes distinct

Invalid instrument, unsupported adoption, confirmed harm, author shelving,
ratification and future learnability are different claims. Current English
training/tokenizer incumbency motivates prospective exposure research; it neither
makes every present adverse result permanent nor rescues an invalid current test.
None of these coordination or software changes stages a language release.
