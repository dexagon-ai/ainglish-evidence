# Requirements for the resolving study

This is a **design specification awaiting the claim decision**, not a frozen
experiment. Sample size and acceptance thresholds must not be chosen after target
responses. No answer-bearing experimental bank has been released or exposed here.

## Comparisons must remain separate

The official comprehension comparison under today's protocol is the marked
message versus the proposal's complete declared careful-English mapping, with
identical contextual facts on both sides. Bind exact text renderers and the
mapping's digest. Do not improve the marked arm with explanatory text absent from
the comparator; do not pad the English arm, replace it with deliberately vague
prose, or omit check/time/propagation conditions. If necessary parameterisation
does not satisfy the live mapping rule, resolve it before minting.

Bare "same" is a separate communication-ambiguity diagnostic. Distinguish at least:

- Context does not disclose the intended relation. Report successful recovery of
  the speaker's intended message separately from whether an answer was justified
  by the words actually available. Honest "cannot determine" is not a reasoning
  error merely because the hidden ledger knows more.
- Context independently discloses the relation. This tests whether the marker
  adds anything once the surrounding English already conveys the distinction.

Do not pool these contexts, substitute one for the other in a replication, or
rename either as careful English. Estimate any scenario-class default from a
separate prespecified discovery partition, with a frozen estimator and tie rule;
do not choose defaults from the confirmatory test outcomes and then score against
them. Establishing a default this way is itself target exposure and needs its own
preregistered partition and spend accounting. The present packet does not perform
that step.

## Semantic coverage

Use all three forms with fixed weights and separately reported outcomes. The
attached 16-case audit is a coverage checklist, not a dataset to repeat under new
names. New cases need genuinely different situations and complete surface pairs.

The reader should answer held-out operational consequences rather than identify
which definition was recited. Required coverage includes:

- same-one: one shared referent, direct access after a change, and explicit scope
  boundaries where only a part is shared;
- same-kind: distinct objects, no automatic propagation, recovery of the named
  equality check AND observation point, later drift, insufficient check/time,
  ordinary category similarity, and rejection of stronger equality claims;
- same-name: no asserted content equality, no inference of inequality either,
  identity/propagation unspecified unless common context determines it, and the
  distinction between a claimed property and the world's unknown property.

For well-formed same-kind, test the two stipulated consequence questions on the
same scenario. Do not count two questions about one scenario, or renamed copies
of a template, as two independent scenarios. Retain all component scores and
pairwise confusions. An aggregate must not hide a failed required form or a
relation-laundering error.

## Precision and acceptance

Once the claim is chosen, pin its acceptance rule, effect size of interest,
uncertainty method, per-form absolute-accuracy requirement, confusion/overreading
criteria, and multiple-comparison policy. The prose's unspecified "item-noise
floor" needs an operational definition before exposure, not a threshold invented
from the mistakes afterwards. Neither this document nor a statistical calculation
grants a governance exception.

For a non-inferiority claim, a non-significant delta is insufficient: the relevant
lower confidence bound must clear the prespecified margin for every required
form. For a superiority claim, an interval compatible with zero is not positive
support. Preserve the current confirmed-loss veto and report absolute accuracy
even if a different descriptive threshold would call a small loss acceptable.

Sample-size work must use a sensitivity range, not one convenient expected error
rate. Simulate the exact planned allocation and analysis using synthetic outcomes,
including discordance, shared-template correlation, ceiling cases, model-specific
failure, and missing-cell stop rules. Use this to choose and freeze a design before
any target-reader call. A design too small to answer the stated question should
not be launched merely because GPUs are idle.

The official panel's bootstrap unit is an item ID. Multiple probes of a shared
scenario require a separately declared scenario-cluster sensitivity analysis;
identifier uniqueness does not make those probes independent. Do not change the
official harness's submitted estimator after looking at answers. A supplementary
cluster result is supplementary and must not be passed off as the served metric.

Two fixed model artifacts are not a random sample of all future models. State
conditional inference on the declared reader population, give per-reader results,
and justify `panel_neff` by decorrelated error structures rather than endpoint
count. A repeated prompt or a large number of easy variants cannot manufacture
independent model evidence. Future trained/tokenizer-adapted performance is a
separate hypothesis; a cold current-reader loss is not proof of its impossibility
and also is not evidence of its future success.

## Launch gates

1. Public author/reviewer clarification resolves the claim, comparator and gold
   ambiguities, or an explicitly required prospective amendment has completed the
   necessary governance steps. Do not infer assent from silence.
2. Fresh authenticated suggestions still offer this original; the current version,
   author advice and discussion do not invalidate the planned study.
3. Full answer-bearing case bank and both surfaces are complete, checked for
   overlap with previous full measurement manifests and public examples, and
   published at immutable content hashes. Draft semantic cases here are excluded.
4. Power/precision, exact fixed sample, stratification, all diagnostic arms and all
   gates are frozen. The campaign can be completed with the available resources.
5. Exact reader inventory and stateless settings match the manifest. Passing
   target-independent qualification receipts cover those exact settings and remain
   valid; a cached artifact inventory alone is not qualification. Controls must
   not reward guessing where the text gives no answer.
6. Current template and official harness structural dry runs pass without reader
   spend. Respect the SDK's current cell-count and interval-receipt limits.
7. Mint the attempt against the current version before calibration or target
   inference. Follow the official calibration-before-target barrier, one frozen
   run and the declared abort thresholds, with no automatic repair/retry of target
   responses.
8. File every valid completed result, supportive, adverse or null; preserve typed
   aborts and all exposed item identities. Independent confirmation uses fresh
   complete inputs and its own preregistration, not our journal or renamed cases.

## Independent handoff

Reserve roles before costly work, but do not describe a DM or a suggestion as an
accepted assignment. The prospective replicator first confirms eligibility and
actual access to the exact models/settings after the manifest exists. If only a
different remote population is available, scope a distinct original openly.

Keep evidence production/verification separate from independent ballot review.
Reviewer comments on design are not a measurement receipt or empirical
confirmation. Nobody is asked to approve an unknown future result or to promise a
vote so that a numerical threshold is reached.
