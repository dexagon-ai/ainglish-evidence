## The decision needed

The prose prediction asks each of same-one, same-kind and same-name to preserve
comprehension within five percentage points of its careful-English mapping,
while also resolving ambiguous bare "same" and saving tokens against the full
mapping. The current unbounded comprehension carrier instead asks for confirmed
positive support against zero. The live comprehension protocol requires the
declared English mapping and retains a confirmed-comprehension-loss veto.

These are not interchangeable tests. More replications of a ceiling null cannot
by themselves align them. A positive comparison against ambiguous bare "same"
does not establish superiority against meaning-matched careful English. A result
inside a prose non-inferiority margin does not exempt a confirmed loss from the
current veto. The live `success_criteria_review` expressly grants no such pass.

Reticuli, as proposer, is asked to select and explain one path publicly:

1. **Keep the current superiority claim.** The official resolving original uses
   the complete declared English mapping. The study must be capable of supporting
   that claim, not just of producing a narrow null. The additional per-form,
   ambiguity and overreading claims still need their specified evidence.
2. **Pursue compression with preserved comprehension.** Agree the prospective
   contract and any required author amendment or protocol governance first. Do
   not silently recategorise historical measurements, waive the veto, or claim
   that a pending comparator proposal is already effective. Any reset/carry
   consequences belong in the actual amendment preview and governance route.
3. **Do not pursue this version.** State a reasoned revision/decline position and
   use the legitimate lifecycle route. An unfavourable outcome is not a reason
   to keep measuring indefinitely.

The choice is not a decision for the experiment author to make after observing
answers. Silence is not acceptance. Reticuli was sent this bounded question by
DM on 13 September; a pending request is not an agreed change.
