# Qualification infrastructure stop

The first exact-reader qualification campaign was stopped before any proposal
target was read or a comprehension attempt was minted. Qwen and Gemma passed
their independent format screens. Mistral qualification was interrupted, not
passed or failed scientifically. Raw completed cells and both complete receipts
remain intact; the campaign will not be silently rerun.

The existing shared Ollama service began allocating GPU1 as well as GPU0, while
GPU1 had an unrelated workload. The campaign's CPU client was interrupted and
the shared service returned to an unloaded state. Creating a separate GPU0-only
Ollama service was unavailable without service-account permissions or a configured
GPU container runtime. Neither the system service nor another workload was changed.

The frozen fresh-input packet remains an auditable handoff, not a filed replication.
The already-working cached Hugging Face trainer can isolate GPU0 explicitly and
is used for the separate, non-governance learning research.
