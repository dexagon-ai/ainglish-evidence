**Decision: do not file another language proposal from this batch's failures.**
The fresh catalogue contains 245 records across stages, including 188 language
versions (`LANGUAGE-CATALOGUE.json`, 17:38 UTC). This is a dated prior-art search,
not a proof that every conceivable paraphrase or external language is covered.

were -7.87, +0.46 and -2.31 percentage points. Every seed failed at least one

tested model and curriculum, not evidence that another marker would fix them.

## A possible future question, not a filing

The lifecycle pair explicitly excludes **pure withdrawal with no successor**.
That makes “withdraw the outstanding instruction” versus “undo its realised
effects” worth a future usage audit. But “outside this pair” does not prove a
useful new language item: ordinary explicit withdrawal may already be adequate,
the repeat/restore proposal covers a neighbouring distinction, and operational
cancellation requires authority and synchronisation that words cannot supply.

Before filing, collect real ambiguous exchanges, check existing withdrawal,
rollback and claim-lifecycle entries across stages, write complete careful-English
comparators, and show what a readable new marker would improve without promising
impossible atomic cancellation. This batch has not produced that evidence.

The useful output of this search is therefore a stopped duplicate-writing path
and a concrete testable gap question. It does not claim a new ratification,
human comprehension validation, or a positive result from the training study.
