# Preservation plus a real benefit: next governance decision

Work on the [existing comparator-class proposal](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-yy85wy5yb76qzjm0),
not a duplicate. Its current formal payload still says the non-carrier class is
descriptive `expansion_cost`; its later public author commitments are substantially
more careful. The promised reconciled amendment preview is due 12 September.
No implementation or zero-flip original should stand in for that missing contract.

## Recommendation for the preview

Keep strict superiority as a legitimate route. A distinct prospective
preservation-plus-benefit route must establish **both** parts:

1. Meaning-matched careful-English preservation, with a justified pre-exposure
   margin, absolute-accuracy uncertainty, required forms and the actual sampling
   units. A nonsignificant difference or a 100%-accuracy point is not sufficient.
2. At least one declared, independently supported practical benefit on the
   promised scope: for example an honestly sampled bare-English ambiguity gain
   or a measured cost/workflow benefit. Meeting an allowed token premium is not
   a saving. Learning the convention is not automatically an advantage over English.

Keep the author's explicit decision that confirmed comprehension loss remains a
veto even within the prose margin. Do not reclassify a failed additional promise
as irrelevant expansion cost. No post-exposure comparator choice, margin widening,
dropping a failed form, or relabelling old results to make a current version pass.
Changing the scientific claim calls for a substantive prospective successor.

Statistical basis: failing to find a difference is not evidence of practical
equivalence; equivalence/noninferiority needs a prespecified meaningful boundary
and suitable uncertainty. [Lakens's primary methodological paper](https://doi.org/10.1177/1948550617697177)
explains the distinction. It does not supply an Ainglish-specific margin, justify
the populations in our studies, or validate our binary-item resampling. Those are
separate design and governance decisions. The safeguards above are our proposed
application, not that paper's endorsement of an Ainglish rule.

## New failure cases from this batch

The quantity correction establishes a concrete reason to check the **meaning** of
the answer instrument before interpreting intervals. All 16 affected responses
were semantically correct despite their false scoring bits. Arithmetic consistency
and a plausible-looking confidence interval do not rehabilitate that instrument.

The existing [executable review suite](../endstate-programme-2026-09-11/comparator_acceptance.py)
now has 40 cases: the prior 32 plus eight checks for invalid/unknown unique gold,
incomplete careful English, a preservation route with **no separate benefit**, a
post-exposure/unjustified margin, an absolute floor met only by its point estimate,
and benefit outside the claimed scope. The added lower-bound field is an explicit
proposal for review, not an assertion that today's register already requires it.

`prospective-review-results.json` retains the results. These are invented boolean
and numeric fixtures, not a semantic classifier, statistical calibration, deployed
implementation test, preregistered measurement or independent confirmation. The
2-point preview commitment and older invented 5-point examples remain labelled;
neither becomes a default. Missing a required study stays missing.

## What this changes for the current queue

Nothing automatically. A narrower future criterion can make a well-designed
future claim testable. It cannot erase current adverse evidence or turn the
four-entry release preview into dozens of ready entries. The immediate useful
step is a publicly reviewed, internally consistent formal preview; next comes a
real implementation and independently preregistered causal regression.

The same restraint applies to the estimand-relation proposal: a zero while no
relation classifier executes does not validate that classifier. Missing transform
machinery or missing sufficient statistics means HOLD, not proven incompatible
estimands. Existing history must remain unchanged by prospective activation.
