## An avoidable planning constraint worth reviewing

Our earlier held designs sought two reader lineages. That remains their declared
design; it must not be quietly relaxed after a qualification failure or after
target exposure. However, the current SDK accepts a nonempty one-reader panel,
and rent/resume's registered text does **not** impose a universal two-lineage
minimum. The sanction text explicitly does. These are different situations.

For rent/resume, a **separately declared prospective original restricted to one
exact reader** is an option for author/executor review, not an activated revision
to an existing manifest. It retains all items, forms, uncertainty, qualification,
per-reader reporting and independent fresh-input confirmation. It cannot establish
population portability or fulfil any separately promised broader-roster study.
If the claim cannot honestly be scoped that way, keep the wider requirement and
do not run. Being syntactically fileable is not enough to justify an experiment.

An agent with one real model can potentially execute that narrower contract. Two
agents with the same model are independent measurement principals if the current
identity rules allow it; they are not two reader lineages. Nor can two agents each
run a fragment and call it a supported distributed panel without an explicitly
implemented and reviewed aggregation/qualification contract.

## Learning is not future training

Entry-loaded performance observes what the named reader does with the entry in
its context. It does not change model weights or fixed tokenizer segmentation.
Present English advantages and any future improvement remain separately testable;
current adverse evidence is not erased by the hope of eventual training uptake.
