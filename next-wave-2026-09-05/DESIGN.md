# Mean/median and check-result campaign — prospective design

These studies follow newly independently confirmed, source-audited token prerequisites.
They are new comprehension originals, not replications of the narrow older panels, not human
validation and not forecasts of future trained-model performance. Every finite admitted outcome
is retained and filed. Reader or transport failures trigger the declared abort, not a rerun.

## Separate questions

- **mean.careful:** 256 fresh form-balanced reporting cases, 128 matched base frames. Primary
  outcome is joint recovery of calculation and exact finite population, against complete careful
  English. Eight signed-quantity domains, eight finite-data families and two windows are crossed.
  All population bytes and computed means/medians are frozen before reader calls. Report each
  form, domain, family, population-binding errors and statistic-confusion errors separately.
- **mean.practical:** the same matched primary frames against the conventional short alternatives
  `arithmetic mean of P`, `median of P`, `mean(P)` and `median(P)`, with common resolved population
  context. This is a separate comparison, never pooled with the complete-English primary.
- **mean.hard:** separately scored validity and consequence diagnostics: extreme observations,
  even medians not observed, exclusions, sample scope, unsupported weighting/approximations,
  categorical modes, uncertainty and causation. The absolute arm results matter; a null difference
  between two poor arms is not evidence that the mapping is understood.
- **verdict.careful:** 256 balanced scheduled-check reports against complete careful English.
  A common retained execution log pins completion/finding state independently of the outcome
  wording. Questions ask whether the current check adds evidence of a broken target, whether
  retrying the check is needed to obtain a first result, or which object the failure concerns.
- **verdict.bare:** the same frames and consequences with ordinary `failed` as the comparison.
  The common log stays in both arms. If it lets ordinary English recover the outcome just as
  well, that is the declared hypothesis being tested, not a reason to remove the helpful log.

The mean proposal's bare-`average` prediction is **not silently converted into a hidden-intent
accuracy score**. In equal-mean/median cases, a bare report need not identify the intended
statistic. Its ambiguity is recorded as a design limitation; these studies cannot establish the
separate claimed 20-point advantage over bare average. Summary/translation/corruption/adoption
requirements also remain separate. No partial panel is labelled the complete normative plan.

## Readers, scoring and stopping

Use the two existing local Q4 reader lineages with exact artifact/settings-bound, unexpired
target-independent qualification receipts. No target-informed requalification, retries, hidden
conversation, model download or change of reader roster. Eight fresh target-independent planted
controls per comparison precede real cells; each reader must independently clear the declared
gap. Every form has separately balanced opaque option positions. Legal wrong answers count as
wrong, not as a technical failure. Off-option, absent, truncated and transport-fault budgets are
all zero, including controls and drained started cells; all journals survive an abort.

The headline is the official SDK Ainglish-minus-English accuracy difference in percentage points,
with equal form weighting. Primary non-inferiority margin is −5 points **for each form**; a
pooled point above −5 does not establish it. Retain SDK intervals and add report-only paired
base-frame cluster intervals (2,000 draws, seed 2026090567), preserving both forms and readers.
These conditional intervals describe the fixed readers and authored frame population, not all
people or all future models. Do not alter the filed estimator to obtain a preferred decision.

The hard diagnostics have 27 yes and 133 no gold responses. Opaque option positions are
balanced, but semantic answer classes are deliberately not: aggregate accuracy must be read
with per-probe results and the always-no baseline (83.125%). The categorical, weighted and
approximate probes explicitly describe adversarial worlds that do not license the report;
their common context overrides the valid-data example, rather than asserting a valid centre.

Only freeze/run a comparison after the complete item/gold/analysis audit passes, immutable input
bytes are published, and fresh exact-ID work selection and preflight still permit it. A later
independent participant must design fresh cases; copied labels or record IDs are not a scientific
replication. The control/reference overhead, actual sample sizes and all aborted conditions are
reported explicitly.
