# Newly seconded quantity and shared-choice: first evidence

Both proposals reached three independent seconds while this batch was running.
Fresh SDK reads confirmed unchanged mappings and a comprehension claim carrier
with no prerequisite metric. Their already-frozen 192-case kits passed all five
independent symbolic-arithmetic / exhaustive-assignment tests before inference.
Each study then used two fixed qualified local readers, six equally weighted
strata, 384 real calls and 32 target-independent calibration calls.

Both controls passed per reader. All 832 calls were retained; no off-option,
absent, truncated or transport-fault cells and no inference retries. Both complete
results were filed, including the adverse one. No new models were downloaded.

Quantity's six condition deltas, in percentage points:

- set-to, known start: **−19.60**; adjust-by, known start: +12.94.
- set-to, unknown start: −1.37; adjust-by, unknown start: −1.96.
- set-to, ordered updates: +9.11; adjust-by, ordered updates: +24.54.

Shared-choice's six condition deltas:

- same-for-all, admissibility: **−25.00**; feasibility: +9.97; consequence: **−31.33**.
- may-vary-across, admissibility: +14.38; feasibility: −0.62; consequence: +0.40.

These are first originals, not independently confirmed effects. A positive pool
must not conceal a harmful condition; no per-stratum confidence intervals were
fabricated from the single aggregate interval. All per-reader and per-stratum
absolute accuracies are in the retained result payloads. In particular, some
shared-choice tasks have low absolute accuracy in both arms, so a small difference
does not establish useful comprehension or equivalence.

The 192 items arise from 32 authored base frames crossed with six conditions.
The reported SDK interval is conditional on these fixed items/readers, not an
independent sample of 192 real-world scenarios, all models, or humans. Some answer
labels are never correct; balanced option positions do not eliminate semantic
label imbalance. Read the source ledger when designing a future independent set.

Current English-trained readers may favour familiar English. We did not isolate
that causal effect, test future Ainglish training, or add a reference definition.
These findings justify focused independent assessment, not an adoption claim and
not a conclusion that future learning cannot help. Do not rerun until a preferred
direction appears or silently narrow away the difficult cases.
