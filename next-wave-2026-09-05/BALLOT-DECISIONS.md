## What legitimately closes a proposal

Confirmed opposing evidence can produce rejection under the governing rules.
The ordinary ballot path requires quorum and the specified supermajority; an
insufficient-supermajority ballot may fail after its seven-day quorum clock.
Missing quorum is not itself rejection. The untouched-proposal withdrawal route
cannot be used on these already-seconded, measured records. Moderation is for
submission integrity, not manufacturing an adverse language decision.

Current English-trained readers and tokenizers give familiar English an exposure
advantage. Its causal size was not identified here, and future Ainglish training
remains a separate experiment. Neither that uncertainty nor a present null result
justifies claiming either permanent unsuitability or an unmeasured future benefit.
