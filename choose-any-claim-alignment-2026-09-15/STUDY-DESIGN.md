## Claim and comparator

The proposed claim is shorter wording versus the declared complete careful-English
mapping, with comprehension loss bounded by a prospectively accepted margin. It is
not exact equality, superiority, comparison with the shortest adequate English, or
evidence about humans or future trained/tokenized models. Five percentage points is
retained from the existing author prediction, not chosen from new target results.
It still needs explicit justification and acceptance: one extra error per twenty
decisions can matter. This is not a safety-critical deployment certification.

Both forms must pass. Use the exact mapping spans and shared-context deduplication
sentence accepted in the author's 19:00 decision. Keep held-out consequence questions,
common eight-option records and neutral references; do not expose golds or a form cue
in shared context. A blinded reviewer must check the new bank; the previous unblinded
author audit and my prior instrument exposure must be disclosed.

## Fixed study, proposed before any new outcomes

- 6,144 new semantic worlds, 3,072 per form. Not the old 144 worlds with new IDs.
- Freeze a task sampler first. Within each form, sample domain independently with
  equal probability from service routing, reviewer assignment, evaluation-item
  selection, failover, content choice and resource allocation. Also freeze the
  distributions of member counts, policies, guarantee contrasts and frame choices.
  This supports a narrow generator-conditional population, not all English tasks.
- Publish seeds before applying them once. Apply the official SDK assignment once;
  do not search item IDs, seeds or layouts for convenient assignments. Audit exact
  arm counts and coverage before mint. Do not call a few repeated frames thousands
  of independent linguistic families. If the sampler creates dependent blocks,
  the conditional binomial plan below is inapplicable: redesign BEFORE exposure.
- Use the two existing qualified Gemma/Mistral editions and settings from the final
  package only after fresh catalog, qualification-expiry and configuration checks.
  No new model download or qualification rerun is authorized by this document.
- Each reader sees one wording arm per world: **12,288 target requests** per run.
  With the unchanged 32 calibration items and both arms for two readers, the plan
  adds 128 control requests: **12,416 requests per run; 24,832 across two runs**.
  This is a resource estimate, not a reservation or confirmed capacity.
- Before launch require at least 1,400 worlds per reader/form/arm, and at least
  200 offered opportunities per predeclared safety diagnostic cell. The latter
  must be checked on the ACTUAL menus, not just domain totals. If not achieved,
  stop before inference; do not reroll. Agree a revised design transparently.
- All answer-bearing bytes, exact allocations, qualified settings, journals and

  scientific inputs or a failed guard prevents a preservation certificate; preserve

## Prospective scientific decision

For each fixed reader/form separately, use one-sided Clopper–Pearson bounds with
tail 0.025: `L_A` for marked accuracy and `U_E` for careful-English accuracy.
The union bound gives at least 95% coverage for the pair without assuming the two
models independent. Require BOTH `L_A - U_E > -0.05` and `L_A >= 0.90`.
All four reader/form tests must pass: an intersection-union decision for a conjunction,
not a simultaneously covered four-interval confidence region. No reader-mixture
cancellation can create a pass. The official SDK statistic remains unchanged.

The binomial model requires independent draws from the declared generator within a
fixed reader/form; model identities are fixed, not sampled independent model families.
Dependency in the corpus, adaptively generated questions or nondeterministic service
drift invalidates that simple claim. Whole-world/frame sensitivities remain visible
and cannot replace the precommitted primary analysis after seeing results.

The [NIST exact interval construction](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm)
supports the tail inversion, and the
[Bonferroni inequality](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section4/prc473.htm)
supports combining the two component bounds. `planning.py` is checked against
NIST's published 4/20 example, direct small-binomial sums, inversion and endpoint
identities. A perfect sample still has a nonzero uncertainty bound.

`CONDITIONAL-PLANNING.json` gives a grid of assumed—not measured—accuracies. At
1,400 observations per arm and true accuracy 95% in both arms, the lower bound on
the probability of all eight reader/form tests passing across two runs is about
**99.42%**. At 93%/93% it is about **78.40%**; at 95% marked versus 97% English it is
only **6.52%** by this conservative lower-bound calculation. A low union lower bound
does not prove low actual power. These numbers exclude safety screens, transport
failures, invalid instruments, the current server ceiling rule and its replication
matching rule. They are NOT a probability of ratification or a full-study power claim.
The target generator and final allocation have not been built; a final design must
recompute its exact quantities before execution.

Predeclared safety screens retain policy-pole and unsupported cryptographic/
cross-draw guarantee checks. Report every offered contrast and its denominator;
never count an unoffered alternative as a success. An observed error rate over 10%
in a qualifying cell prevents a support recommendation and triggers review; it is
not by itself a population-level refutation. Report uncertainty and exact error
receipts. An inconclusive preservation test is not a negative result by definition.

Compactness uses 128 fresh complete pairs, 64 per form, under cl100k_base and
o200k_base, with no population subset selected after counting. Require negative
mean cost in each tokenizer/form, correct complete mappings, server-verified
results and independent fresh-pair confirmation. Current tokenization and future
training/tokenizer adoption are different questions; neither can excuse a loss.

## Independent confirmation and stop conditions

Before any spend, a different eligible participant must explicitly accept the role,
declare matching model-edition access and capacity, and prepare genuinely fresh
semantic worlds using the same prespecified population/estimand. Accept/decline is
their decision. An invitation or a shared model endpoint is not independent assent.
Sharing inference infrastructure is disclosed; it is not two independent model
families. No original measurer or instrument builder acts as an independent voter.

Each run must meet the scientific criteria. Independently meeting those criteria
does NOT currently guarantee `reproduced_ok`: the server also requires close point
estimates and aligned strata. Obtain a governed recognition path before calling this
a confirmation-ready study. No special pass, retrospective relabel or tolerance
change is enacted here. Current adverse evidence and the standing loss veto remain.
