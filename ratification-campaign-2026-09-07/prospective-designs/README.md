# Full-claim follow-through: designs held at their real dependencies

These are **prospective design notes and tested deterministic oracle prototypes**,
not frozen reader instruments, completed comprehension studies, or confirmation.
No model calls are authorised by running the tests. Never feed these public tests
to a reader as supposedly unseen scientific items.

## Windows: per-clock / per-any

[Proposal](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-vq5925e9710c574a).
Cost original `c62dbdaa...` is +0.75 against at_most +1, awaiting independent
confirmation when filed. Preserve separate clock and sliding populations and
both bare and full-careful contrasts; do not pool bare English into the
careful-English non-inferiority estimate.

Use two-burst logs with an independently available reset/enforcement anchor.
Cross whether bursts straddle a clock boundary, whether their total exceeds the
cap, and whether the span actually fits the declared sliding duration. Include
day boundaries with named zones, DST-short/long days, the exact duration endpoint,
and no-breach controls. Keep the held-out question about whether the second burst
breaks the limit. A clock-versus-sliding tag must not be needed when the shared
anchor already supplies everything: that is the proposal's bare >=90% falsifier,
not a cell to discard.

Report both forms, absolute accuracy, bare contrasts, careful contrasts and the
5-point non-inferiority uncertainty separately. Do not claim a ceiling zero is
supportive. The cost set's four time-unit spellings are not empirical coverage
of every timezone/month representation.

## Stock versus flow: remain-in / departed-from

[Proposal](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-xffrm7wz2wt3xhzf).
Cost original `88324788...` is +2 against at_most +4, awaiting confirmation.
The comprehension population must be **separate** from that cost set, as the
proposal explicitly requires. Plan at least 96 fresh scenarios, 12 in each of
staffing, rooms, evacuation, queues, inventory, replicas, subscriptions and fleets.

Generate and independently inspect actual identity-keyed transition histories,
not just an asserted final count. Vary initial stock, arrivals, repeat departures,
returns and events exactly on interval endpoints. The oracle counts distinct
people/objects departing, not departure events; a person may contribute to the
flow and also be inside at cutoff. Apply simultaneous changes in an explicitly
declared sequence, or label an unresolved sequence rather than inventing it.

Ask an operational mode-and-count consequence, not a request to repeat the tag.
Keep both readings plausible in paired contexts. Preserve the >=30-point bare
gain, >=90% absolute recovery, 5-point careful margin and <=5% other-mode error
as separate declared claims. Hyphen loss, missing distinctness and missing temporal
arguments are an additional robustness instrument, not silently part of the
comprehension scalar.

## Incident impact versus cause

[Proposal](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-mxcehfr17mygjpsv).
Cost original `d8ac746b...` is -2 against at_most +2. It covers **individual**
marker claims on the 256 frozen semantic worlds, not a separately settled
conjunction-cost population. Independent confirmation and an adequate conjunction
cost check remain before claiming the complete prerequisite is empirically covered.

Build the eventual at-least-160-vignette study from those frozen semantic cells,
with resolved incident/check/time/cause/test references and both world axes live.
The cells are design metadata: labels such as “narrow probe” are not already
complete vignettes. Cover the four actual world states, each individual marker,
the conjunction, and the separate bare-fixed diagnostic. Do not select only
worlds where a positive claim is true or only favourable hard cases.

Most importantly, **absence of an asserted cause claim is not a claim that the
cause remains present**. Separate message-commitment gold from world-truth and
operational-routing gold. A recovery assertion pins one check at one time;
it is not a current, permanent or whole-system guarantee. A cause assertion
requires its named post-change test but does not establish backlog drainage or
the absence of another cause. Missing references require clarification.

Preserve per-form/conjunction, 2x2 and domain reporting, the 5-point careful
margin, >=25-point bare gain and <=5% cross-axis errors. Robustness must separately
test omitted/unresolved references, narrow probes, irrelevant tests, stale times,
hyphen loss and near-miss spellings. Future training remains a different study.

## Before any of these reader studies starts

1. Refresh the exact live proposal, evidence plan, source confirmations and actor eligibility.
2. Finish complete claims/questions/options/gold and independent deterministic checks;
   review answer leakage, comparator symmetry and meaningful competing readings.
3. Freeze every cell, population/stratum weighting, separate contrast, exclusions,
   metric/scorer, finite-result rule, reader artifact/settings and preregistered gates.
4. Qualify the exact readers on target-independent controls; use task-specific
   calibration capable of detecting meaning recovery, not answer copying.
5. Preflight and mint before target exposure. Preserve an adverse, null, censored
   or aborted outcome. Submit no result as a successful full prediction merely
   because the API accepted a payload.

Run `python -m unittest discover -s . -p 'test_oracles.py'` here for the model-free
oracle checks. They cover edge cases, not validity of an unbuilt language instrument.
