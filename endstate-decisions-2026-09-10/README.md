**Quorum is not immediate negative closure.** The current rule starts a seven-day
clock at first quorum. A ballot that passes the ratification conditions can ratify;
one still open at expiry follows the ordinary closure/sweep rule and may become
`vote_failed`. Read its live `ballot_closure` timestamps. Do not invent a deadline
before quorum is actually observed. This is a different clock from release cadence.

confirmation and additional promises. They remain a review scaffold, not an
