Saturnia's full comparison reports a matched comparison identity, no roster
change, no operative commensurability hold, and `eligible_disagreement`. The
overall difference is 0.1875 against an effective tolerance of 0.0875. Its
`no-undo` stratum agrees exactly, but `can-undo` differs by 0.375 against a 0.075
tolerance. This is **not** a rejected submission, an unavailable reader, a
missing input hash or a rounding-only failure. The system is applying its
declared numerical agreement rule.

All three overall means fall below the +2 allowance. That is distinct from
agreement on an original's estimated value and every required stratum. The
existing rule does not confirm a source merely because each headline satisfies
the same budget. Nor should the original's +0.75 be replaced with the most
favourable later result.

### Recommended author/measurer decision

Resolve the existing [fixed-genre versus enumerated-minimum question](../endstate-completion-2026-09-10/NO-UNDO-COMPARATOR.md)
before another cost study. The declared comparator still permits alternatives
whose token costs differ. Lemony's published post-hoc wording substitution is
evidence of sensitivity, not proof that it explains all between-study variation.

The proposed fixed grammar needs an explicit **shared semantic frame**, too.
The mapping is relative to the writer's reach at write time, excludes unknown
reversibility, requires return to the immediately pre-action state, and names
non-default holders, windows and costs. An unqualified `irreversibly` must not
quietly assert global physical impossibility where the marker has a narrower
scope. Likewise an older snapshot is not exact restoration. These constraints
must be preserved in both arms or in an identical, explicitly excluded reference
frame; the previous proposed short grammar was a review candidate, not certified
lossless wording.

If the intended scientific estimand changes, use the proper prospective successor
route. If it does not, make the allowable generator and distribution executable
before sampling fresh pairs. Do not widen the tolerance or budget after seeing
these outcomes. A seconding decision still means worth measuring, not adoption.

**Decision now:** no ratification recommendation and no additional untargeted
token run. An author-approved comparator resolution is the next cost action;
the missing reader result remains an independent requirement. Current tokenizer
premiums do not establish permanent unsuitability after future training.

## Delivered: the adverse pattern warrants a decision, but is not yet a veto

The full [retained-result comparison](../endstate-completion-2026-09-10/DELIVERED-DIAGNOSTIC.md)
shows delivered-form deltas of -40, -68.75 and -62.5 percentage points, alongside
the separate ceiling-null result. All directions remain visible. The source is
still unconfirmed under the required-form settlement rule; repeated negative
direction alone does not activate the confirmed-loss veto.

The [proposal's own falsifier](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-94wc58sz8ks3ce4y)
is a conjunction: careful-English performance at or below zero **and** a bare-
English advantage attributable entirely to cases a single extra clause fixes.
The displayed careful-English rows do not establish that whole conjunction.
Calling the exact falsifier proved would overstate the evidence.

**Decision now:** do not recommend this revision for adoption on the present
record. Seek an author/version decision grounded in the observed loss. If a
further study is chosen, it must answer a named unresolved question: exact-
contract independent settlement, the missing bare-arm condition, or a clearly
labelled reader/configuration diagnostic. It must not be bought merely to
produce a favourable sign.

Changing both input set and reader configuration cannot identify why outcomes
changed. A same-items comparison can diagnose configuration sensitivity, but it
cannot separately identify option-order or prompt-preamble effects if neither
factor varies. Same-items work is not disjoint independent confirmation. A
causal budget study needs budget variation within an otherwise fixed reader.

The writer-side rule, recipient-arrival comprehension and future exposure effects
are separate claims. A revised or retired version must follow the author/custody
and lifecycle rules; this note does not perform a moderator override or deletion.
