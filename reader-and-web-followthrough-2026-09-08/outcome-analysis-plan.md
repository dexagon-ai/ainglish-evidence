# Outcome programme: prospective analysis and execution boundaries

The primary programmes remain **240 careful-English and 240 compact-technical-English targets**, 120 per predicate per comparator. They share worlds; they are not independent replications. The careful contrast is primary, while compact English tests whether any result survives a stronger incumbent comparator. Every stateless cell receives identical definitions in both arms. No claim of cold comprehension or trained-weight improvement follows.

The original four-bit question covers truth, support membership, uniqueness of the mode, and next-outcome guarantee. It does **not** directly measure the greater-than-one-half misconception. Two separately frozen 120-target supplements ask that question and model certification explicitly. A 32-target specification supplement checks the finite-discrete scope and includes sufficient-model controls. These shorter questions must not be pooled into the primary scalar or substituted after a disappointing result.

Before minting, record a real independent review covering each exact item digest. Confirm both declared full-coverage cost originals remain active and independently confirmed within the +6 allowance; check every reported tokenizer, predicate and comparator cell, not just the least-favourable summary. Re-read the proposal, exact-target suggestions and public discussion. If the meaning, prediction, source status, reader qualification, endpoint or disk gate has changed, stop. The runner’s missing-review-file failure is intentional, not something to bypass.

Use the official fixed-seed panel, mint before any experimental calls, and retain calibration, every target cell, aborts and transport outcomes. Two cached qualified model families, no substitution or downloads. With this panel design, each item is randomly assigned an arm per reader; it is not a within-reader two-arm retest. Qualifications/calibration are instrument checks, not language results.

Replay the filed commitment, cell allocation, answer scoring, stratified estimate and attested item-bootstrap interval from retained cells. Publish separately for each predicate and comparator:

- Exact four-bit accuracy, raw correct/total counts and official stratum-weighted estimates.
- Difference and 95% interval. The declared -3 percentage-point noninferiority bound is passed only if the relevant lower bound clears it; a crossing interval is inconclusive. No multiplicity or template-cluster robustness is claimed by the ordinary item bootstrap.
- The declared 90% target, adverse <85% signal and >10% critical false-guarantee rate, with denominators and uncertainty. Reporting a point estimate does not establish a threshold with certainty.
- Every domain, boundary, variant and reader, including failures. Majority, certification and specification results remain separate diagnostic tables.

Do not interpret noninferiority as an advantage. Do not use current English-trained model or tokenizer costs to prove permanent unsuitability of Ainglish, or hypothetical future training to excuse a present failed allowance. Independent confirmation requires another eligible principal, genuinely fresh complete inputs, and preservation of the source estimand and reader population. Repeating these same files is a reproducibility check, not independent confirmation.
