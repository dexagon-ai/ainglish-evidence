## What the retained errors show

World-outcome truth was usually recovered; obligation discharge was much less reliable. On successful effort with **no required report**, attempt discharge was correctly denied in only **1/19 Ainglish** and **2/13 English** cells. Readers frequently treated success as sufficient despite the reporting obligation. With failed effort and no report, exact scores were **7/14 Ainglish** and **2/18 English**. These are descriptive allocated cell counts, not newly pooled causal estimates.

When adequate failed effort **was** accurately reported, attempt discharge was recognized in **12/18 Ainglish** and **11/14 English** cells. That differs from the earlier study’s 0/12 and 0/20 failure-completion scores, but the study changed definitions, question and design. It neither repairs the old result nor identifies a single cause of it.

One important boundary deserves semantic review: the new diagnostic explicitly keys `ensure` by whether its outcome holds, even when the worker made no effort. That key scored **2/15 Ainglish** and **0/17 English** cells. A reader may be importing a causal/agency condition from ordinary “make sure”; this is a competing interpretation to examine, not a reason to rewrite the observed golds. The frozen definition and factual record must be read alongside this result.

Adding the explicit discharge explanation did not uniformly solve the problem: descriptive exact accuracy was 66.99% Ainglish versus 67.42% English in that condition; the mapping-only condition was 68.93% versus 60.67%. Every framing, definition, domain, boundary, tag and reader table is in `execution/analysis.json`. These point comparisons are not a confirmed explanation of mechanism.

## Progression consequence

This is additional **original diagnostic evidence**, not an independent confirmation, cold-reading test, test of actual agent execution, human validation or future-training result. It does not by itself clear the proposal for voting. Preserve the earlier findings and obtain an independent review of the obligation-versus-outcome interpretation before commissioning a fresh-input confirmation of any precise claim. Do not rerun toward a more favourable score or silently alter the discharge key.
