# Full-careful comprehension progression studies

The completed studies, exact evidence links and current decision boundaries are in
[RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md). Additional zero-spend designs are in [NEXT-KITS.md](NEXT-KITS.md).

These are new, prospective studies, not re-labelled replications of older aggregates.
The `*-v2` item files are the reviewed freeze. Earlier local draft files were not spent
or published and are not part of this packet. Gold/balance tests run before readers.

## Primary studies

| Study | Real items | Separate form strata | Question |
|---|---:|---|---|
| by-construction / by-rule / in-practice | 192 | 64 per form | Could an exception occur, and what would an observed counterexample imply? |
| some-or-all / some-but-not-all | 256 | 128 per form | Joint recovery of the lower and upper count bounds, with question order/polarity balanced |

Each has eight **separate, target-independent** planted controls, two existing qualified
reader lineages, an exact frozen sampler and a fresh preregistration before any real model
call. No models are downloaded. A small runner guard strengthens the SDK's pooled calibration
gate: each reader must individually achieve the frozen gap before the first real call.
Its test specifically proves that a competent partner cannot rescue a flat reader.
The guard never examines real answers to decide whether to continue. Raw calibration and
real-cell journals, including failures, are retained by the ordinary SDK harness.

All finite directions are filed. There is one fixed run per study, no extension or
outcome-conditioned model choice. Item-bootstrap uncertainty is conditional on these fixed
domain/template frames and readers; repeated frames with different context IDs are not
independent samples of all possible human language. Neither study is human validation.

The regime source `40702354347269f4230a1e2964522d8da3081fc7a188229204a00b833dba0d0e`
has two floor-bound strata and asks readers to decode terse consequence letters. This
motivates a readable, joint-consequence instrument, but does not prove the old result invalid.
The some source `d4c3d08e4533cc2fda6cc1e5b5636e5d795175a390d51994e2d98f1cf179d22c`
mixes bare and careful controls on 12 items. A full-careful, per-form panel answers a different
question. Runspecs use `legacy_contract_repair_of` for source lineage, never `replicates_hash`.
Linking a new original does not authorize retirement of its source.

Per-form non-inferiority within five percentage points is an interpretation threshold, not
permission to relax the registered claim or replace missing tests. A pooled success cannot
hide a failing form. Bare-English, broader boundary/coverage, robustness, and post-definition
learning tests are **not** established by these primary studies. All earlier adverse/null
results and flagship deferrals remain visible until their substantive conditions are met.

The readers have extensive English training and are not assumed to know Ainglish. These
measure present cold readability. They neither establish nor rule out improvement after
future exposure/training. Current failures are retained, not excused by that possibility.

`prepare_primary.py <published-item-commit>` creates modern source-linked contracts and
binds exact existing qualifications. `run_primary_once.py regime|some` freshly checks the
proposal, satisfied token prerequisite, budget, GPU availability, commitment and qualification;
it then uses SDK preflight/mint/run/file-or-abort. Existing attempt receipts prohibit reruns.
Credentials are supplied by the local helper, never included in this public packet.
