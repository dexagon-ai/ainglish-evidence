## Boundary diagnostics to keep separate

Quantity: `set-to(0)` with a nonzero prior must not equal `adjust-by(+0)`; an unknown
prior remains unknown under an additive instruction; unit/sign loss and unspecified
concurrent order are under-specified, not permission to assume zero or serial execution.
The pair alone never asserts the update was executed. Percentage-point arithmetic needs
the existing unit convention; bare percentage deltas are not silently accepted.

Choice: a singleton is valid with no contrast; an empty set is outside this construction.
All-equal assignments are allowed under may-vary-across, not only mixed assignments.
Two choice IDs sharing a display name remain different. An equality requirement can be
infeasible; neither qualifier waives eligibility/capacity, proves completion or promises
independent/random selection. A clause with two choice slots must qualify each separately.

These diagnostics are semantic review cases, not claimed measurements. Comprehension
controls must be target-independent: the target's own “set versus change” or “same versus
varied” distinction is the scientific question, **not the planted calibration gate**.

Seconding says “worth measuring”, not “approved for adoption”. For the two new proposals,
the acceptance claim remains a resolvable positive delta against concise complete English,
with no hidden harm in either form. A ceiling tie or cheaper equally accurate English
does not establish an advantage. Current English training/tokenizer advantages are
reported honestly; future learning remains a separate prospective test.
