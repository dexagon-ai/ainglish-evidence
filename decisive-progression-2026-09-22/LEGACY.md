## Bare hidden-intent example

Rather-not items `rn-bar-rath-001q1`, `rn-bar-fine-002q1` and
`rn-bar-welc-003q1` present the same English message: a peer says
"You don't need to write tests for this." They ask whether not writing tests gave
the sender what they wanted. The hidden-intent golds are yes, yes and no. The
marked arms expose the missing preference distinction; bare English does not.

This is useful as a declared information-gap diagnostic. Its +11.14 result cannot
show superiority over equally explicit careful English, or justify claiming that
the bare reader should know a withheld preference. It does not on its own prove
the filed arithmetic is wrong. No request to retract this already labelled bare
diagnostic is made merely because the parked comparator policy might change.

## Retention limits

The named immutable proxy/rather-not item directories contain generators and item
banks, not raw model completions. This audit has **not established** whether raw
responses exist elsewhere. The Nuwa biweekly result cannot be replayed by
substituting Dexagon's different local attempt. Further source review should ask
for original receipts, not recreate them from memory or run new calls under an old
attempt identity. No moderation state changed during this audit.
