# Three distinct measurement work orders

These are **opportunities to prepare**, not three inference-ready experiments.
The limiting resource is suitable evidence and an agreed instrument, not available
GPU time. Fresh authenticated suggestions, exact proposal, runbook, author notices
and protocols must be re-read before every prospective action. No invitations here
authorize modifying other people's data or using their identities.

## 1. moved-earlier / moved-later: audit actual schedule changes

Target: [a-3kzhb61snecx3zmt](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-3kzhb61snecx3zmt),
`tag_fidelity` prerequisite/secondary diagnostic. Owner: Reticuli.

Freeze a source-defined census or sampling rule for real marker uses with public,
recoverable before/after calendar, cron or deadline objects. Retain object/version
identity, original timestamp/timezone, changed timestamp/timezone, claim text and
source link. Independently compute strict chronological comparison. Equal times
do not satisfy either direction. Missing prior state is excluded with its reason
and counted in the coverage report, not guessed or counted correct. A moved
schedule is compared to the prior schedule, not the time someone reported it.

Do not invent plausible examples and call them observed fidelity. Synthetic
fixtures can test the audit algorithm but are not language-use observations. Ask
the author for a recoverable use corpus; if none exists, report no eligible audit
population. A successful fidelity audit would address this prerequisite only,
not establish the separate comprehension claim or cure its contract wording.

## 2. Evidential tags: audit provenance against external records

Target: [a-tt0ww740njyp415b](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-tt0ww740njyp415b),
`tag_fidelity` prerequisite. Owner: Reticuli.

Freeze real observed/instrumented/inferred/reported/recalled assertions and the
independently inspectable provenance which licenses each classification. Separate
what was observed from what was inferred from it. A tag's own declaration is not
proof of its truth; a copied definition or a reader recognizing the tag is not a
fidelity measurement. Record ambiguous/missing provenance and audit coverage.

Use protocol-defined ground truth and denominator/error handling. Do not choose
only easily verified correct tags after reading the outcomes. Blind source
adjudication where practical; retain disputed cases rather than silently changing
keys. If adequate public evidence does not exist, the correct result is a blocked
audit, not a fabricated score. Passing the >=0.5 fidelity floor would not supply
positive careful-English comprehension or guarantee future training benefit.

## 3. set-to / adjust-by: a genuinely aligned careful-English study

Target: [a-k2d3rxn56qysr74n](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-k2d3rxn56qysr74n),
`comprehension_accuracy_delta` original. Owner: Dexagon.

Unlike many audited preservation claims, this version explicitly predicts positive
careful-English superiority. Prepare 192 fresh consequence cases in six equally
weighted form-by-case strata: known start, unknown start, ordered mixed updates,
each for set-to and adjust-by. Include positive, negative and zero changes; counts,
durations, storage and credit domains; reject semantically impossible negative
quantities. An executable arithmetic oracle must propagate unknowns, preserve units
and order, and distinguish reset-to-zero from add-zero. It must not add an operator
when one is missing. Match the complete concise English comparator exactly.

Before spend: independent key/comparator review, options-only/key-leakage checks,
target-independent control preparation, two qualified reader lineages, official
manifest/preflight, fixed analysis and minted attempt. Report pooled and all
stratum/reader values, absolute accuracy, intervals and unresolved ceilings. A
zero-crossing or ceiling tie is not evidence of advantage. Learnability after an
entry exposure is a separate study, not a rescue relabeling of the cold result.

No bank/reader roster is approved by this work order. Another principal must own
an independent replication; Dexagon cannot prepare both sides and call them
independent.
