# Compatibility-rule sensitivity: all 36 prespecified cases

**Synthetic numerical study, not Ainglish comprehension evidence or a formal
`unclaimed_verdict_flips` measurement.** Plan/source were frozen at commit
`089e141853720addef837066f36754bfa95517c3` before the full campaign. All 36
cases completed: 12 conditions × 3 sizes × 500 simulated pairs = **18,000
original/replica pairs**. No reader calls, downloads or governance writes.

Six tests passed, including actual SDK raw-bootstrap/point parity on both
fixed numeric fixtures, complete-journal matrix hashes, reproducibility,
degeneracy and the unobservable-arm refusal. Full counts, widths, draw counts,
matrix/source hashes and compiler are under `results/`; no case is omitted.

## The useful improvement and its limit

Percent of simulated pairs called compatible by the **proposed** pooled-and-
every-form interval rule, unconditional on holds:

| Prespecified true-effects condition | N=32 per form | N=128 | N=512 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Equal effects, moderate accuracy | 97.8% | 99.4% | 98.0% |
| One form differs by 5 pp | 98.0% | 96.8% | 91.4% |
| Both forms differ by 10 pp | 91.6% | 68.2% | 7.0% |
| Opposite 10 pp form shifts cancel in the pooled effect | 94.2% | 82.4% | 19.6% |
| Both differ by 10 pp, 12.5% marked-arm allocation | 91.2% | 84.4% | 38.4% |

Each percentage has denominator 500. These are conditional Monte Carlo
estimates, not exact long-run rates; small differences between cells are not
evidence of a meaningful trend. N counts synthetic items per form: a simulated
run has 4N reader cells, not N calls and not N independent real participants.

For the equal-effect/moderate-accuracy case, the **current** pooled-interval
plus per-form-point component accepts **0/500, 0/500 and 1/500** at those sizes.
Thus replacing near-exact form-point coincidence can remove a substantial
source of failed replication on these synthetic designs. It does not make
agreement evidence of equal, precise or sufficiently good effects.

The large separated-effect overlap rates at small N explain why the label
must remain **compatibility**, not demonstrated equality. Mean original
form-0 interval width for the equal/moderate case is **38.36, 19.42 and
9.79 pp**. At N=512 under the intentionally imbalanced allocation it is
**14.70 pp**, and 38.4% of the 10 pp-separated pairs still overlap throughout.
Those imbalance overrides are numeric stress inputs, not SDK-valid
hash-allocation plans or a proposed language study.

This is not a calibrated “false equality test” error rate: interval overlap
never promised such a test. The counted risk is precisely **compatibility
despite a prespecified effect separation**. Do not redefine it after observing
the table, or choose live sample size/thresholds solely to obtain agreement.

## Degeneracy, guards and actual holds are different

- At .999/.999 accuracy, proposed compatibility is 100%, 100%, 97.8%; an
  original has an exact-zero/one arm in 100%, 100%, 97.8% of trials. All
  originals also trigger the **current** generic resolution guard. Interval
  compatibility does not itself repair a degenerate bounded prerequisite or
  make an evidence-ready proposal. These are distinct calculations.
- At .95/.95 and N=512, 492/500 pairs are interval-compatible, no original
  has an exact-zero/one arm, and all 500 originals trigger the current generic
  ceiling guard. The proposed opted prerequisite reading and unchanged generic
  stance must stay visibly distinct; this study does not simulate their full
  lifecycle combination or the independent confirmed-loss veto.
- The unobservable-arm sentinel holds **500/500 pairs at every size**. No
  replacement outcomes or draws are generated. Its absent main arm makes all
  2,000 replica draws unobservable; these are counted as nonaccepted without
  computing useless quantiles. Other fixed complete-allocation cases had no
  missing-arm draws. Separate joint-mask boundary tests remain necessary.
- Near-zero and shared-frame stress cases are retained in the complete output.
  Near-zero is a failed-reader stress condition, not a qualified experiment.
  Shared-frame cases intentionally violate item independence; these outputs
  do not validate coverage. A different interval method is not silently used.

A degenerate-arm frequency is **not** automatically the bounded-rule hold
frequency: a valid opposing component can take precedence over another held
component. This campaign does not collapse those into a single pass rate.

## What this supports doing next

1. Continue validating the prospective rule: the synthetic comparison shows
   why near-exact form-point coincidence can obstruct even equal-effect pairs.
2. Preserve explicit interval widths, per-form findings and the compatibility
   label. An accepted replication is not a precision or claim-quality test;
   author-declared directional requirements and the loss veto still matter.
3. Complete the **separate** server-counterfactual F1–F11 and frozen-population
   checks. This study does not establish unchanged historical verdicts,
   correct mint applicability, exact server rounding, actual attestation or
   independent settlement eligibility. It cannot substitute for that evidence.

No minimum N, confidence promise, language threshold or current rule was
changed here. The 36-case campaign is complete; protocol adoption and the
subsequent operative implementation remain governance decisions.
