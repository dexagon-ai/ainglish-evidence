## Role-cardinality correction

Canonical hashes verified against the filed manifests. Across the two bare comparator banks,
all 120 paired scenarios have the same English text, question and answer vocabulary. Twenty
assign **opposite gold answers** according to the hidden intended marker. Option order differs;
it does not communicate the intended cardinality.

Example: “Rowan and Sable are distinct reviewers, and both approved the release. Instruction:
A reviewer must approve the release.” The same satisfaction question is scored yes for the
one-or-more experiment and no for the exactly-one experiment. The English reader was not told
the latter restriction. In the other conflicting family, the extra-principal violation question
is likewise answered according to the hidden marker rather than what the bare text states.

This can describe recovery of an assigned sender intention under a deliberately incomplete
baseline. It cannot be treated as meaning-equivalent comprehension evidence or as proof that
the safe English reading is wrong. Both source records are withdrawn from authoritative CAD
evidence, regardless of sign; nothing is deleted from their public history:

- `ac6bb7c60d7338568cee1c9f7560877d7861e0c870a4016698ec7f2cb4f61263`, +7.63 pp.
- `c6d3e3bd47a72207a4d2df223adb14791428107ae793d2aea79720a0440d25b6`, -0.52 pp.

The **careful-English** originals remain separate and unchanged:

- Exactly-one `31b5db3d…`: +8.21 pp, interval [-3.94, 20.2009], marked 62.61%, English 54.40%.
- One-or-more `e0530e7a…`: -1.29 pp, interval [-12.6263, 10.4799], marked 70.63%, English 71.93%.

The latter has reader deltas +14.14 and -16.16 pp. Neither form has independent confirmation.
These are not ready-made noninferiority passes; passing a pointwise bound in one slice would
not certify the full bundle. The audit does not invent a new benchmark result or revise golds
and resubmit favorable old responses. An honest bare control needs a separate descriptive
question/scoring contract, while any successor must predeclare its complete careful comparator.
