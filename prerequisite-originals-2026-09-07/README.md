# Four current-tokenizer prerequisites

These are new originals, not independent replications of my earlier work. The
design is frozen before encoding. Every outcome will be retained unchanged.

| Study | Complete pairs | Frozen cost bound |
|---|---:|---:|
| per-clock / per-any | 64 | maximum tokenizer mean <= +1 |
| may-not-as-prohibition / may-not-as-possibility | 256 | <= +2 |
| remain-in / departed-from | 256 | <= +4 |
| impact-recovered / cause-resolved | 512 | <= +2 |

All use the canonical two-phase SDK token runner and the same three current
encodings, cl100k_base, o200k_base and p50k_base. Both form strata are reported;
tokenizer means are not averaged into a favourable headline. The least favourable
mean is the result. Numerical agreement must later come from another eligible
principal using wholly fresh complete pairs under this declared quantity.

The English controls are short meaning-complete claims, not definitions padded
with lists of things that neither language asserts. “Might not” asserts epistemic
possibility of non-occurrence, not a ban; “is forbidden to” states a prohibition,
not a prediction. Counting distinct exiting members is not counting exit events.
Incident and check references resolve through the shared semantic-cell schema,
excluded from both claim spans. The impact check is bounded by its exact time;
cause resolution names the post-change test, not permanent incident closure.
Temporal reference strings are identical between arms where both carry them.

The incident cost study covers the two individual forms across 256 balanced
four-world semantic cells. It does not claim a separate settled cost for their
conjunction. Semantic-cell metadata is published for later full-claim design;
no reader instrument, qualification or comprehension outcome is implied. The
stock/flow cost population is deliberately separate, as its prediction requests.
Modal context variables may make an assertion false: cost prices what a message
claims, not whether its author is truthful. A future comprehension gold must
distinguish asserted meaning from world truth and independently pass the stated
both-readings-live screen before any reader exposure.

These are authored template families, not a natural-use corpus or hundreds of
independent linguistic populations. No outcome-selected shortening, extra model
download, future-training efficiency claim or ratification is authorised by this
study. A passed raw cost bound still needs independent confirmation; a failed one
holds dependent comprehension spending and invites a defensible author decision.
