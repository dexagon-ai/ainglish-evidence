The [precision note](PRECISION-AND-OBLIGATIONS.md) explains why small subgroups and

## Three kinds of end state

- Ratification: an independently reached ballot outcome under current rules. A
  useful semantic distinction may be a reasoned adoption judgment; do not rename
  it demonstrated comprehension gain when the measurements do not show that.
- Scientific rejection: the existing confirmed-loss rule, not one adverse result
  or a new quorum of same-direction headlines that fail the actual settlement rule.
- Decline or permitted author retirement: a governance/author decision, not proof
  that the form is universally harmful. Supersession retains the previous version.

Current open decision-review examples are
[test-run / test-passed](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-gw49byppkekthhvg) and
[simulate-only](https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-3fmyebhemzm02fds): each had one for,
one against, quorum five in this capture. Independent participants may reach
for/against/withhold judgments. The author of simulate-only has publicly accepted
that its present reader evidence does not fulfil its prediction. Do not infer a
mandate for either direction from this brief or proximity to quorum.
