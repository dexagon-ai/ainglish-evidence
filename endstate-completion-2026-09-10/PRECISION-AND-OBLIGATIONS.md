# Decide what a study can establish before spending

This is planning mathematics and a reading of existing promises. It does **not**
add a confidence level, sample-size gate, veto exception or acceptance rule to
Ainglish. The official protocol and prospectively reviewed claim remain decisive.

## Perfect observed performance is not arbitrary population precision

For `n` genuinely independent Bernoulli trials with **zero observed errors**, a
one-sided confidence bound obtained by inverting the binomial tail is
`upper = 1 - alpha**(1/n)`. This is the zero-failure specialization of the exact
binomial method described by [NIST](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm),
with alpha in the one-sided rather than alpha/2 two-sided tail. It is not a
paired-comprehension effect interval or a replacement for the panel estimator.

At illustrative one-sided 95% confidence, zero errors in 10 independent cases
still permits an error rate of about 25.9%; 20 cases permit about 13.9%; 32 permit
about 8.94%. At least 59 zero-error independent cases are needed for this particular
upper bound to fall below 5%. This is an optimistic best case, not a guarantee that
a 59-case study will show a 5% bound. Multiple simultaneous claims need their own
prospective uncertainty treatment; Bonferroni is shown only as one conservative
illustration in the executable planner.

The simple binomial assumptions **do not automatically hold** for paraphrases,
several answers about the same world, multiple calls to one model lineage or
arbitrarily selected fictional examples. Do not treat nominal rows as independent
trials merely to use the larger denominator. Do not infer a population interval
from all-success bootstrap cells without validating the sampling scope.

## Specific consequences for this cohort

- **May-not:** the existing blind packet has 20 unique bare clauses supporting 160
  contextual primary rows. Whether the proposed minimum 128 applies to contextual
  rows or independent clause/scenario units is an author/design decision still
  awaiting clarification. Its four extra non-entailment types per form also have
  distinct false-inference and positive-sensitivity questions; one pooled number
  cannot prove all the error ceilings. Keep the balanced controls already prepared.
- **Remain-in / departed-from:** the careful-English packet contains 128 worlds
  and 512 questions. Repeated questions per world are not four independent worlds.
  Per-domain and per-form reports must retain their actual smaller denominators.
  The bare-left benefit remains a different, carefully identified contrast.
- **Sanction:** the 64-row routing component has 32 items per form but only eight
  examples in a given form/force cell. A reported point threshold must not be
  described as a well-estimated universal error ceiling on that basis.
- **Rent:** learning controls are an instrument-sensitivity check. Passing those
  eight controls is neither accuracy on the 64 targets nor the missing separate
  comprehension evidence. A taught-vs-cold effect is not weight training.
- **Most importantly, keep the comparator honest:** if the same visible context
  already specifies the intended interpretation in the bare-English arm, then a
  truthful reader may have little room to improve. Do not strip that context only
  from English, invent hidden-intention golds, or treat honest abstention on an
  underdetermined message as a comprehension error.

## The advisory flag is not the full set of scientific obligations

At this capture the register flags 31 proposals for preservation/superiority review.
The primary text of **may-not** and **remain-in / departed-from** also promises
noninferiority and separate bare-English benefits, while their current advisory
flag is absent. This is why an absent flag cannot be taken as an acceptance-policy
clearance. Read the full prediction. Do not treat the 31 as exhaustive, or as 31
automatic passes after a policy update.

Today's confirmed-comprehension-loss veto remains. A materially changed hypothesis
or margin needs a prospective, openly reviewed successor; old exposed outcomes
must not be relabelled into success. English's incumbent training/tokenizer
advantages are relevant limitations, not evidence of a future training benefit.
