## Prospective crossed diagnostic, if shared access is verified

1. Author and independently review one **new, never executed** form-balanced item
   set. Keep the same resolved witnesses, transport setting, temporal scope and
   ordinary-English informational content in each paired arm. Include explicit
   adequate, inadequate, absent and unresolved-witness cases, with their diagnostic
   scope declared. Do not borrow any exposed source/replication example as fresh.
2. Freeze the full items, gold ledger, prompt, opaque answer mapping, controls,
   scoring and a fixed primary comparison before any target calls. No online
   revision of an ambiguous key after observing which reader got it wrong.
3. Every declared reader configuration receives every complete item in separate
   stateless cells, with both wording arms and prespecified order/counterbalancing.
   All reader configurations must be chosen and qualified before any target is run.
4. Treat `reader configuration` as a bundle of model, provider, precision, context,
   reasoning settings and output budget. **To identify a budget effect**, vary
   budget within the same otherwise-fixed accessible model configuration. If only
   different models/providers are available, report a configuration association,
   not the causal effect of budget, quantization, training or model family.
5. Report every model and required form, paired item contrasts and uncertainty at
   the justified world/item cluster level. More question variants or endpoints
   are not automatically more independent units/lineages. Do not select one of
   several scopes, uncertainty recipes or readers after seeing its sign.
6. Preserve refusals, truncations, malformed outputs and calibration failures.
   A failed run yields its actual abort/partial receipt; do not silently buy more
   output budget and splice new answers into the same frozen study.

### What this can and cannot finish

This **same-items** cross is a prospective diagnostic, not a fresh-input independent
replication between participating executors. It can locate a sensitivity and inform
whether a narrowly scoped successor is justified. To settle an original under the
existing rule, use a separate eligible principal, exact target contract and wholly
fresh inputs; name the original's actual hash. A different-population diagnostic
must not carry that hash merely to acquire settlement weight.

If the official live runbook does not offer the diagnostic as a governance
measurement, publish it as a clearly labelled research artifact, not a substituted
claim-carrier filing. No diagnostic reader cells have been run for this design.
No exact shared roster, qualification, budget or complete final item set is yet
claimed to exist. This is a planning contract, not preregistration.

The next decision after admissible evidence is an independently judged adoption,
rejection under the current veto, or an authorised revised/retired version. Neither
more spend nor a favourable direction is mandatory.
