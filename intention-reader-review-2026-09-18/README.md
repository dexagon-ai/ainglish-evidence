## What checks out

There are 96 scientific items and 12 calibration items. The scientific split is
48 intended outcomes, 24 unforeseen unintended outcomes, and 24 knowingly
accepted but unwanted risks. They are separate load-bearing strata with weights
2:1:1. Six domains and first/third-person active/passive frames are represented.
The two readers are exact digest-pinned Q4_K_M editions of Mistral Small 3.2 24B
and Gemma 3 12B, not a claim about those model families generally.

Both arms share each item's context, report core, question and options. The
only rendered difference is `on-purpose` versus `deliberately`, or `by-accident`
versus `by mistake, without intending that outcome`. The question asks whether
the doer meant to bring about the outcome. The accepted-risk cases explicitly
say the possible side effect was unwanted and not sought, so their `no` gold
answer is consistent with the current intention-only definition. Foreseeability
alone is not labelled intention, and the answer does not assign blame.

Gold positions rotate evenly in every reader/stratum/arm exposure: 8:8:8 for
the positive stratum and 4:4:4 for each negative stratum. This is **not** an
all-first-answer-position instrument.

The published qualification receipts report 24/24 detectable and 0/24 other
correct per reader; the panel reports calibration gap/recovered of 1/1, no
transport faults, and no truncations. We verify the frozen screen's hash and
the reported counts, not missing raw qualification/calibration responses.

### Assignment limits causal interpretation

Within each reader, positive anchor template and arm are perfectly linked:

| Reader | Plan-a anchor | Plan-b anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Gemma | English only, 1/24 correct | Marked only, 5/24 correct |
| Mistral | Marked only, 24/24 correct | English only, 24/24 correct |

Plan-a says the doer chose and followed a plan with the outcome as its objective.
Plan-b says the doer adopted the result specified in a signed work order as its
objective. The pooled design counterbalances these across readers, but it has
no within-reader, within-template contrast. It cannot separate a marker effect
cleanly from a reader-by-template interaction. This is a design limitation,
**not proof that the reported difference was caused by the templates or an
automatic claim of protocol invalidity**.

The negative strata have a similar unobserved comparison: Mistral receives
marked wording only for first-person cases and English only for third-person
cases; Gemma receives the reverse. Both active and passive cases occur, but
within-reader/person/arm coverage is missing. All observed negative answers
are correct, so this is a coverage limitation, not observed negative-stratum
harm.

### Scope of the scientific claim

Every item explicitly supplies the doer's prior aim and instructs the reader
not to infer intention from benefit, harm, blame, or foreseeability. The shared
anchor makes the question answerable even without the target expression.
This is therefore **context-supported comprehension**, not proof that a bare
marker is intuitively understood, nor that it adds useful information to an
otherwise ambiguous sentence. Bare renderings were frozen but not run and
cannot be counted as a measured third arm.

Current zero-shot reader performance is not an estimate of future trained or
tokenizer-adapted performance. The present token result is a bounded cost, not
a saving; future efficiency remains a hypothesis until separately measured.
Nothing in this audit establishes human comprehension, training adoption, or
future tokenization benefit.

   both arms inside each anchor-template/person/voice block, and balance gold

   enough. At the existing 96-item size each anchor-by-frame block has six
   domains, allowing 3/3 arm exposure per reader; a larger bank is not inherently
   required to address this assignment defect. Freeze the content, assignment,
   seed, roster and scoring before any target-reader exposure. ID/assignment
   balancing must follow declared content-only rules, never outcome selection.

   new original rather than a compatible replication. Do not replace Gemma
   simply to obtain a more favourable score.
