## Why the simple contract swap is not a solution

The existing schema accepts a `token_delta` carrier and a bounded comprehension prerequisite.
However, `EvidenceReadiness::stanceFor()` retains a generic unresolved stance and otherwise compares
the point estimate to the bound. It does not invert a confidence interval or validate a NI design.
The current perfect-score row therefore stays unresolved, and a point above −5 is not proof of NI.
The independent confirmed-comprehension-loss veto also remains. We are not asking for this swap
as a way to turn existing evidence into a pass.
