## Meaning reviewed here

Each fresh world explicitly resolves a frozen, nonempty set of 2–8 distinct identities. Scores
are unique, with a minimum different from the first member. The five candidates each return once.
Constant-first and criterion-based have point-mass distributions. The unequal-weight procedure
assigns one identity weight 2, 3 or 4 and all others weight 1; with at least two identities it is
genuinely nonuniform. Equal-probability assigns every eligible identity exactly 1/N. The outsider
is explicitly excluded. Fractions, not floats, establish normalization and membership.

For choose-any every in-set distribution is allowed, including uniform sampling; no randomness
is prohibited. For draw-uniform only the equal-probability candidate is allowed. No realised
single outcome is used to judge that distribution. The guarantee gold follows the *instruction*,
not the additional properties of a permitted implementation: one eligible result for both, equal
odds additionally for draw-uniform, neither adversarial unpredictability nor later independence.
Public verifiability, replacement conventions, finite-run balance and speech-act flexibility are
not directly tested; invalid sets and duplicate-identity resolution are outside this valid-input
study. No result will be described as validating every boundary in the complete proposal.

The fresh file-only auditor checks the stored distributions against independently reconstructed
probabilities from world facts, derives expected allowed policy *kinds* directly, and derives the
guarantee labels separately. It does not merely rerun `permitted()` and call that independent.
This is a second implementation by the same investigator, not an independent principal.

## Comparator and scoring

The common context supplies the resolved/frozen/distinct-set preconditions to BOTH language arms.
The English request states each form's relevant obligation and non-guarantees explicitly. It is
an authored per-form expansion, not a claim of byte-identical reproduction of the combined register
paragraph. It deliberately helps the English reader; no meaning is omitted to create a gain.
The author must review these actual exported bytes before launch.

Both counterfactuals receive exactly the same ordered eight-record menu, policies, guarantee
statements and shared facts. Gold records differ. This repairs the demonstrated full options-only
solution on the paired audit envelope, not every conceivable shortcut in the unpaired sample.
One record encodes BOTH complete label lists. Joint exact accuracy is the ordinary official CAD
score; policy-only and guarantee-only scores are supplementary, decoded from that same answer.
Malformed live responses are joint wrong but their partial decisions are unknown, not invented.
Typed missing cells follow the official guard and remain visible in all reports.

Only memberships whose inclusion AND exclusion are offered receive an error-opportunity
denominator. The presence of a policy in a question does not create an observed rejection test.
Wrong-pole classifications count omitted licensed / included unlicensed policies in that actual
opportunity set. Report per-form/domain and per-policy counts, not just pooled percentages.

## Freshness and sampling

There are 144 distinct worlds, 24 per domain, with changed scenarios, identities, scores, weighted
probabilities and wording. Neutral worlds and the complete counterfactual menus are committed
BEFORE applying the fixed prospective assignment seed. Forms are assigned 12/12 within domain.
No seed search or post-assignment rebalancing is allowed. Only ONE assigned form per world enters
the official study. Its other form is audit-only and never an independent sample unit.

This remains an authored six-domain instrument with 12 shared frame families. More identities do
not prove independence or natural-language generalization. Same-host reader families are a fixed
panel, not a random sample of future models. The eight-choice burden may interact with language;
equal presentation does not prove that the effect cancels in the arm difference.
