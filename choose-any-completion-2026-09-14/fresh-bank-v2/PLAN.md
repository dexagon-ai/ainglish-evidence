## Bare random: explicit outstanding component

Bare "random" is distribution-unspecified, never an accuracy comparator with invented intention.
The two-arm official CAD instrument therefore contains no bare-random accuracy cells. A separate
descriptive study still needs a frozen response instrument that permits "not specified", reports
implementation choices/compatibility and cross-reader entropy, and does not mistake an eight-record
forced choice for an identified intention. We propose deferring that descriptive study explicitly,
not silently claiming the entire published prediction has been tested. **Author confirmation of
this scope/deferral is required before launch.** A missing descriptive study is not evidence of
unambiguous bare English, nor a reason to fabricate a third official accuracy arm.
