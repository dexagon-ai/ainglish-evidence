# Collect actual claims, not successful classification answers

The moved-earlier/moved-later prerequisite needs actual uses audited against
recoverable schedule transitions. The previous classifier result was retracted;
this packet does not revive it. No measurement has been filed from these fixtures.

If you genuinely need to report a schedule change during your work, retain:

1. The exact operative assertion, author, public source URL and source-byte digest.
2. The event's unambiguous identity and immutable before/after schedule records,
   including timezone, revision or record identifiers, URLs and byte digests.
3. Whether the assertion is a real report, a quotation/example, or a controlled
   laboratory use. Keep laboratory and natural-use populations separate.
4. An independent source inspection confirming that the records really support
   the extracted times and refer to the same event. No secrets or private logs.

Do not reschedule something just to generate evidence, repeat a billing action,
invent a calendar record, or retrofit a tag onto a quoted example. Unrecoverable
prior times are excluded and counted, not guessed. A real same-time transition
falsifies either claimed strict direction. Time-of-speaking is not the baseline.

`schedule_claim_audit.py` supplies a tested arithmetic audit after the source and
assertion-scope review. It compares timezone-aware instants and retains false,
excluded and unreviewed cases. It does **not** fetch or certify sources, select a
representative population, establish independent authorship or mint an attempt.
Its unit fixtures are never evidence. Zero eligible uses yields no fraction.

For the eventual scientific audit, freeze a new, genuinely sampled set of use
references and the population/exclusion policy, check the live SDK suggestion and
protocol, and mint before auditing. Keep the complete eligible sample, not only
examples that seem likely to be correct. File the audited fraction via the SDK,
then obtain the required independent confirmation on different uses. This cannot
be substituted with a reader comprehension quiz or an oracle-generated list of
correct tags.
