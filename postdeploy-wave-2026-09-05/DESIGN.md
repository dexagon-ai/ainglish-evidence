# Prospective post-deployment reader programme

This is a new instrument and new authored corpus, not a retry or re-score of the
previous mean primary aborts or of the instruction-scope source. All target items,
gold, strata and analysis are committed before target inference. Two existing
digest-bound Q4 models passed fresh target-independent qualification with the new
64-token response cap. Responses are option letters; explanations belong in the
common question. No reader retries, replacements, imputation or new models.

## Seven executable comparisons

1. Mean/median versus complete English: 128 joint statistic-and-reference questions,
   64 finite paired frames, both forms, eight quantity domains and eight numeric
   families including outliers, negatives, duplicate centres and even-sized medians.
2. A matched mean/median comparison with conventional short `Arithmetic mean of…`
   and `Median of…` English. Same marked arm, cases and assignment; not independent.
3. Separate mean/median consequences: 128 cases asking about individuals, observed
   centres, sample boundaries and exact re-checks. Diagnostic, never substitutes for
   the primary joint-identification result. Report each probe and both forms.
4–7. Quantity updates and shared choice, each cold and with a brief reference visible
   in both arms. Each comparison has 96 new items: 16 in each of six form/task cells.
   All earlier harmful cells remain represented. Quantity tests known and unknown
   starts plus ordered updates. Choice tests assignment validity, feasibility and
   whether variation is permitted under the complete eligibility constraints.

Each comparison adds eight unrelated planted controls, tested in both arms by each
reader before real items. Each reader must show a control gap of at least 0.5.
Zero absent, off-option, truncated or transport-fault cells are permitted. A failed
comparison closes as an abort; separately declared comparisons may still run.
Mean tests require the existing independently confirmed valid token prerequisite.
Quantity and choice have no declared token prerequisite. All targets must remain
visible, nonterminal and semantically unchanged immediately before mint.

Every admitted result is filed, including adverse and inconclusive results.
The official item-bootstrap interval is conditional on authored items and these
fixed readers. Report-only base-frame cluster intervals use 2,000 draws, seed
2026090583, with every stratum retained. No all-model or human-population inference.
Report per-reader and per-form/task absolute accuracies, baseline answer frequencies
and first observed faults. A −5 percentage-point noninferiority margin is report-only;
passing it is not an outcome admission filter or automatic ratification.

## Reference exposure is not model training

Both arms receive the corresponding complete conceptual explanation with the same
**50-word maximum budget**. The audit reports the actual word counts; it does not
claim equal native token count. No solved target examples are in the reference.
Cold and reference conditions share new held-out cases and random assignment;
their contrast is related descriptive evidence, not independent replication.
Report whether instructions help each language, not only their difference. No
claim about changed weights, future training efficiency or human comprehension.

## Instruction scope: design-only pending author decision

The additional 128-case corpus follows the CURRENT mapping: from-now-on applies to
later comparable work even in another project unless an explicit boundary/revocation
removes that application. It covers current work, same/other project, different work
kind, revocation, storage prohibition, audit retention and an explicit project limit.
All controls are unrelated; the old source's target-bearing calibration is not reused.
No inference or mint on this target until the mapping/gold issue has an explicit
author resolution. Old keys and observations remain immutable.

## Limits and safeguards

This campaign has no holdout human-validation quota. Editorial intuitive appeal is
separate. Semantic classes can be imbalanced even where option letters are balanced;
publish class counts and per-condition accuracy. Current readers already know English;
these results test this fixed cold/reference setting, not a future Ainglish-trained
model. Replication eligibility, token prerequisites, experimental fidelity, language
quality, ballot outcomes and release readiness remain distinct decisions.
