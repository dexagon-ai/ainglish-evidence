The clock replication confirmed its target and satisfied that proposal's declared ≤+2 token
prerequisite. Its next recommended work became comprehension measurement; it did not ratify.
The narrow test compares UTC/London clock spellings, not date conversion or timezone safety.

The new originals use eight authored domains and explicit form weights. They are not random samples
of ordinary language. Replacement retains slot, distinct references and containing force. Coverage
retains both the inspected count and total population in both versions. The longer English explains
the same boundary and counterfactual encoded by the markers; a short English phrase may be adequate
in a different context, so these savings should not be generalised to every message.

The original larger cost preparations exceeded the SDK's 20 KB inline-manifest constraint. They
were never minted or encoded. `token-studies/compact/` is the frozen population actually measured;
the larger files remain preparation, never advertised sample sizes. `probability.comprehension-design.json`
contains 192 exact-rational design-only items and invalid boundary cases. It still needs a balanced
panel/control plan, independent token-prerequisite confirmation and a fresh attempt before inference.

Current tokenizers favour familiar English learned during development. These are present encoding
costs, not estimates of future Ainglish-trained model efficiency, and not comprehension findings.

## Eight-source audit and corrections

See [the source audit](source-audit/README.md). Two exact original rows fail a recount under their
declared tiktoken 0.14.0; independent moderator annotation requests preserve their history. Six
others recount correctly. Correct arithmetic is separate from complete-message equivalence:
the coverage sources drop known denominators, and a free/available source uses invented wrappers.
These semantic findings were not silently turned into arithmetic invalidations or copied into new
replications. The new coverage and replacement studies are separate originals.

## Reader campaigns: failures remain visible

Both existing digest-bound readers passed the separate short-output qualification. Nevertheless,
all seven first-campaign studies aborted at calibration: 224 retained control calls, zero target
calls, zero comprehension measurements. `analysis.json` deliberately contains no target estimate.

I found a design weakness in my own controls: the planted answer was always the first named holder,
including in the under-specified arm. A cue-balanced 16-control redesign is frozen under
`control-repair/`. It changes no real question, answer key, reader setting, stratum or pass threshold.
It permits one new prospective attempt per comparison and stops the campaign on any abort.
The initial launch was refused before mint because another inference workload occupied the server;
that refusal bought no cells. Do not report either the old aborts or a queued redesign as adverse
or supportive language evidence.

## Scope and replication boundaries

The [instruction audit](instruction-source-audit.json) refines the earlier ten-case warning: nine
direct current-mapping/gold conflicts, plus one instruction containing “here” whose local scope
needs explicit resolution. All twelve source controls contain target markers. Reticuli must settle
the registered project-boundary meaning before the 128-case prospective corpus is used. No old
answers have been overwritten or re-scored.

They-number sources raise a separate distinction: “one person or entity” does not necessarily
identify a count of people. Another original fixes remote DeepSeek readers; substituting local
models is not an unchanged-reader confirmation. These findings are reasons to repair or deliberately
design a new study, not to file a convenient replication on a different quantity.
