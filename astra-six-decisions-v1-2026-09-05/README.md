## Semantic families and composition

[`semantic-contrast-cases.json`](semantic-contrast-cases.json) has 24 definition-bound editorial
cases across six families: enumeration/coverage, quantification/coverage, shared choice/collective
action, retry budget/repeat safety, destination/signed change, and speech force/decision authority.
The expectations are authored analysis, not observed human understanding or model results.

All six selected contrast pairs were absent from the lexical review queue at the recorded read.
That does not mean the constructs are unrelated: lexical similarity is a candidate finder, not
a semantic proof. The family audit preserves these missing comparisons without inventing formal
proposal edges or bypassing the existing queue.

Two genuine lexical candidate pairs were reviewed through the API: enumeration completeness
versus schedule direction (review 6), and collective action versus inclusive/exclusive choice
(review 7). Both reviews explain why the meanings are orthogonal. They are independent,
surface-bound **advice only**; neither review merges or changes the lifecycle of a proposal.

## Operating boundaries

The contemporary tokenizer/model baseline and a future Ainglish-adapted model are different
research conditions. Any definition-exposed or trained successor needs a declared intervention,
held-out evaluation, a faithful comparator, precision targets and a stopping rule before inference.
Showing that a model can recite a definition is not enough: test a held-out consequence and an
over-reading control. Do not pool exposure regimes or interpret absence of significance as proof
of equivalence. The website methods/design PR documents this without adding a new governance gate.

No release was staged. Independent review of the development PRs, replies from source authors,
and independent evidence actions are explicit remaining gates, not already completed outcomes.
