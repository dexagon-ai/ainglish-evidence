# Clusivity comprehension recertification carrier v1

This packet modernises the comprehension evidence path for Ainglish's clearest ratified flagship example: `we-including-you` says the addressee belongs to the relevant first-person-plural group; `we-excluding-you` says the addressee does not.

The earlier 2026-08-23 diagnostic cannot carry the claim: long answer labels hit an undeclared 40-character output boundary, and the post-run prefix observation was never a corrected measurement. A later opaque-code original avoided that truncation but used a reader roster that was not independently qualified under the current gate. Both records remain visible; neither is silently reinterpreted.

## New carrier

Two answer-bearing packets are frozen before any scientific call:

- `zero-shot.json`: 80 new contexts crossed with both forms, 160 scientific rows and 12 construct-free controls;
- `definition-conditioned.json`: a disjoint 80-context/160-row packet with one exact reference card, plus 12 different controls.

Every context has a hidden intended inclusion value and two otherwise identical bare-`we` messages with opposite gold values. This makes the bare arm genuinely unable to recover both intentions from topic or wording. The marked and careful-English arms carry the value explicitly. Eight balanced frames cover future commitments, obligations, permissions, completed actions, current activity, ownership, recommendations, and notification membership.

Each isolated arm must jointly recover:

1. whether the addressee is inside or outside the intended we-group;
2. whether the sentence routes its predicate to the addressee through that group;
3. that the marker does not rule out outsiders independently having the same consequence.

Short answer labels and opaque transport codes avoid the prior truncation defect. Support is form-specific: at least +20 percentage points versus paired bare English, no more than 5 points below complete careful English, and at least 85% absolute joint accuracy. The additional per-question, per-frame, form-collapse, and outsider-overread falsifiers in each packet prevent a pooled score from hiding the dangerous mistakes.

## Training-data interpretation

English was present in current readers' training data; Ainglish generally was not. Zero-shot performance therefore measures present surface transparency under that asymmetry, not the full efficiency expected after future Ainglish training. The definition-conditioned packet measures one-card learnability on wholly disjoint inputs. It cannot overwrite an adverse zero-shot result, and neither condition alone proves future pretraining behavior. Token prices likewise remain current-tokenizer descriptions rather than verdicts on a future Ainglish-aware tokenizer.
