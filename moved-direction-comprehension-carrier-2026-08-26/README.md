# `moved-earlier / moved-later` comprehension carrier

This is a fresh, no-model freeze for the live flagship candidate
`moved-earlier / moved-later`. The live proposal is formally ballot-ready but its declared
evidence contract is not evidence-ready: the confirmed token prerequisite is present, while the
`comprehension_accuracy_delta` claim carrier and `tag_fidelity` prerequisite are missing.

Four campaigns keep both forms and both comparisons separate:

- `moved-earlier` versus its full careful-English mapping;
- `moved-later` versus its full careful-English mapping;
- `moved-earlier` versus a balanced bare schedule-change phrase;
- `moved-later` versus the byte-identical bare phrase in the paired opposite-intent world.

Each campaign has 120 scientific pairs—20 in each of meetings, maintenance, scheduled jobs,
governance closes, deadlines, and delivery slots—plus eight construct-free calibrations. The
scientific set contains 48 calendar/date-recovery probes, 48 old-trigger action-consequence probes,
and 24 over-reading probes covering unstated amount, absolute time, notification, and finality.
Every bare comparator frame occurs in two hidden-intent worlds with identical comparator text and
opposite correct direction. No default interpretation can score well on both.

The carrier deliberately stops before runspec construction, attempt minting, or model exposure.
The terminal v7 reader receipt has no qualified two-lineage roster, and its digest is pinned in
`index.json`. A later qualification successor may consume these already-public items, but it may
not tune readers on them or relabel them as a fresh holdout after exposure.

Rebuild and audit offline from the repository root:

```bash
python3 moved-direction-comprehension-carrier-2026-08-26/build_items.py
python3 moved-direction-comprehension-carrier-2026-08-26/audit.py
python3 moved-direction-comprehension-carrier-2026-08-26/audit_external_originals.py
```

The first two commands make zero network, governance, tokenizer, or model calls. The third performs
public reads of the four content-addressed original measurements and item sets, and refuses unless
every frozen campaign has zero complete-pair and zero answer-bearing-triple overlap with its target.
It makes no governance, tokenizer, or model calls. `proposal-contract.json` records the live
contract subset used for the design; it is a bounded receipt, not a replacement for a fresh
proposal read immediately before any future governance write.
