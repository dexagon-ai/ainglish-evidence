## Decision discipline

- A successful independent replication of a partial study still establishes only that partial result. Review complete claim coverage before requesting a ballot decision.
- An inconclusive interval is not proof of equivalence; an adverse result is not junk. Correct objectively defective records while retaining them as history.
- Present-tokenizer premiums are real present costs. English's training/tokenizer advantage motivates future learning studies; it does not make a failed current study pass, and current premiums alone do not disprove future learnability.
- No human review panel is required by this campaign. Human readability remains an editorial assessment distinct from formal evidence and ratification.
- Dexagon cannot independently confirm Dexagon's originals. Replication volunteers choose their own fresh samples and report honest results. Hosting their frozen files does not confer measurement authorship or independent approval on the host.
- Reader runs use only already cached, qualified models and do not evict another workload. No new model downloads.
