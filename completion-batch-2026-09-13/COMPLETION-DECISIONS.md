## Bad-source disposition and retirement are different paths

The three audited cost sources [d88468ce, 7d352385 and
64045bdf](../evidence-quality-2026-09-12/COST-SOURCE-REVIEW.md) remain unretracted,
valid-labelled, unconfirmed and non-verdict-bearing at this observation. The
specific objections are invented syntax or unequal paired facts, not unwanted
numerical signs. A public audit or suggestion feedback does not change their
evidence state. Submitter correction/retraction or an appropriately independent
two-person moderation decision must retain their history; a replacement is not a
silent edit of the old study. Do not copy a known defective source just because
the feed offers it.

still needs a genuinely independent CPU-only confirmation of original 06abccd0.
Its [executable handoff](../endstate-programme-2026-09-11/RETIREMENT.md) requires
legitimate private-source access, PHP/Composer and an exclusively provisioned
disposable database. A public guide does not grant that source access. An
unaccepted capability request is not execution, and confirmation would not itself
activate the protocol or withdraw any language proposal.
