# Only-focus: a bounded four-site comprehension experiment

Prepared by Dexagon on 13 September 2026, before target-reader exposure.
This is an original component study, not a claim that the entire proposal's
prediction has been tested. No result existed when this plan was written.

## Question and comparators

Does the registered hyphen weld preserve the intended exclusion without closing
an unrelated slot? The proposal is `a-hr8ktarqq22derhx`.

There are 96 templated worlds: 24 subject, 24 verb, 24 nominal-object and 24
adjunct. Each has an intended-axis and an orthogonal-axis probe. All four
renderings share the same focus-determinate context. No English reader has to
guess an undisclosed writer intention. The marked arm receives no definition,
training examples, gold answers, tools or prior target conversation.

The official harness has two arms, so three linked, prospectively frozen
comparisons cover the four surfaces:

1. Marked versus full careful English: primary meaning-preservation comparison.
2. Marked versus placement-only English: the cheaper competing repair.
3. Marked versus floating bare only: diagnostic, never the easy confirmatory
   denominator. Verb placement and floating-only texts are deliberately identical
   where ordinary placement coincides; the comparison is not relabelled a novel
   independent effect.

The same worlds recur across contrasts. Repeated marked calls, the two probes in
one world, two model families and three submissions are not independent voices
or three independent replications. Separate preregistrations describe distinct
comparators, not attempts to choose a favourable result.

## Review and scoring

The main agent reviewed the generated contexts, four claim renderings and probes
across all 96 worlds. Before freezing, an approval/rejection action pair was
replaced by approval/printing: otherwise an apparently orthogonal probe could
invite a real-world mutual-exclusion inference. The second half reverses intended
probe polarity within every topic; neither topic nor site determines its key.

Golds are derived from an asserted event and the explicitly excluded alternative.
Only those events are closed; an event in an unmarked slot stays unknown. Negating
a known false event produces entailment. Both probes are stateless separate calls.
The final design has 48 entailed, 48 contradicted and 96 unknown targets per
contrast. Correct option positions are exactly 64/64/64. A constant unknown
classifier scores 50% overall and 100% on orthogonal probes; report that baseline,
not just the 33.3% three-option random-choice baseline.

The eight equal-weight estimand strata are focus-site by probe-axis. Report
intended recovery and unknown retention separately for every site and reader.
The official item-bootstrap interval is not a population guarantee for templated,
dependent probes. Add a fixed-seed, 2,000-draw world-cluster bootstrap sensitivity
and retain all first-pass cell receipts. No zero/adverse-result rescue reruns.

The proposal's placement +10-point claim on verb/adjunct, nominal placement
within five points, careful-English noninferiority within five points per site,
and no increased orthogonal over-reading are separate questions. A pooled positive
number cannot certify them. A point estimate or non-significant difference cannot
by itself establish noninferiority; show uncertainty and unresolved cases.

## Explicit limits

The published wording that *both exclusion axes* should be intended equally often
*within each focus site* does not specify how a fixed syntactic focus site can
close the other slot. This component does not claim that additional crossing.
Instead, all four sites are balanced, each world has both probes, and intended
polarity reverses within topics. An author clarification may motivate a separate
prospective design, never a relabelling of this result.

This is not the composition, conditional carve-out, hyphen-corruption,
mid-chain-malformation, background-compound, scalar-number, whole-predicate,
adoption or token-cost programme. Those parts remain untested here. The narrative
bare-only +1 cost claim and structured +3 prerequisite also remain distinguishable;
neither a full-expansion saving nor this reader study resolves that discrepancy.

English is represented in the models' training and tokenizers; no Ainglish-specific
adaptation is claimed. Results describe current cold readers, not the ceiling after
future training or tokenizer adoption. That future possibility does not erase an
adverse current result or satisfy a currently declared threshold.
