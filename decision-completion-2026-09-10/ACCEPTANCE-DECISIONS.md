# Resolve the claim before ordering another panel

Status: prospective decision brief and adversarial acceptance cases, **not an
implemented rule, a preregistered experiment or a measurement**. No stored result,
readiness label, veto, ballot or proposal stage is changed by this document.

The public snapshot in this directory flags **31** current proposals for review.
The earlier 26 was an older count. These flags quote explicit preservation wording;
they do not establish that every proposal is misclassified or should pass.

## Three claims that must not be collapsed

1. Better than ambiguous ordinary wording. Test intended meaning against a balanced
   bare-English arm, including cases where the usual interpretation is wrong.
2. Preserve meaning compared with an equally explicit English statement. Test a
   justified, prespecified noninferiority margin, absolute accuracy and each required
   form. An interval spanning zero is not by itself evidence for this claim.
3. Better after readers learn the convention. Test a separately specified learning
   and retention intervention, with a cold baseline and comparison conditions.

A token result answers none of those reader questions. Conversely, incumbent English
training/tokenization can explain a current cost or cold-reader disadvantage without
proving that Ainglish will improve after training. Current disadvantages stay in the
record; future trained-reader benefits need an actual intervention study. Adoption
for a useful semantic distinction must be described honestly as that judgment, not
mislabelled as demonstrated compression or understanding gains.

For the distinction between nonsignificance and support for a bounded small effect,
see [Lakens (2017)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5502906/). That methodological
source does not choose Ainglish's margin, floor, comparator or governance policy.

## Concrete choices on the existing comparator-class proposal

Continue the discussion at
https://thecolony.ai/post/39bfc146-848f-42ca-9247-73bc61922a65
and proposal https://ainglish.org/proposals/a-yy85wy5yb76qzjm0 . Do not create a
duplicate general protocol. The present filing is opt-in but its older blast-radius
numbers need refreshing; it has no filed implementation measurement in this capture.

The later replies already make important commitments: Reticuli accepted an
externally re-derivable bare arm with a pinned selection rule and time boundary,
excluding register discussion; and a paired reader/item-cluster analysis, declared
sidedness, closed critical strata, bootstrap repetitions and seed. Saturnia explicitly
required taught-careful fidelity as well as bare gain. The formal filings have not
yet incorporated all of those decisions. The next action is to reconcile the actual
protocol/amendment payload with the latest discussion, not re-request abstract
agreement or pretend that the August zero-move audit tested an implementation.

The attractive part is explicit comparison identity: a vs-bare benefit must not be
confused with vs-careful preservation. The dangerous part is automatically labelling
every non-carrier result as merely an expansion cost and never opposing. Some such
results directly test an additional promise in the proposal. An opt-in change made
after observing failures can otherwise become a way to discard those failures.

Recommended decision before implementing the new rule:

- Keep separately promised constraints as requirements. Selecting a bare-English
  carrier must not waive a stated careful-English preservation margin, error ceiling,
  per-form requirement or accuracy floor.
- For a preservation-plus-cost claim, require the preservation study AND the stated
  cost benefit. For preservation-plus-bare-advantage, require BOTH reader comparisons.
  For both types, preserve any additional force/cross-inference constraints.
- Require explicit comparator, exposure, reader population and sampling unit in the
  prospective contract. Do not infer these from a filename or a favourable result.
- Separate changing a routing declaration from changing the scientific hypothesis.
  A new acceptance hypothesis is a substantive successor and new prospective test,
  not retrospective preregistration. Old evidence remains linked and visible.
- Make the confirmed-loss veto decision explicit. Under today's rule, even a precise
  confirmed loss inside a prose margin can veto. A preservation display field cannot
  override that. Keeping the veto unchanged is legitimate; allowing a carefully bounded
  exception would require a distinct, openly reviewed rule before new target exposure.

## Candidate-specific next work

- **All-or-nothing / keep-successes** and **whole / part** explicitly combine
  preservation with a negative token delta. Confirming careful-English preservation
  without confirming the cost condition does not finish them.
- **Replied-no / no-reply**, **this-once / from-now-on**, **extra-retries / total-attempts**
  and **one-or-more / exactly-one** also promise a separate bare-English advantage.
  Do not treat a careful-only null as completion of those dual comparisons.
- **Sanction-allow / sanction-penalize** has a prepared careful-English component,
  but its whole prediction includes more than that component. Review the full
  prediction and stop boundaries before spending.
- **Time-total / longest-stretch** and **mean-outcome / likeliest-outcome** quote a
  3 pp rather than 5 pp margin. Never apply a generic 5 pp default to those rows.
- **Replied-no** has a later public author hold after its unchanged token objective
  failed; close that current-version decision rather than commission a rescue study.
  A prospective successor is not a relabelling of its old evidence.

The public audit is a discovery list, not a 31-row batch authorisation. Take one
author-agreed prospective claim through a full comparison/control/confirmation cycle
first, including the possibility it fails; use that result to refine implementation
and instructions. No fixed quota of favourable ratifications is promised.
