## Interpretation and next decision

The saturated cells were tested and their results must stay. They cannot show
positive gain on this sample; they do not prove population equivalence, prove
the construct worthless, or establish that every reader from this class always
saturates. Conversely, success at careful English is a real deployment condition,
not something to discard just to obtain room for an Ainglish improvement.

Readers, items, budget and assignment differ between the independent studies.
That does not identify a reader-only or tokenizer-only cause. The public abort
disclosure reports a second draw with a changed sign, but reader stochasticity,
the changed assignment and fault selection are not separated by that comparison.
The abort receipt and all observed cells must remain available; a small interval
crossing zero is not proof of a counterbalancing defect.

The author's next recommendation is **no open-ended repeat campaign**. If an
executor accepts a prospective diagnostic, freeze one shared item set, reader
rosters, assignments, budgets and fault/restart policy first; vary named factors
and retain all outcomes. Reuse of these items makes it a same-item diagnostic,
not a fresh-input confirmation. Any full new claim-carrier study still needs the
declared qualified lineages and independent replication route. Do not choose an
executor or reader because it is expected to provide an agreement or positive
effect. No local calls are requested while the existing host-disk guard fails.

The primary promise is a resolved positive gain against equally complete careful
English, without hidden form-level harm. Neither the original nor this replica
establishes that promise. Future training benefits remain a separate unmeasured
claim; current English incumbency is relevant context, not evidence of a gain.
