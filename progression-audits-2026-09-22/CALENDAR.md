## Allocation

There are 21 feasible divergent ordered anchor/target weekday relations per
week-start convention. Crossing two forms and Monday/Sunday starts gives 84
relation cells. The accepted minimum is two distinct worlds per cell: 168 total,
84 per form, 42 per form/week-start. Impossible divergent same-weekday cells
receive zero weight, not invented examples. `calendar_design.py` checks this.

For useful precision, propose 12 fresh distinct worlds per relation cell: 1,008
worlds, 504 per form, 252 per form/week-start. Every world has marked, bare and
complete-careful arms with the same context and question. Do not count arms or
reader repetitions as extra independent worlds. Do not reuse a single world's
date under several cosmetic identifiers to reach the sample size.

Within each 12-world relation cell, propose three examples in each epoch class:
ordinary, month-end, year-end, leap boundary. The future builder must prove actual
date feasibility and freeze a replacement allocation BEFORE bank authoring if
any combination is impossible. These are requested quotas, not a certified full
Cartesian design. Across the 12 replicates, rotate four answer positions three
times and six operational domains twice. Freeze the epoch/domain/position joint
table before authoring; do not let a single domain predict a key. Distance is
determined by the weekday relation (next-up 1–6, next-week 8–13 here), not a freely
crossed independent variable. Primary weights are equal per feasible relation and
then equal per world; separately report observed weekday and distance weights.

Add distinct controls OUTSIDE this denominator: 28 same-weekday strict-after
worlds, 28 other convergent worlds and 28 already-resolved timestamp distractors,
balanced by form and week-start. These small control blocks are failure detectors,
not proof of a 5% rate bound. The five nonclaim axes each need a separate minimum
120 worlds per form (1,200 total) to estimate their error ceiling; positive and
negative answer positions must be balanced. Nonclaim worlds must not leak their
questions/keys into the scientific messages.

agree on 22,400 cases. Both were implemented by Dexagon: algorithmic diversity
does not claim independent authorship. Require a second principal to audit the

Exclude these exposed 1999–2001 control dates and all previous target inputs.

Use fresh, stateless requests per arm and world. Freeze arm ordering and request

ask for date AND signed offset. Freeze four joint answer choices, mapping and
scorer, retain raw completions/request journals and count every transport/parse
failure according to the official protocol. Careful English explicitly states
strict-after or next-calendar-week, anchor and week-start. Bare wording omits

Propose three fixed available reader endpoints, each passing a target-independent
calendar/arithmetic qualification frozen beforehand. Freeze provider, served model
identity/version, decoding, precision, system prompt, retention and resource access.
No endpoint is selected for poor English or favourable Ainglish performance. Report

endpoints are three independent model populations or human readers. Source author
Dexagon does not take the required source-independent execution role. A prospective
executor must accept the actual call budget and retention contract after review.

## Statistical gates and the current policy boundary

Per form and reader, exact joint date/offset accuracy is primary. Compare marked
versus careful and marked versus bare separately. Preserve every form/week-start,
epoch/domain/distance result; do not pool a failing form away. Bare improvement
must reach the promised 20 pp with a prospectively reviewed paired interval;
careful preservation is separately tested against the 5 pp margin. The absolute
protocol floor must be read from the live official protocol, explicitly frozen,
and tested, not replaced by an invented numerical floor in this packet.

The supplied exact-binomial sensitivity calculation treats an English-correct /
marked-wrong world as a loss. A one-sided upper bound on that probability below
5% is a conservative sufficient preservation test, ignoring offsetting gains.
It is NOT the official comprehension carrier estimator and does not change it.
Likewise each false-inference axis gets its own one-sided 95% upper bound. These
are marginal bounds, not a claim of simultaneous 95% coverage over all displays.
The published power table quantifies why 84 worlds/form and tiny control blocks
can be indecisive even on good readers. Independent review must assess dependence,
the intended inferential population, gain intervals and operating characteristics
for ALL required gates before approving any spend.

Crucially, the current machine contract still requires **positive** comprehension
support against the careful comparator. Non-inferiority alone, a ceiling, bare gain
or token saving cannot complete that carrier. Comparator-class v5 is not active.
Ask the author to acknowledge this execution/ratification boundary explicitly:
the study may establish practical preservation yet remain unable to ratify under
today's rule. Do not spend a large panel under an assumption that the parked policy
already applies. Retain the original pooled +14.565 pp and all adverse/null replicas
unchanged; this would be a genuinely new original, never a renamed replication.
