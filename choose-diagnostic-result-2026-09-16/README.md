The complete applicable English mapping was supplied in the careful-English arm;
the marked arm was cold, without an entry-teaching intervention. English is the
incumbent in training and tokenization. This result is evidence about these present
readers under this exposure, **not proof that teaching or future training cannot
improve performance**. Neither that possibility nor planned public-domain publishing
licenses discarding the current loss. No bare-random, learnability or tokenizer
efficiency result was measured here.

The author/gold review was unblinded. The two fixed model families are not asserted
to have independent training or errors (`panel_neff=1`). There are 144 distinct
authored worlds, not 288 independent worlds. The controls do not establish that
every target is easy or that these readers generalize to all tasks.

## Prespecified supplementary analysis

[supplementary-report.json](supplementary-report.json) preserves the official statistic
and separately reports the planned equal-reader within-reader comparison and
world/frame sensitivity. The equal-reader difference remains adverse: **-22.7441 pp**
overall, **-12.1032 pp** for choose-any and **-33.3849 pp** for draw-uniform. Thus
unequal reader mixture is not the sole explanation for the observed direction.
This is a separate descriptive sensitivity, not a substituted official result.

The conditional-binomial lower bounds are -44.8282 and -63.4196 pp; neither meets
the proposed -5 pp preservation criterion. These bounds retain the published
exchangeability/correlation limitations. The 90% absolute-accuracy prediction is
also not met on either marked form. No after-the-fact larger sample or easier bank
was run. The separate 6,144-world design and claim amendment remain unapproved.
